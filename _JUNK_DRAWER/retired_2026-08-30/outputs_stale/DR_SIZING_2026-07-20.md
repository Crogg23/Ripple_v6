## What a full backup actually costs (estimates)

- **Parquet export size:** 0.7–1.3× compressed → **~11–21 GB**.
- **Storage:** ~$0.023/GB-month (S3/GCS standard) → **~$0.25–$0.47/month**. Even with versioning ×3, under $1.50/month.
- **One-time export compute:** ~15.8 GB at 50–100 GB/hr COPY throughput → 0.2–0.3 X-Small credits; doubled for per-table overhead across ~1,900 small tables, still **≤ 1 credit (~$2–3) total**.
