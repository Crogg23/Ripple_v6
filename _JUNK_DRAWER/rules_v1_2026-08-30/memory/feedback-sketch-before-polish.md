When Chris asks to visualize/communicate findings, he wants **rough sketches of
chart OPTIONS plus plain-language commentary on each** — not a finished artifact.
Two sessions in a row I skipped straight to a fully-designed HTML page (Plotly
report, then a typeset editorial artifact with a custom palette). Both were
rejected as wasted tokens.

**Why:** Chris is choosing the *direction* at that stage. Polish spent before he
picks a direction is thrown away, and a finished-looking page pressures him to
accept it instead of steering. He also explicitly wants to start high-level and
"slice our way down" — full detail on one slice before he's approved the shape is
backwards.

**How to apply:**
- Default deliverable when asked to visualize = a cheap list: for each finding,
  what chart shape would carry it, why that shape, what it would show — plus
  a rough sketch (ASCII, tiny inline SVG, or one bare matplotlib/Plotly draft).
  No design system, no palette work, no typography, no dark mode.
- Say the finding in Ripple tone (bar-talk plain, harm named) next to each option.
- Wait for him to pick before building anything polished.
- "Cosmetics we can refine later" means *do not touch cosmetics at all*.
- Related: [[feedback-open-brief-means-range]] (bring 2-3 options, let him choose)
  and [[feedback-grilling-calibration]].
