# Ripple — one-page pitch

**One warehouse of US public records, joined so one company, doctor or place can be followed across every agency that tracks it.**

Built solo by Chris Rogers, a healthcare data analyst, with AI agents doing the legwork and a skeptic checking every claim.

## The problem
Public data is scattered across hundreds of agencies. Each one uses its own IDs.
Medicare knows a nursing home by one number. OSHA, the IRS and the courts each know it by another.
The story is almost never inside one file. It's in the join.

## What Ripple is

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| The warehouse | Snowflake, about 1.4 billion landed rows across 2,200+ source tables, as of Sep 5 2026 |
| The shelf | 592 analysis-ready tables: health, money, environment, courts, politics, labor |
| The catalog | Every table and column explained in plain English |
| The ledger | A checklist of every table, join and place a story could hide, each with a status |
| The traps log | Columns that look real and aren't: empty IDs, constant flags, repeated totals |

## How it finds things
1. **Look at every table.** Each one got an automated check, then a proper look by an agent.
2. **Join on real IDs first.** NPI, EIN, CCN, UEI, facility and docket numbers.
3. **Join on names only with a second field agreeing.** City, ZIP or street address.
4. **Measure the match.** A 500-company name match graded 87.5% clean on a hand-checked sample. A shuffled-ZIP test put false donor matches near 0.2%.
5. **Send every claim to a skeptic.** A fresh agent tries to break it. Most first numbers shrink; very few die.

## What it has found, after checking

| Lead | The number |
|---|---|
| Las Vegas Sands in Texas | $20.58M in lobby "media" through one lobbyist, 70-71% of all Texas lobby media, 2021-26 |
| One Missouri nursing-home chain | 30 homes, 35% fewer nurse hours than peers, 3.8x the harm citations |
| Two medical-supply firms | $593.6M of Medicare's $649.2M for one wound dressing in 2024, 91.4% |
| One company, two states | Iowa plant failed smokestack tests on 49 days, no Iowa order; its Indiana plant drew 7 |
| One weather office | Supplied 63% of all US heat deaths in the federal storm record, 2018-23 |

Every name is a data match, not yet verified against primary records.

## What it proves
- **Entity resolution** at scale: deterministic and probabilistic matching, blocking, match-quality measurement
- **Data quality** as a habit: hundreds of traps found and logged before they could mislead a finding
- **Pipelines** that hold up: Snowflake, dbt, Python, single loads of tens of millions of rows
- **Plain-English communication:** findings written for a reader, not for a data team
