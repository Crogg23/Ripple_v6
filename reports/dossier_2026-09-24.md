## What worked and what didn't

**Worked**
- ✅ **The skeptic step.** It caught a bad first number on almost every lead, before you saw it.
- ✅ **Company names as the joining glue.** IDs only link money, labor and environment tables. Health, courts and politics connect by name alone.
- ✅ **Comparing like with like.** Ranking a company against its own industry made the SpaceX and Ford results fair.

**Didn't**
- ❌ **Crosswalk "hops."** 55 rows checked, nothing live.
- ❌ **Harm per contract dollar.** It just ranks the biggest companies, like Amazon and Delta.
- ❌ **Presence counts.** 278 companies appear in five domains, mostly because big companies appear everywhere.

## Data traps found today

Dozens of columns that look real and aren't. Examples:
- A payment column that's empty on 1.9 million rows
- A "serious violator" flag that says No on all 3.1 million rows
- Injury hours with typos up to 52 million for a 165-person firm
- Two tables that are copies of each other

All are saved to memory, and the checklist auto-skips most of them.
One old trap turned out to be wrong: the federal grants data is no longer capped.
