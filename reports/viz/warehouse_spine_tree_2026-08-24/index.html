Join keys, by entities linked

How to read it: the root is the warehouse. Each first-ring node is one join key — the identifier that actually lets Ripple merge records from different agencies into one entity. Leaves are the tables that carry that key, sized by distinct entity count. NPI (health providers) and FRS_ID (EPA facilities) are the biggest, and mostly self-contained inside Health and Environment. EIN is the one that actually crosses domains — IRS, OSHA, FEC, and courts all key off it, which is what makes it the closest thing this warehouse has to a real cross-domain spine today.
