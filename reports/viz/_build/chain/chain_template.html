How to read it
Click a dataset — its own joins open in a new column to the right.
Keep clicking — the chain keeps growing.
It stops when a dataset has no joins left, or when everything it touches you could already reach. The card labels tell you which.
Each card shows
Left — the dataset; under it, when you reach it, its agency and row count.
Right — match % (share of this step’s IDs found there), then its onward joins and how many of those are new.
+ — opens the table’s name, size, notes and every recorded column, without moving anything.
