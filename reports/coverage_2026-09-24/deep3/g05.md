## Traps found

**Home health enrollments**
- `LOCATION_OTHER_TYPE_TEXT` null on all rows. `PRACTICE_LOCATION_TYPE` is only "HHA BRANCH" or blank.
- `INCORPORATION_DATE`: 2,297 blank, 33 before 1900. Use the date in `ENROLLMENT_ID`.
- The file holds today's enrollees only. Year counts are survivors.
- `HEALTH__FED_CMS_POS_OTHER` holds **0 of 11,508** HHA CCNs. It can't supply HHA counties.
- One enrolled agency, Align Home Health (Enginuit Design Ltd, Beavercreek OH), went on the OIG list 2026-08-20. Check the file's pull date before calling that a miss by CMS.

**Hospital enrollments**
- The glance said NPI 1083668669 sits on 68 rows. **Not reproduced**: max is 4 rows per NPI (Salem Health).
- 290 incorporation dates before 1900 over 166 dates. Only 4 are the 1800-01-01 filler; the rest look like real old charters. 3,149 blank.
- Enrollment-ID date is not an opening date (503 CAH IDs since 2019).
- 3 REH rows with no conversion date and no old CCN.
- `FINDINGS.HOSPITAL_CLOSURE_RISK` marks 97% of rural hospitals in these states negative-margin (1,120 of 1,149). The flag separates nothing. Some `FY_END` values are 2013-2018.

**ASM participants**
- `ASM_CY28`-`CY31` columns hold the text "Null" on every row.
- `STATE` disagrees with the clinician's 2024 Part B state on 12% of matches (784 of 6,490). Whole practices are mislabeled: Sutter Valley Medical Foundation shows VA (25 clinicians), Part B says CA. Sentara NY vs VA, Steward FL vs RI/MA/PA.
- 125 NPIs appear twice, once per organization. Dedupe on NPI.

**OTP providers**
- 858 of 1,558 rows (55%) say effective 2020-01-01, the day Medicare's OTP benefit began. A launch default, not an opening date.
- One NPI can cover 49 sites. Count sites by address.
- 89 NPIs billed Medicare as OTPs in 2024 but aren't on the list. The list is a floor.

**CDC portal**
- `PAGE_VIEWS` null on all 1,471 rows.
- NNDSS "weekly" tables freeze each January by design; stale doesn't mean stopped.
- NIOSH's 177 entries are files and links, zero datasets.
