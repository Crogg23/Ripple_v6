## The answer first

**No leads. All five are lookup tables, and all five are clean enough to use.**
The real finding is about the glance, not the tables:

> **Bad news: the glance's "top values" on near-unique columns are made up.**
> The glance said ZIP area 26237 has 57 rows. It has **1**. Every ZIP area has 1.
> It said one GRID ID sits on 291 research orgs. The real maximum is **2**.
> Cause: the glance used `APPROX_TOP_K`, a quick estimate. On a column where almost every value is different, it inflates the counts.

## The glance trap, with its proof

**What was checked:** exact counts of the same columns the glance summarized.

| Column | Glance said (top value) | Exact count | Non-blank rows ÷ glance count |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZCTA5CE20 | 26237: **57** | **1** (all 33,791 are unique) | 33,791 ÷ 57 = 593 |
| ROR GRID_IDS_ALL | grid.507419.e: **291** | max **2** | 103,681 ÷ 291 = 356 |
| ROR ISNI_IDS_ALL | 0000 0004 0623 6436: **168** | max **2** | 59,825 ÷ 168 = 356 |
| ROR WIKIDATA_IDS_ALL | Q51781143: **166** | max **4** | 59,072 ÷ 166 = 356 |

**What a hit means:** the three ROR counts are all "rows ÷ 356". Real counts wouldn't line up like that. That's what an inflated estimate looks like.
**Where it came from:** `reports/coverage_2026-09-24/battery.py`, query Q4: `APPROX_TOP_K(col, 5)`.
**Where it holds up:** columns with few values. The glance said DSMZ wrote 18.8K ITIS comments; the exact count is 18,789 (S08). That one is right.
**The rule:** top-value counts from Q4 on an ID-like column are not counts. Recount exactly before quoting one.

## 2. XC_ROR_RESEARCH_ORGANIZATIONS: skip

The Research Organization Registry: universities, companies, hospitals, labs. Its job is linking the same org across ID systems (GRID, ISNI, Wikidata, Crossref funder IDs).

**Checked (S04, S05, S09, S10, S12)**
- 135,710 rows = 135,710 ROR IDs. One load run. Created 2018-11-14 to 2026-08-03.
- Repeated names are real, not duplicates: "Ministry of Health" is 50 orgs in 50 countries. "Argosy University" is 19 separate US records with the same name, most now retired. **A name alone is not an ID here.**
- Outside IDs are almost 1-to-1. Only 2 GRID values, 144 ISNI values and 79 Wikidata values are shared, by 2 to 4 orgs each. Example: Wikidata Q12067802 sits on 4 "S P Jain School of Global Management" campuses in 4 countries.

**Status test: does "active" mean the place is open?** No.
Five for-profit college chains that shut between 2015 and 2023 (closure years from public reporting, not from the warehouse):

| Chain | Records | Still "active" |
|---|---|---|
| Argosy (closed 2019) | 20 | 0 (3 inactive, 17 withdrawn) |
| Art Institutes (closed 2023) | 25 | **4** |
| ITT Tech (closed 2016) | 1 | **1** |
| Everest / Corinthian (closed 2015) | 1 | **1** |
| Virginia College / Brightwood (closed 2018) | 3 | **1** |

- **7 of 50** still say active.
- "Withdrawn" mostly means a duplicate record retired in favor of another: 445 of 469 US withdrawn rows point to a successor.
- "Inactive" is mixed: 145 of 337 US inactive rows point to a successor (merged or renamed). The other 192 don't say.
- **What a miss means:** ROR is useful for linking IDs. It is not a record of closures. Don't use STATUS to say an org shut down.

**Traps**
- **Main ID vs full list.** ISNI_ID is filled on 10,300 rows; ISNI_IDS_ALL on 59,825. **49,525 orgs (83%) have an ISNI only in the list.** Wikidata: 47,003 (80%) only in the list. Join on the `_IDS_ALL` columns, not the main ones. GRID has no gap (103,681 both).
- **CONTINENT_CODE is blank on exactly the 41,194 North America rows.** The code "NA" was read as empty.
- **RECORD_LAST_MODIFIED_DATE starts at 2024-12-11 in every status group.** Every record was touched on or after that day, likely a bulk format change (the format version reads 2.1 everywhere). The date can't tell you when something real changed. Not tested further.

## 4. FED_ITIS_COMMENTS: skip

Notes by the scientists who keep the federal species list.

**Checked (S06-S08)**
- 70,524 rows = 70,524 comment IDs. 345 different writers. One load.
- 4 comments are linked to no species.
- 5 exact duplicates: same writer, same text, same timestamp, different comment ID.

**Time check (S08):** comments per year, 1996 to 2026.
- Every other year holds between 3 (1997) and 4,862 (2018) comments.
- **2015 holds 27,011, which is 38% of the whole table.** 17,985 of them (67%) are by DSMZ, the German bacteria collection. DSMZ wrote 804 in 2014 and none in any other year.
- **What that means:** 2015 is a bulk import of bacteria names, not a year of extra work. Any trend line on this table is an import log.

## 5. FED_ITIS_PUBLICATIONS: skip

Books and articles ITIS cites as evidence for species names.

**Checked (S06, S07, S11)**
- 30,772 rows = 30,772 publication IDs. One load.
- 166 content duplicates: same author, title, journal, date and pages under two IDs.
- 50 publications are cited by no species. 0 cited IDs are missing from the table.
- No future dates. Earliest is 1753 (4 rows), which is Linnaeus's plant-naming starting year, so it's real, not a placeholder.

**Traps**
- **The word "None" instead of blank:** publisher 26,832 rows (87%), place 26,863 (87%), ISBN 28,792 (94%), comment 15,720, ISSN 15,146, title 2,511. `IS NULL` finds none of them.
- **12,659 printed dates (41%) are January 1:** only the year is known. Don't count by month.
- 772 rows have an actual date that differs from the printed one.
