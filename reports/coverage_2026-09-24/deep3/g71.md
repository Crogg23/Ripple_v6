## 1. OPEN_DATA__INTL_FR_DATA_GOUV: **probed**

**What it is:** 130,431 rows = 130,431 dataset IDs = 130,431 slugs [q02]. One row per dataset. The export is dated 11 Aug 2026.
**The key move:** `ARCHIVED` is text. It holds `False` (73,875 live) or a timestamp (**56,556 archived, 43%**). The "full list" has the dead ones in it.

### Time: archiving by month [q04]
- Normal months: 100-1,000 archived.
- **21 March 2024: 12,190 archived in one day**, across 124 publishers [q06].
  - 10,230 of them were departmental territory offices (DDT/DDTM), 680 were regional environment offices (DREAL).
  - The listings were created 2013-2021, median year 2016. That's the old state geodata import.
  - Sample title: "PPRI de Vauvert (30)", a flood-risk plan. **2,439** of the 12,190 are flood-risk plans (PPR/PPRI).
- Summer 2026, three harvested waves:
  - Région Hauts-de-France: 2,467 on 11-14 June.
  - Office Français de la Biodiversité: 1,383 in July.
  - Région Grand Est: 3,100 on **11 Aug 2026, the export day**.

### The dull test: is "archived" the same as "gone"? [q05, q07, q24, q27]
Test: does a live dataset exist with the same title (normalized)?

| Wave | Archived | Live twin, same office | Live twin, any publisher |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-03-21, whole day | 12,190 | 6,312 (**51.8%**) | 6,752 (55.4%) |
| — flood-risk plans only | 2,439 | — | 1,436 (59%) |
| OFB, Jul 2026 | 1,383 | 1,350 (97.6%) | — |
| Région Grand Est, Aug 2026 | 3,100 | 461 (14.9%) | 461 |
| Région Hauts-de-France, Jun 2026 | 2,467 | 9 (0.4%) | 15 |

- Six biggest DDT offices in the 2024 wave: twin rates run 33-95%. Their twins' median creation years are 2013-2019, the same era or older, so much of the wave was duplicates from the legacy geo import.
- Exact-title matching is strict. A retitled replacement counts as "no twin", so treat the no-twin share as an upper bound on what's truly gone.
- Headline datasets that left with no exact twin, checked by keyword [q23, q24]:
  - Alim'confiance restaurant hygiene results: **dull.** The archived copy was data.gouv.fr's own. The Agriculture Ministry's copy is still live (46,197 downloads).
  - Social housing register (RPLS), statistics office SDES: **dull.** A new SDES version was created 2025-08-13, 8 days before the old one was archived on 2025-08-21.
  - Météo-France station observations: **dull.** "Archive Synop OMM" is live (Météo-France, created 2025-07-10).
  - Culture Ministry list of Musées de France (307,508 downloads, archived 2026-04-01), Interior Ministry list of public-utility foundations (archived 2026-05-20), SDES Sitadel building permits and Pégase energy stats (archived 2025-08-20/21): **no live same-publisher copy found by keyword.** The likely reason is that they moved to the ministries' own portals. Not tested; the warehouse has no copy of those portals.

- **The dull explanation (likely, not tested):** both regions pull their listings from their own portals (Grand Est: datagrandest.fr / data4citizen; Hauts-de-France: opendata.hautsdefrance.fr) [q08]. When a source portal moves, data.gouv.fr archives whatever its harvester can no longer find. The data probably still sits on the regional site.
- The Grand Est wave is dated the same day as the export, so the snapshot may have caught a harvest halfway through.
- A hostile editor kills this in one phone call. **Not a lead.**

### Concentration: usage [q09]
- Live datasets: 73,875 = 26,879 native + 46,996 harvested. Native ones carry **74.4%** of downloads.
- The top 10 live datasets hold **36.6%** of 1.38B downloads; the top 100 hold **73.3%**.
- #1 is "Le calendrier scolaire" (Education Ministry): **235.4M downloads, 1.69M views**. #2 is Troyes bus data: 51.2M downloads, 6,266 views. Downloads are apps polling, not people reading.

### Time: native datasets created per year [q19]
- 2015-2017 cohorts: 77-87% archived now (the geo duplicates). 2024: 4.7%. 2025: 0.9%.
- Publishers per year keep rising: 124 in 2013, 1,222 in 2025.

### Traps
- **`CREATED_AT` on harvested rows is the source's date, not the portal's.** Values include 0004-11-21, 0016-01-03, 0199-02-10, 1827, 1905, and the future dates 2027-10-31 and 2028-07-01. 50 rows fall before 1990 and 2 after the export date [q02]. Of the rows before 1970 or in the future, all but one are harvested [q09].
- **`ARCHIVED` is text, either `False` or a timestamp.** Filter `ARCHIVED='False'` for the live catalog.
- **`NB_RESOURCE_DOWNLOADS` is machine traffic.** A transit feed with 6K views shows 51M downloads.
- **Archived ≠ deleted.** Re-harvests archive the old copy and create a new one. Test for a twin before saying anything vanished.
- The no-org wave of Jan 2025 (1,915 archived) came from **one user account** harvesting catalogue.doterr.fr [q08]. It was not a government act.
- 3,911 organization names vs 3,920 organization IDs: a few names are shared by more than one ID.

## 2. OPEN_DATA__INTL_AR_DATOSGOB: **probed**

**What it is:** 3,556 rows, **501 dataset IDs, 3,271 distinct file URLs** [q15]. One row per file, not per dataset.
The glance's "3.6K datasets" is really 3.6K file rows.

- `LAST_MODIFIED` is text but parses on every filled row: 2025-03-24 to 2026-07-02.

### Traps
- **224 rows are a province lookup riding inside the catalog.** They have no org, no description and no date. All 224 share 1 dataset ID and 1 URL, and they carry `PROVINCIA_ID` (24 values). They're the same 224 rows where `C_ORGANIZATION`, `DESCRIPTION` and `LAST_MODIFIED` are blank [q15, q22].
- 3,556 rows, but only 3,328 distinct dataset+URL+format combinations. The lookup block accounts for up to 223 of the 228 extra rows.
- `SERIE_ID/VALUE/DATE` are empty. `MUNICIPIO_ID` and `DEPARTAMENTO_ID` are filled on 100 rows.
- `C_ORGANIZATION` has leading spaces (" Ministerio de Justicia"). Trim before grouping.

### Read the words: sources worth pulling [q26]

## What a hit / miss means here
- **Hit:** a publisher losing most of its listings while its peers don't. That happened twice (Grand Est, Hauts-de-France).
- **Miss:** the twin test and the harvest-host check both point to portal plumbing, not a government pulling data. The test that would settle it lives on the regional portals, which aren't in the warehouse.
- **Dead tables:** the capped slices (GR, DE, CL) can't hit anything about time or share. You don't know what the other 95%+ of the catalog looks like.
