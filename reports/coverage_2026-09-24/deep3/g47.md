## 1. OWID fertility: **probed**

**What it is**
- Children per woman, by country or region, by year. 19,402 rows, 261 names.
- Most names run 1950-2023 (74 rows each). Sweden goes back to 1891 (133 rows).
- 236 names carry a 3-letter country code. The rest are regions, income groups and UN groupings.

**Checks run**
- **Load check (S03):** the text copy in REFERENCE and the number copy in TIMELINE hold the same 19,402 rows. No duplicate country-years. Every value casts. Every value agrees.
- **Time, same year across decades (S02, ranked locally):**

| Year | Countries under 2.1 (replacement) | Under 1.5 | Under 1.0 | Median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1950 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 5.81 |
| 2000 | 90 | 36 | 1 | 2.58 |
| 2015 | 111 | 37 | 1 | 2.21 |
| 2020 | 125 | 51 | 8 | 2.03 |
| 2023 | 129 | 65 | 9 | 1.96 |

(Out of 236 countries each year.)

- **Series lows:** 148 of 236 countries post their lowest value in 2023, the last year in the table.
- **Ten-year drop, 2013 to 2023:** the median country fell 0.34 children (13.5%). Only 23 of 236 rose.
- **Lowest in 2023:** Macao 0.66, Hong Kong 0.72, South Korea 0.72, Saint Barthelemy 0.80, Taiwan 0.87, Puerto Rico 0.94, Singapore 0.94, Ukraine 0.98, China 0.999.
- **Biggest 10-year falls, as a share:** Curacao 49%, Hong Kong 43%, Macao 42%, China 42%, South Korea 40%, Philippines 37%, Puerto Rico 36%, Argentina 36%, East Timor 36%, Ukraine 36%, Chile 35%.
- **Peer check for the US:** 1.84 to 1.62 (2013 to 2023). High-income countries as a group: 1.67 to 1.47. Europe: 1.59 to 1.39. The US falls at about the same pace as its peers and stays above them.
- **Break check:** the biggest one-year jumps are real history, not load errors.
  - North Korea 1951: -2.40 (the war).
  - China 1962 and 1963: +2.22 and +1.43 (the rebound after the famine).
  - Romania 1967: +1.71 (the year abortion was banned).
  - So the series do catch real shocks.
- **Covid check:** 54 of 236 countries rose from 2020 to 2021. Then 212 of 236 fell from 2021 to 2023.

**Hit means:** most of the world is at its fertility floor right now, and nine places are under one child per woman.
**Miss would have meant:** 2023 was a dip, with most countries still above earlier lows. It wasn't.

**Why it's not live**
- These are the UN's modeled estimates, republished by Our World in Data. They aren't our count of births. The UN and every big outlet have already run this story.
- No join adds anything. The only key is a 3-letter country code. Nothing in the warehouse hangs harm, money or enforcement on it.
- A hostile editor asks "what did you find that the UN didn't publish?" The honest answer is nothing.

**The dull explanation:** fertility has fallen steadily for 70 years, so the last year in any series is usually the lowest.
- Tested: 212 of 236 fell 2021-2023, and only 23 rose over ten years. The dull explanation is the whole explanation.

**Traps**
- ⚠ **Vatican is 1.000 in all 74 years.** It's a filled placeholder, not a measurement. Rank "lowest fertility" without dropping it, and the Vatican lands at #10.
- Fertility and year are stored as text in the REFERENCE copy. The TIMELINE copy stores them as numbers. Use that one.
- 387 rows have a blank code: England and Wales, Scotland, and five UN development groups.
  - Twelve more names use `OWID_` codes, Kosovo among them.
  - A filter on "3-letter code" keeps 236 names and drops Kosovo.
- England and Wales, and Scotland, both stop in 1949. They're historical stubs, not the UK.
- ⚠ **The glance's value counts don't reproduce.**
  - It said the value 1 shows up 211 times, 3.724 143 times, SWE 207, ESP 176.
  - Real counts: 74 (all Vatican), 2, 133, 102.
  - The name count (Sweden 133) and the blank-code count (387) did match.

## 2. Census state map table: **skip**

**One check (S05):** joined it to REF__DIM_STATE on FIPS, full outer join.
- 56 rows on each side, 56 matched, 0 orphans.
- Postal codes agree on all 56. Names agree on 54.
- One load run. Map year 2023. Area-type code 00 on every row.
- Land area of the 50 states plus DC sums to 9.15 million km². That's the right size for the US.

**Traps**
- ⚠ **The load timestamp is off by a factor of a million.**
  - `_INGESTED_AT` holds 1,787,427,725,357,615. That's microseconds since 1970, stored as if it were seconds.
  - Snowflake reads it as the year 56,643,299.
  - The Python door crashed fetching it (S04: "Python int too large to convert to C int").
  - The real load time was 2026-08-22. Read the column as text, or leave it out.
  - Same family as the known "INGESTED_AT holds epoch micros" trap.
- Land and water area are stored as text. Cast before summing.

## 3. REF__DIM_STATE: **skip**

- 56 rows: 50 states, DC, 5 territories. Five columns: FIPS, postal code, name, Census region, Census division.
- FIPS is 2 characters, leading zero kept, on every row.
- Regions: South 17 (DC counts as South), West 13, Midwest 12, Northeast 9, Territory 5.
- No load-lineage columns at all.

**Trap**
- Two territory names differ from the Census table.
  - Census table: "Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands", "United States Virgin Islands".
  - This table: "Northern Mariana Islands", "Virgin Islands".
  - A name join between the two misses 2 of 56. Join on FIPS.

## 4. USGS TopoView: **dead**

**What it actually holds (S07, S08)**
- 250 rows, 250 distinct map IDs. No duplicate rows. The glance's "2 rows per ID" doesn't reproduce.
- 217 are Puerto Rico sheets at 1:10,000 scale, published 1943-1950.
- 32 are mainland 1:100,000 sheets, 1979-1994. Their place names run alphabetically, Abbeville to Albuquerque, and stop there.
- 1 stray: Fort Pierce, FL, 1:10,000, 1972.
- 241 distinct titles. Nine mainland sheets appear twice under one title: two scans of the same map.

**Why dead**
- It's one alphabetical page of the TopoView catalog, not the catalog. There's no count here worth ranking.
- ⚠ **8 of 18 columns are empty on every row:** MPDESC, FILEFORMAT, COUNTIES, MAPSCALE, FIPS, STATE, DATASETS, FILESIZE.
  - SOURCENAME says "ScienceBase" on all 250.
  - State and scale live only inside the title text.
- No load-lineage columns. S06 failed looking for one.
- The catalog's plain-English note says FILESIZE holds text "like 77KB". Here it's empty on all 250.

## 5. ITIS rank types: **skip**

**One check (S09):** pulled all 182 rows.
- 182 rows, 182 distinct keys. 7 kingdom IDs, 21 to 33 ranks each. 41 distinct rank names.
- Every "parent rank" points to a rank that exists in the same kingdom. The tree is intact.
- One load run, one source file.

- That's the edit history of a code list. No story in it.
