## 1. PENDING_INITIAL_LOGGING_AND_TRACKING_NON_PHYSICIANS: **live**, one name

**What it is:** 6,880 NPIs with a name, and nothing else. No date, no state, no provider type.
The list landed 2026-07-26. An "initial" enrollment means a **new enrollment record**, not someone's first time billing Medicare.

**Checked**
- Joined each NPI to NPPES (for NPI age, taxonomy and deactivation), PECOS enrollment, the order-and-referring list, OIG exclusions (LEIE), opt-outs, Part B 2024 billing and Part D 2024 prescribing. Each side was grouped to one row per NPI first, so no fan-out: 6,880 in, 6,880 out.
- Did the same for the physicians' pending list (7,240 NPIs) to get a peer group.
- For the top payee: pulled every Part B service line, ranked her against all 203,212 NPs in Part B 2024, and checked skin-graft share for the top 60 NPs.

**The lead:** NPI 1760952360, Mary Huntly, NP, Las Vegas NV.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Part B 2024 payment | **$8,519,636** for 329 patients |
| Biggest line | Q4205 "membrane graft or wrap, per sq cm": **$5,912,560** (69%), 31 patients, 5,678 units |
| Lines CMS hid (under 11 patients each) | $2,406,634 |
| Rank among all 203,212 NPs | #71 nationally, #5 in Nevada |
| Median NP, nation / Nevada | $13,301 / $15,784 → she is **640x** the national median |
| Next-largest 2024 payee on **either** pending list | $638,449 (a Florida dermatologist, physicians' list) → she is **13x** that |
| Median of the 42 pending non-physicians who billed in 2024 and have no current PECOS record | $9,352 |
| Current PECOS enrollment | **none** |
| Order-and-referring list | **not on it** |
| OIG exclusion list (landed 2026-08-27) | not on it |
| NPPES | active NPI, enumerated 2018, taxonomy "RN, wound care" |

**What a hit means:** she billed as an enrolled NP in 2024. She now has no active record in either CMS file, and she is in the queue for a new one.
**What a miss would have meant:** nothing. Most pending people never billed (only 111 of 6,880 did).

**Peer check: is she unusual among NPs?** Not among skin-graft billers.
- 160 NPs got over $5M from Part B in 2024. 1,265 got over $1M.
- 40 of the top 61 NPs got more than half their pay from skin-graft codes (Q41xx-Q43xx, A20xx).
- 7 of the 160 over-$5M NPs (4.4%) are missing from current PECOS. For all NPs it's 1.8%.
- **She is the only over-$5M NP on either pending list.**

**Dull explanation, not tested:** she moved, changed employer, or let her old enrollment lapse, and is simply re-applying. Skin grafts were legal to bill in 2024.
**What would settle it:** CMS's revoked-provider list. It's not in the warehouse; the CMS catalog (table 5) lists it. After that, court and Nevada nursing-board records.

**Table facts (not stories)**
- All 6,880 NPIs are distinct. 31 fail the NPI check digit.
- 453 (6.6%) are not in NPPES. 422 of them pass the check digit. NPPES landed 2026-07-02, the pending list 2026-07-26, so most are probably brand-new NPIs.
- Taxonomy mix: counselors (101Y) 1,689, NPs 1,629, social workers 1,082, PAs 447, marriage and family therapists 249. **Counselors plus family therapists are 28%**, and both only became Medicare-eligible in 2024.
- 2,400 (35%) are already on the order-and-referring list, but only 104 of those are in PECOS. The likely reason: they are enrolled only to order and refer, and are now applying to bill.
- 3 NPIs are on the OIG exclusion list (excluded 2020, 2021, 2025, none reinstated). They're pending, not approved.
- 40 NPIs are currently deactivated in NPPES.

**Date of the list:** the newest supplier NPI was issued 2026-02-13, so this is a 2026 list. Billing data is 2024. There's only one billing year, so no time comparison was possible.

- Part B 2024 provider totals for every listed supplier and every "Medicare Diabetes Preventive Program" provider.
- NPPES for all 307 supplier NPIs: all 307 found, and 31 were issued in 2024 or later.

**What came back**

- **Concentration:** the top 5 suppliers hold 40% of patients. The median supplier had 22. Johns Hopkins alone had 175 (14%).

## 3. IHS_FACILITIES: **live**, but thin

**What it is:** 1,006 clinics and hospitals, as of June 2023. The raw load has 1,010 rows. The 4 extras are most likely the spreadsheet title and header rows that the staging filter drops. Not checked row by row.
The 44 hospitals have 1,583 of the 2,236 beds on the list: 21 run by IHS, 23 by tribes.

**Checked**
- Matched each hospital to Care Compare (HOSPITAL_GENERAL) on state + ZIP or city, then eyeballed every name. 43 of 44 matched. Dilkon Medical Center didn't: its ZIP hit Winslow's hospital, so that match was rejected.
- Pulled bed counts for every Care Compare hospital from the Provider of Services file (on CCN), to size-match peers.
- Peer group 1: other acute-care hospitals in the same 13 states, split by bed band. Peer group 2: VA and military hospitals.

**What came back: can patients see a quality grade?**

| Beds (POS file) | IHS-list acute: rated | Same-state peers: rated |
|---|---|---|
| 25 or fewer | 0 of 4 | 4 of 32 (12%) |
| 26-50 | 2 of 12 | 28 of 48 (58%) |
| 51-100 | 4 of 7 | 74 of 77 (96%) |
| 101-200 | 1 of 3 | 100 of 105 (95%) |
| **Total** | **7 of 26** | expected at peer rates: **~17** |

- Patient-survey scores (HCAHPS) missing: **12 of 26**, where peer rates predict about 5.
- Unrated includes Phoenix Indian Medical Center (179 beds in POS), Tuba City (103), Pine Ridge, Rosebud.
- IHS-run 3 of 14 rated; tribal-run 4 of 12. **Who runs it isn't the difference.**
- Critical access hospitals: 1 of 17 rated, vs 20% of same-state peers.
- **Federal peers:** VA hospitals **112 of 132 rated (85%)**. Military hospitals 0 of 32.

**Dull explanation, not tested:** IHS hospitals may not be required to report. And the death and readmission scores come from Medicare fee-for-service claims, which a younger, IHS-covered population produces fewer of.
The patient-survey gap doesn't depend on Medicare volume, so that explanation covers less of it.

**Traps and oddities**
- 16 of the 43 matched CCNs are **missing from the Medicare hospital enrollment file**. For acute hospitals overall, 97% are present; for critical access hospitals, 99.6%.
- CMS's ownership field contradicts the IHS operator on 6 of 43. Lawton and Hopi are IHS-run but called "Tribal". Alaska Native Medical Center, Hu-Hu-Kam, Cherokee NC and MS Choctaw are tribal-run but called "Government - Federal".
- The two IHS lists disagree on beds for 11 of 44 hospitals (Gallup 99 vs 74, Claremore 46 vs 32). The code book has no date, so there's no telling which list is newer.
- 22 facilities active in June 2023 are marked Inactive in the code book, e.g. Colville Indian Health Center and Bishop Toiyabe.
- WORKLOAD is a 0/1 flag. APC_FLAG has no definition.
- Care Compare says "no emergency services" at 8 of 43, including Phoenix Indian Medical Center and Whiteriver. Not checked against primary records.

## 4. VA_SUICIDE_APPENDIX: **probed**

**What it is:** two report tables stacked into one sheet. Block 1 is the totals by sex, 23 rows. Block 2 is by age group, 115 rows. There are 6 header and blank rows.

**Checked**
- Every data row against VA_SUICIDE_NATIONAL (veteran cohort): **0 mismatches** out of 138. **It's a copy.**
- Veterans vs non-veterans from the national table. Men compared to men, because veterans are mostly male and an all-ages comparison mostly measures sex mix.

**What came back**

| | 2001 | 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Veteran suicides | 6,021 | 6,398 (+6%) |
| Veteran population | 25.9M | 18.2M (−30%) |
| Age-adjusted veteran rate | 24.1 | **39.0** (+62%) |
| Male veterans 18-34, per 100K | 25.8 | **54.8** |
| Male non-veterans 18-34 | 20.1 | 26.7 |
| Ratio | 1.28x | **2.05x** |

- **Dull explanation tested:** a shrinking, aging veteran population. The age-adjusted rate still rose 62%, so aging doesn't explain it.
- **Not story-grade:** the VA publishes these numbers in its annual report.

**Traps**
- COL_1 holds deaths in block 1 and the age label in block 2.
- "All" rows repeat the totals. Summing COL_2 gives 294,008; the true 2001-23 total is **147,180**.
- The age groups sum short of "All": 4 short in 2001, 41 short in 2023. Probably deaths with unknown age.
- Female age bands differ: 55+ where men have 55-74 and 75+. A "." marks blank cells.
- **Use VA_SUICIDE_NATIONAL instead.** It's typed and has non-veteran and VHA-user cohorts.
