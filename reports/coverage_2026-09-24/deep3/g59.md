## 1. ECONOMICS__INTL_FAO_FAOSTAT_FOOD_SECURITY: **live**

**What it is:** 279,470 rows. 69 measures × 249 areas × years. 43 areas are regions or income groups; the rest are countries.
**The key measure:** the survey-based hunger scale (FIES), which counts people who skipped meals or went a day without eating. It comes in "moderate or severe" and "severe" versions, as 3-year averages from 2014-16 to 2022-24.

### Concentration with a denominator: whose hunger has no name [q06, fao5.py]
World total vs the sum of every published country figure, same item, same period:

| Measure (millions of people) | Period | World total | Sum of published countries | No country attached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Severe food insecurity | 2014-16 | 567.2 | 177.0 (71 countries) | **390.2 (69%)** |
| Severe food insecurity | 2022-24 | 836.5 | 383.7 (101 countries) | **452.8 (54%)** |
| Moderate or severe | 2022-24 | 2,287.9 | 1,297.1 (123 countries) | **990.8 (43%)** |
| Undernourishment (FAO's model, not a survey) | 2022-24 | 685.6 | 616.5 | 69.1 (10%) |

- The model-based count (undernourishment) mostly adds up. The survey-based count doesn't. So the missing half isn't missing data. It's missing *publication*.
- **Countries hold these numbers back** [fao2.py]. 21 areas carry flag `Q` and a blank value on **every one of the 9 periods**, on both FIES measures:
  India, China (mainland, Hong Kong, Taiwan), Bangladesh, Türkiye, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, Syria, Sudan, Venezuela, Nicaragua, Bolivia, Panama, Congo, Gabon, Guinea, Jordan, Mozambique, Rwanda.
- Another 26 places are flag `O`, blank everywhere. These are mostly small or closed: North Korea, Cuba, Morocco, Turkmenistan, Gulf states, Pacific islands.
- **Denominator, using FAO's own model** [fao3.py]: the `Q` countries hold **226.7M of the world's 685.6M undernourished people (33%)** in 2022-24. **India alone: 172.1M (25%).** In 2014-16: 222.8M of 568.7M (39%).

**Time check:** the `Q` list is the same in every period since 2014-16. This is a standing policy, not a new blackout. The share with no name fell from 69% to 54% because 30 more countries got published.

**What a hit means:** half the people FAO counts as severely food insecure can't be placed in any country from FAO's own public table, and a handful of big governments explain most of the gap.
**What a miss would have meant:** if the published countries summed to the world total, suppression would be cosmetic. They sum to 46%.

**The dull explanation, partly tested:**
- FAO publishes a country's survey result only with that government's consent (FAO's published rule, outside the warehouse). Governments like India dispute the method: a Gallup poll of roughly a thousand people a year, when they run their own surveys. That's a method fight, not proof of concealment.
- Not all of the gap is `Q`. Some is FAO's fill-in for the 26 no-data places, and some is 25 "<0.5" values (at most 12.5M together). Neither comes close to 452.8M.
- Not tested: FAOSTAT's flag legend reads `Q` as "missing value; suppressed". The warehouse has no flag lookup. Confirm it on FAOSTAT before print.

**Side note, US:** the US figure is flag `A` (official figure, taken from USDA's survey), 8.0% → 10.3% moderate-or-severe from 2019-21 to 2022-24. That's not an outlier among rich countries: Canada 10.2%, Australia 13.8%, New Zealand 17.3%, Finland 13.4%. If USDA's survey stops, this series has no source (outside knowledge, not checked).

### Traps [q02, q06]
- ⚠ **Aggregates hide under country codes.** `AREA_CODE` under 5000 is supposed to mean a country. But 351 = China (the sum of mainland, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan), 420 = Sub-Saharan Africa, 429 = Northern Africa excluding Sudan. Summing "countries" double-counts **282M** undernourished people.
- ⚠ **Every FIES row comes three times:** the value, a lower bound, an upper bound (`ELEMENT`). Sum without filtering and you triple it.
- ⚠ **3-year averages overlap** (2020-22, 2021-23, 2022-24). Never sum across `YEAR_CODE`.
- ⚠ **`VALUE` is text.** "<0.1", "<0.5" and "<2.5" become NULL under `try_to_double` and quietly drop out: 1,353 rows on undernourishment prevalence alone.
- The yearly (not averaged) FIES series exists only for the 45 regional aggregates, never for a country.

## 2. ECONOMICS__INTL_GLEIF_RELATIONSHIPS: **probed**

**What it is:** 485,285 links, one per child, parent and link type. No duplicate edges; each child has at most one parent per type [q01].
Types: fund-managed-by 149,585 · ultimately consolidated by 132,600 · directly consolidated by 126,429 · subfund-of 73,338 · branch-of 1,948 · feeder-to 1,385.
The file runs through at least 2026-07-28. The companion entity table (`ECONOMICS__INTL_GLEIF`, 3.38M LEIs) is a 2026-07-23 snapshot [q11, q12].

### The join: OFAC SDN list × GLEIF ownership [q09, q10, q11, q12]
- **LEIs on the primary OFAC table:** 173 SDN rows carry "Legal Entity Number" in `REMARKS`. That's 164 LEIs; **163 land in GLEIF (99%)**.
- OpenSanctions (`INTL_OPENSANCTIONS_DEFAULT`, SDN rows only) yields 523 LEI-shaped tokens, and 451 land. 163 of the 164 primary LEIs are among them. The rest are OpenSanctions enrichment, so they need a name check (see the Castle Peak trap).
- Parents on the SDN list (OpenSanctions set): **45**. Lukoil's LEI is also on OFAC's own record. Rosneft's comes only from OpenSanctions, and its name agrees. Companies GLEIF says they consolidate: **128**. Not on the SDN list themselves: **94**. Outside Russia and Belarus: **60**.
- Still current (`ISSUED`) outside Russia and Belarus: **12**. The eyeball cut it to 9 [q12]:
  - ✗ Castle Peak Power Finance (BVI): false match. OpenSanctions pinned Castle Peak Power's LEI to an SDN record named "Gifted Peak Limited".
  - ✗ Dega Retail Holding (BVI): Sistema's stake is 42.09%, and the link record itself is LAPSED.
  - ✗ LUKOIL Securities Ltd (Cyprus): the link record lapsed 2026-07-26.

**Peer comparison, by when the parent was listed:**

| Russian SDN group | Foreign subsidiaries in GLEIF | Still ISSUED |
|---|---|---|
| Lukoil + Rosneft (listed Oct 22, 2025; date from OFAC, outside the warehouse) | 18 | **9 → 8 after the eyeball** |
| Sovcomflot, Sberbank, VTB, Severstal, Megafon, VEB, Gazprombank, Alfa (listed before 2025, outside the warehouse) | 28 | **0** |

- **Renewed after Oct 22, 2025** (registration updated, next renewal set a year out): LUKOIL Finance B.V. (2025-11-01), PETROTEL-LUKOIL SA (2025-11-26), LUKOIL International Finance B.V. (2025-12-07), LUKOIL International Upstream Holding B.V. (2025-12-16), Rosneft Deutschland GmbH (2026-02-18), LUKOIL Securities B.V. (2026-03-24), LITASCO SA (2026-07-13). **7.**
- Ownership on file: the four Dutch B.V.s 100%, Petrotel 99.77%. That's above OFAC's 50% line, so they're blocked without being named (OFAC's 50% rule, outside the warehouse).
- None of the 9 is on any sanctions list by LEI in OpenSanctions. The only by-name OFAC hit is "SISTEMA ASIA PTE LTD", registration 201218988Z. GLEIF's Sistema Asia Fund is 201530536M: **a different company** [q11, q12].

**Why not live, the dull explanation:**
- Groups listed earlier did the same thing. VTB Capital plc's registration ran to 2023-06-15. So it was renewed around June 2022, after VTB's listing, and then lapsed.
- Renewals during a licensed wind-down or sale are expected. Lukoil's foreign assets are up for sale under OFAC licenses. Rosneft Deutschland is under German state trusteeship (both outside the warehouse, not tested).
- **What a hit would need:** a renewal or a new subsidiary with no license covering it. The warehouse can't see licenses.

**Use it as a tool:** re-run q09 monthly. A Lukoil subsidiary whose ultimate parent changes means a sale closed; one that lapses has wound down.

### Traps [q01, q10, q12]
- ⚠ **Ownership share over 100%: 15 rows**, up to 213,800. The glance's "15 rows over 10x median" are these typos, not big stakes. The share is filled on only 11% of links, and it's mostly 100.
- ⚠ **Dates:** 167 period-1 start dates are in the future (up to 2070), and 39 are before 1900 (from 1832).
- ⚠ **Stale links:** 29% of ultimate-parent links are LAPSED (38,356 of 132,600): the claim hasn't been re-confirmed in a year. 41.5% are ENTITY_SUPPLIED_ONLY, never checked against an outside source.
- ⚠ **OpenSanctions `IDENTIFIERS` hold other companies' LEIs.** The Castle Peak case, plus Sistema's LEI sitting on the Motovilikhinskiye record. Use the OFAC `REMARKS` LEIs as the primary key, and name-check any OpenSanctions extras.
- ⚠ **The OpenSanctions `SANCTIONS` column is not "government sanctions."** It includes fines and regulatory actions: Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs and BNP Paribas showed up [q08, discarded]. Filter on the `DATASETS` name.
- Wrong in an older note (F-083): links are not "all active". 60 are INACTIVE and 322 have a blank status.

## 3. ECONOMICS__INTL_IPC_FOOD_INSECURITY_GLOBAL: **probed**

### Time: how old is "latest" [ipc.py]
- Analyses run from May 2021 to May 2026.
- **6 of 50 are older than 18 months:** Ethiopia May 2021, Angola Jun 2021, El Salvador Aug 2021, Timor-Leste Nov 2023, Burkina Faso Mar 2024, Dominican Republic Nov 2024.
- ⚠ **Ranking by famine phase crowns a 2021 number.** Phase-5 people across the table total 664,810. **Ethiopia's May 2021 analysis alone is 352,896 (53%).** Then Sudan 134,808 (Jan 2026), Gaza 103,800 (Nov 2025), South Sudan 73,306 (projection).
- Dull explanation: Ethiopia stopped endorsing IPC analyses after 2021 (outside knowledge, not tested). This table can't show why a country goes quiet.

### Peer: share of the analyzed population in phase 3+ [ipc.py]
Gaza (`PSE`) 77% · South Sudan 55% · Haiti 52% · Angola 49% (2021, 8% coverage) · Yemen 47.5% (30% coverage) · Sudan 41%. Median across the 49 with people analyzed: 13.8%.
Known story. Nothing here that isn't already in IPC's own releases.

### Traps
- ⚠ **Projections cover different people.** Sudan: the current period analyzes 47.5M, the projection only 8.3M. Ethiopia: 56.3M, then 19.7M. Comparing current and projected counts is apples to oranges. Compare shares.
- ⚠ **Coverage:** in 9 countries, under half the population was analyzed (Uganda 3%, Angola 8%, Tanzania 16%, Pakistan 21%, Benin 21%, Yemen 30%, Madagascar 30%, Kenya 34%, Ethiopia 49%). Counts there are floors.
- ⚠ `TOTAL_COUNTRY_POPULATION` = 0 for Mozambique and Zambia. Ecuador's analyzed population is bigger than its total (1.01). Gambia's current period is all zeros.
- 5 countries have no current period, only projections: DR Congo, South Sudan, Haiti, Central African Republic, Lebanon. Don't mix them into "current" totals.
