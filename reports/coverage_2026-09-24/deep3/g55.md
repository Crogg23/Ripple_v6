## 1. Treasury Daily Statement: the customs refund wave. **live**

**What it is.** One line per cash category per business day, Oct 2005 to Aug 7, 2026. Amounts in $ millions.
The account is the Federal Reserve Account through Sep 2021 and the TGA after that.

**Headline.** From May 2026, cash poured out of the Treasury through the Customs and Border Protection line.
- In June 2026, customs refunds were **twice** gross customs collections.
- The Monthly Treasury Statement, a second Treasury table, books it as **customs duty refunds**.

**Checked**
- **Time, DTS.** CBP withdrawals, Oct 1 to Aug 7 of each fiscal year:

| Fiscal year | CBP cash out | Customs cash in | Out as % of in |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2022 | $6.5B | $97.7B | 6.6% |
| FY2023 | $7.3B | $82.4B | 8.9% |
| FY2024 | $8.8B | $78.7B | 11.2% |
| FY2025 | $9.0B | $154.0B | 5.8% |
| **FY2026** | **$128.6B** | $282.5B | **45.5%** |

- **Time, second source.** MTS customs duties by month, 136 months (Mar 2015 to Jun 2026), no gaps:
  - Median monthly refund before May 2026: **$0.43B**. Highest: $1.86B (Mar 2026).
  - Highest refund-to-gross share before May 2026: 36% (Aug 2015).

| Month | Gross customs | Refunds | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apr 2026 | $23.88B | $1.75B | +$22.12B |
| May 2026 | $21.93B | **$21.97B** | **-$0.04B** |
| Jun 2026 | $23.63B | **$49.18B** | **-$25.56B** |

  - May plus June refunds: **$71.16B**. The 134 months before that, added together: **$60.73B**.
  - Refunds for the fiscal year through June: $81.30B. Same point a year earlier: $5.27B.
- **Two tables agree.** DTS CBP out vs MTS refunds: May $23.33B vs $21.97B. June $51.01B vs $49.18B.
  - The $1.4-1.8B gap is about CBP's other spending, which ran $0.6-1.2B a month in 2025.
- **July, DTS only** (MTS for July isn't loaded yet): $35.54B out through CBP, $26.13B customs in. Net **-$9.4B**.
- **Not a one-day blip.**
  - Since May 11: 63 business days, $113.5B. At the FY2023-25 average of $40M a day, that's about **$111B above normal**.
  - The median day is $612M. That beats the biggest single CBP day of FY2023-25: $379M on 2025-09-25.
  - 38 of the 63 days top that old record.
  - The three biggest days carry 45% of it. Jun 29, 2026 alone: **$35.49B**.
- **Peer.** 75 withdrawal lines have Dec-Jul history for FY2023-26 and at least $1B in FY2025.
  - CBP: 17.1x its FY2025 level.
  - Next highest: business tax refunds, 2.4x.
  - Median line: 1.01x.
- **It started before May.** Oct 2025 to Apr 2026 refunds: $10.15B, against $4.10B a year earlier (2.5x).
- **Collections are sliding too.** Gross customs fell from $33.09B (Oct 2025) to $23.63B (Jun 2026). That's 29% down.

**The dull explanations, tested**
- "A line got renamed or reclassified." The MTS counts it as customs refunds, the same size, on its own line. **Ruled out.**
- "Normal drawback refunds." Those ran $1.6-7.5B a **year** from FY2015 to FY2025. **Ruled out.**
- "Refunds of tariffs after a court or policy reversal." **Most likely.** Neither table says why the money went out.
  - That's the catch: the event itself is probably public. What the data adds is the size, the speed and the negative month.

**Hit means.** For two straight months, and a third on DTS, the U.S. paid back more at the border than it collected.
**Miss would have meant.** The cash out was CBP's own spending, or an accounting move. The MTS rules both out.

**Next step.**
- When the July 2026 MTS loads, check it against DTS's $35.5B.
- Pull CBP's refund notices to name the legal reason.
- Parked for a later pass: business tax refunds 2.4x, Dept of State 2.2x, DHS misc 2.0x.

**Traps (for the ledger)**
- ⚠ **The table is named DEPOSITS, but 279,726 of its 478,149 rows (58%) are withdrawals.**
- ⚠ **Totals are mixed in with the detail lines.**
  - 2,890 total and sub-total rows hold 28.4% of the daily-amount sum.
  - 10,474 public-debt issue and redemption rows hold 49.8%.
  - The glance's "top 1% hold 73%" is debt rollover and totals.
- **Lines get renamed, so a series that "stops" is often a rename.**
  - Customs deposits were renamed on 2025-11-10.
  - The USAID line ends 2025-09-30, after $20.3B in FY2025. In FY2026, "IAP - misc" jumps from $0.78B to $7.42B.
  - "HHS - Payments to States" ends 2025-09-30. "HHS - Payments to Children and Families" starts the next business day.
  - "USDA - Loan Payments" ends 2025-09-30 too. "USDA - Rural Services" starts the next business day.
- **The account changes on 2021-10-01**, from Federal Reserve Account to TGA.
- ⚠ **MTS trap:** `CURRENT_MONTH_NET_RCPT_AMT` is blank whenever refunds beat gross.
  - 25 rows on 4 lines: corporate income tax, FUTA, misc excise and customs.
  - Summing the monthly net silently drops customs' -$25.56B June.
  - Use gross minus refund, or the change in FYTD net.

## 3. PBGC pension data tables. **dead**

**What it is.** PBGC's yearly pension data book, 14 editions (2010-2023), about 80 tables (S-1 to S-59, M-1 to M-23), flattened to one number per row.

**Checked**
- **Can a number be read?** Group by edition + table + row label.
  - **132,712 of 134,534 rows (98.6%)** share that key with other values.
  - Example: 2023 edition, S-1, row "1980" holds -94.6, 430.0 and 524.1.
  - Those are probably assets, liabilities and net position. Nothing in the row says which.
  - The GLANCE page has the same problem. "Number of Insured Plans" shows 24,289, 22,955 and 1,334 with no column.
- **Landing (S06).** The raw load has the same 10 columns. No column-header field. The header was lost at load.
- **Repeats.** Every edition reprints history. S-1's row "2008" shows up in all 14 editions, 42 rows.
  - Adding across `DATA_YEAR` multiplies.
- 20,997 rows have a blank row label.
- The mart has 134,534 rows; staging had 140,454. The 5,920 dropped rows are probably text values like `---`, `*` and `$ 7.7 billion`, which the landing profile shows. Not checked row by row.

**Why dead.** You can't tie a value to what it measures. The glance's "sum 29.7T" mixes counts, $ millions and fractions.
**Fix.** Reload from `SOURCE_FILE` (PBGC's xlsx files) and keep the column header on each value.

## 5. USAspending API sample. **dead**

**What it is.** 300 rows from USAspending's award-search API, all $151M or more, totaling $3.33T.
- 100 grants (83 of them Medicaid grants to states, CFDA 93.778) and 200 contracts.
- Recipient UEI, DUNS and EIN are blank on every row.

**Checked (one join, S09 and S13)**
- **Grants:** 100 of 100 are in `ASSISTANCE_FULL` by FAIN.
- **Contracts:** 100 of 200 are in `CONTRACTS_FULL_R2` by PIID.
  - The 100 misses are exactly the 100 with a blank `END_DATE`. The 100 hits all have one.
  - The misses are IDV parent awards, contract vehicles that orders are placed under. Examples: CDC vaccine IDVs to Merck (`75D30124D18656`), NASA's `NNJ16GX08B` to SpaceX.
- ⚠ **R2 has 38 columns and no parent-award ID.** Orders under an IDV can't be rolled up to it in R2.
  - Rolling up needs `CONTRACTS_FULL`'s `PARENT_AWARD_ID_PIID`, but that table is capped at 1M rows a year.

**Why dead.** Everything here is in the full tables, except IDV totals, and those have no key to join on.
**Trap.** `CFDA_NUMBER` is an empty string, not NULL, on the contracts. An `IS NOT NULL` test calls all 300 rows grants (my S07 did exactly that).
