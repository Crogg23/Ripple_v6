## 1. ATF gun licenses: one Massachusetts building holds 49 gun-maker licenses. **live**

**Checked**
- Pulled all 77,514 licenses (S02). Each FFL number is its own row, no duplicates.
- Types:
  - 45,391 dealers (01)
  - 21,932 manufacturers (07)
  - 5,827 pawnbrokers (02)
  - The rest: ammo makers, importers, destructive-device licenses
- Grouped every license by building: premise street with the suite, unit or box stripped, plus city and state. That's my address cleanup, so a building spelled two ways counts twice.
  - 72,831 buildings. The median building has 1 license.
- **Peer, national:** only 2 buildings in the country hold 10+ separate licensees:

| Building | Licenses | Separate names | Type 07 | Separate phones | Mailing cities |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 410 Great Rd, Littleton, MA | 51 | 49 | 49 | 49 | 38 |
| 992 Bedford St, Bridgewater, MA | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 6 |
| 2121 Old Dunbar Rd, West Columbia, SC | 14 | 9 | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| 379 Liberty St, Rockland, MA | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 7 |

- The West Columbia row is one company's cluster: 9 names share 4 phones. The Massachusetts rows are not. Each licensee has its own phone.
- The Littleton licenses sit in numbered suites like "STE 6-2-19" and "SUITE 622-B". The licensees' mailing addresses are spread across 38 towns: Boston, Cambridge, Newton, Revere, Somerset and more.
- **Peer, by state:** share of a state's type-07 licenses sitting in buildings with 4+ separate licensees:
  - Massachusetts: **24.4%** (74 of 303, in 4 buildings)
  - South Carolina: 2.0%
  - Connecticut: 1.9%
  - Nevada: 1.8%
  - Median state: 0
- **Per capita (2020 census):** Massachusetts is the only state where maker licenses (07 + 10: 312) outnumber dealer + pawn licenses (263).
  - It is 48th of 50 states in dealers + pawnshops per 100K (3.7). The median state is 19.9.
  - Take out the four shared buildings and the makers drop to 238. The ratio falls to 0.90, second behind Arizona (0.92).
- **Join tried, CDC firearm deaths by county (S09).** ATF's county code is the FIPS county code; 99.1% land. Connecticut (656) misses because the county table uses the 2022 planning regions.
  - **The join can't test the question.** Only 946 of 3,133 counties have any unsuppressed firearm-suicide count, 2019-2024. None has under 17,285 people.
  - The smallest third of counties has zero usable counts, and that's where dealer density is highest.
- **Snapshot date, from the expiration codes:** the newest code is July 2029 (421 licenses, a part month). So the list is about July 2026.
- **Side finding: 31 licenses are listed past their expiration.** They expired between September 2024 and March 2026.
  - Another 1,916 expired April-June 2026. That looks like the normal renewal backlog.
  - The oldest: Four Aces Gun and Pawn, Houston MO, expired September 2024.
  - Three pawn licenses of Bullets Inc (Evansville IN, Owensboro KY, Henderson KY) all expired May-July 2025.

**Hit means:** 1 in 4 Massachusetts gun-maker licenses sits in four rent-a-suite buildings. Most licensees get their mail in other towns. The story is how federal maker licenses get placed in a strict-law state. It doesn't show wrongdoing.

**Miss means:** if ATF inspects these suites as real places of business, it's a landlord's business model, and the story is a feature, not a scandal.

**Boring:**
- The dull reading: local zoning and police-chief sign-off keep home-based licenses out of Massachusetts towns, so licensees rent commercial suites. **Not tested.** The warehouse has no zoning or state-license data.
- Type 07 also covers gunsmiths and coating shops (names like "T&B Coatings", "Laser Engraving & Refinishing"). **Partly tested:** 49 separate phones and 38 mailing towns rule out one company splitting its licenses.
- The 31 past-expiration licenses: a timely renewal keeps a license valid until ATF acts on it. A denial under appeal does too. **Not tested.** No ATF revocation list is in the warehouse.

**Traps**
- ⚠ Connecticut county codes don't land in DIM_COUNTY (2022 planning regions). That's 656 licenses.
- ⚠ CDC county firearm counts are suppressed in every county under ~17K people. A dealer-density-vs-suicide join is blind at the rural end.
- ⚠ `FED_FBI_NICS_CHECKS` (the join source) stops at **2023-09**. No 2024 or 2025 rows (S10, S12).
- The ZIP trap from 2026-09-23 is fixed in the mart: every premise ZIP is now 5 or 9 digits.
- `BUSINESS_NAME` holds the word "None" (or is blank) on 32,831 rows. 33 rows have lat/lon 0. One license has expiration code 9M (Dec 2029), past the 3-year window.

**Next join:** the Massachusetts state dealer-license list (MGL c.140 §122) or ATF inspection results by FFL number, for the four buildings. Neither is in the warehouse.

## 2. OFAC SDN: four tankers in NOAA's U.S. AIS feed in Jan 2024 are now sanctioned for Iranian oil. **probed**

**Checked**
- Profile (S05): 19,114 rows, one load run (2026-06-25), no duplicate entry numbers.
  - 9,785 organizations, 7,488 people, 1,497 vessels, 344 aircraft
- Parsed each vessel's IMO (1,489 distinct), MMSI (768) and call sign (898).
- **Join, NOAA AIS pings, Jan 1-8 2024 (S06).** That's NOAA's U.S. coastal feed, so it includes ships just offshore. Matched on any of the three IDs.
  - **4 hulls hit, all on IMO only.** 0 hits on MMSI or call sign.

| AIS name, Jan 2024 | SDN name now | IMO | Program | Pings / days | Where | On the SDN since |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADVANTAGE VIRTUE | LAFIT | 9379698 | IRAN-EO13902 | 1,322 / 6 | Gulf of Mexico off Texas (avg 28.4N, 94.0W) | 2025-08-21 |
| FEDOR | EDOR | 9259317 | IRAN-EO13902 | 642 / 2 | Florida Straits | 2026-04-24 |
| CONQUEROR | ROC | 9275660 | SDGT | 318 / 1 | South of St. Croix, U.S. Virgin Islands (16.2-17.7N) | 2025-05-13 |
| PGC MARINA | NORTH STAR | 9299563 | IRAN-EO13902 | 255 / 1 | Off Grand Bahama, Bahamian waters (avg 26.1N, 78.0W) | 2026-02-25 |

- **Second field:**
  - All 4 are "Crude Oil Tanker" on the SDN and type 80 (tanker) in AIS. Lengths are 228-330 m.
  - OpenSanctions aliases include "Advantage Virtue" (LAFIT) and "Conqueror" (ROC). The other two match on IMO alone.
- **Time:** "on the SDN since" is OpenSanctions' first-seen date (S07). That's 16 to 28 months after the pings.
- **Denominator (S07):** that AIS week had 951 tankers with an IMO. 405 were 200 m or longer. 4 of 405 = 1.0%.
- **The miss (S11):** 720 SDN hulls were already listed before 2024. **None shows up in the feed that week.** 763 were listed after the week. 4 of those pinged (0.5%).

**Hit means:** aging tankers leave the U.S. crude trade and turn up in Iran's shadow fleet within about two years.
ADVANTAGE VIRTUE was a 330 m tanker (VLCC size) working off Texas. By August 2025, 19 months later, it was on the SDN as LAFIT. OpenSanctions lists five other names for it.

**Miss means:** no ship already sanctioned shows up anywhere in the U.S. feed that week. The border held for eight days.

**Boring:**
- Normal secondhand sales of old tankers. The SDN lists build years 2003, 2005 and 2009 for three of them.
- The SDN links each hull only to its current owner, like CHANGBAI GLORY SHIPPING LIMITED for LAFIT. The Jan 2024 owners aren't in the warehouse.
- Trade press has covered the old-tanker-to-shadow-fleet path.
- **Not ruled out, and it's the likely reading.** 4 hits is thin.

**Traps**
- ⚠ **`IMO_NUMBER` is filled on 542 organization rows.** Those are company IMO numbers ("Identification Number IMO 5342883"), not ships. Any IMO join must filter to `ENTITY_KIND = 'vessel'`. None of the 542 collides with a ship IMO in this list.
- TONNAGE is filled on 7 rows, VESSEL_OWNER on 6, GROSS_REGISTERED_TONNAGE on 98. Don't rank on them.
- MMSI and call sign never matched. Sanctioned ships change them when they change flag. Use IMO.
- OpenSanctions FIRST_SEEN has a floor of 2023-04-20 (a rebuild). "Before 2024" is solid. The exact year before that isn't.

**What would make it live:** a longer AIS window, to catch a listed hull in U.S. waters **after** designation.
