## 1. Order and referring list: **live**

**Checked**
- 2,018,354 rows, 2,018,350 NPIs. All NPIs are well-formed 10-digit values.
- 4 NPIs repeat. They're name changes (GUMPERT/HIGH, ORTEGON/RUSH ORTEGON). One of them (CRAMTON) has different flags on its two rows.
- Five Y/N columns: PARTB, DME, HHA, PMD, HOSPICE.
  - **DME is Y on every row but 4.**
  - PARTB is N on 87,220 NPIs. All of them still show DME = Y.
- **Decoded PARTB = N (S24).** The PECOS enrollment file was loaded 2026-08-07, two days after this list.
  - Only 5,236 of the 87,220 Part B = N NPIs are in it (6.0%).
  - For Part B = Y combos it's 94-99%.
  - So Part B = N means "no current Medicare enrollment." It doesn't mean "can't order Part B."
- The same 87,220 are missing from FISS, the sister list. 64 are on it. For Part B = Y NPIs, 99.9% are on it.
- **Join 1: NPPES deactivations (NPI to NPI).** 3,054 list NPIs have a deactivated, not reactivated, NPI.
  - 2,952 of them are Part B = N.
  - 2,966 were deactivated since July 2025.
- **Carbon-dated the list (S14).** Its newest NPIs were enumerated 2026-06-06. That's the last day in our NPPES copy, and FISS matches it. 20,147 of its NPIs were enumerated in 2026. The file is current, not stale.
- **Time curve (S14).** List NPIs deactivated each month, as a share of all NPPES deactivations that month:

- It's a flat plateau, then a cliff. The list was loaded 2026-08-05, so the cliff sits 11-12 months before the load. That points to a fixed retention window, not a random lag.
- **Peer list: FISS**, loaded 2026-08-05 like this one. It holds 49 NPIs deactivated since Jan 2025. This list holds 2,988.
- **Join 2: Part B DY2024 (name check).** 1,058 of the 2,988 billed Part B in 2024. The last name agrees on 1,038 (98%).

- **Join 4: OIG exclusions (LEIE, real NPIs only).** These are purged fast.
  - 331 people were excluded after 2026-05-01. 29 are still on the list, which is normal lag.
  - About 7,900 individuals were excluded before 2026-05-01. **7** are still on the list with no waiver:

    - All five flags Y and on FISS too, but absent from PECOS: GRAY (1128b7, 2024-10-07), SYED (1128b7, 2024-12-12), PIRANI (1128b4, 2026-02-19), HANFLINK (1128b7, 2026-04-30)

- 10 more drawn by hash: all NYNYN or NYNNN. 5 of 10 billed Part B in 2024, and the name agrees on all 5.

**The lead**
- Medicare's order-and-referral list is what DME suppliers and home-health agencies check. It keeps clinicians for about a year after the NPI registry deactivates them.
- On that list, their Part B, home-health and hospice flags go to N. DME stays Y.
- The sister FISS list drops them almost at once. OIG-excluded people are also dropped within about 3 months.

**Hit means:** a dead or departed clinician's NPI still passes the ordering-provider check for DME for about 12 months. That's the classic door for equipment fraud.

**Miss means:** CMS may keep a designed grace window, so rentals and refills ordered while the clinician was enrolled still pay. If so, this is policy, not a hole. It's still worth asking what stops *new* orders.

**Boring**
- **The hinge, not checked:** CMS's rule for how long a departed orderer stays on the file, and whether the DME claim edit also reads NPPES. The warehouse can't answer that.
- NPPES deactivation isn't always death. Retirement, a duplicate NPI or license loss also deactivate. **The public file blanks the reason code on every row (checked).**
- DME = Y is on the whole list, including social workers and counselors, who can't order DME under Medicare rules as I understand them (not checked here). So DME = Y reads as "on the list," not as a real eligibility check.
- A stale file would fake this pattern. Tested: it's current (newest NPI 2026-06-06).

**Traps**
- DME is a constant (Y on all but 4 rows). Don't read it as eligibility.
- PARTB = N means "not in PECOS," not "can't order labs."
- The deactivation reason code in NPPES is blank on every row.

## 3. SNF enrollments (nursing-home operators): **live, borderline**

**Checked**
- 14,425 rows. One per enrollment and one per CCN. 14,421 distinct NPIs.
- **The glance's "one NPI on 107 enrollments" is false.** Each of those NPIs appears once, in the mart and in landing (S29). The biggest real repeat is 3 (Benefis).
- Ownership code: P (for-profit) 9,817, N 4,459, D 149. Chain (affiliation) is blank on 4,200. There are 635 chains.
- **Trap, INCORPORATION_DATE goes dark after Sept 2024 (S06).**
  - It's blank on 5,436 rows overall.
  - By enrollment year: 2024 enrollments 62.8% blank, 2025 94.1% (604 of 642), 2026 all.
  - The newest date is 2024-09-17.
  - 103 rows hold the 1800-01-01 sentinel.
- **Join: Care Compare nursing homes, CCN to CCN.** 14,229 of 14,425 land (98.6%). 974 of the 980 homes in the 2022+ group land.
  - 963 of those 980 homes were certified more than a year before their operator formed. These are operator changes, not new buildings.
- **Peer comparison (S17): for-profit homes only, each home against its own state's for-profit average.**
  - I restricted to for-profit because the 2022+ group is 97% for-profit, vs 66% for pre-2018 operators.

| Operator formed | Homes | Stars vs state (mean / median) | One-star share | Nurse hours/resident/day vs state (median) | Turnover vs state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 to Sep 2024 | 944 | -0.28 / -0.42 | 24.9% | -0.17 | +3.1 pts |
| 2018-2021 | 1,489 | -0.13 / -0.29 | 23.6% | -0.15 | +1.6 pts |
| before 2018 | 4,249 | +0.14 / +0.05 | 18.6% | -0.01 | -1.5 pts |

- **Is it 3-4 chains?** No. The chains in the 2022+ group split both ways.
  - Below state: Saber (13 homes, -1.37 stars, 77% one-star), Aviata (32, -1.06), Hill Valley (23, -0.75), Legacy (12, -0.70), PACS (88, -0.37).
  - Above state: Communicare (32, +0.88), Autumn Lake (20, +0.35), Ensign (44, +0.27).
  - Independents in the group are also below (114 homes, median -0.96).
- **Time test (S22, S26).** The dull explanation is that buyers pick homes that were already bad.
  - Treated: for-profit homes whose operator formed between their last two standard surveys.
  - Measure: standard citations at each survey, minus peers. Peers are for-profit homes with pre-2018 operators.

| Version | Homes | Gap before | Gap after | Change vs peers (mean, SE) | Median change | Homes worse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| State peers | 213 | +0.64 | +2.57 | +1.94 (0.59) | +1.10 | 121 |
| Calendar-matched peers (same survey years) | 206 | +2.70 | +3.87 | +1.17 (0.60) | +0.79 | 108 |
| State peers, surveys 2022 on only | 120 | +1.86 | +3.16 | +1.29 (0.69) | +0.88 | 68 |
| State peers, top 4 chains dropped | 146 | +0.05 | +2.23 | +2.18 (0.72) | +1.90 | 84 |

- Peer mean is about 7.6 citations per standard survey.
- All four versions point the same way. The two strictest are borderline (t about 1.9).
- The treated homes waited about 920 days between surveys, vs 589 for peers. That's the COVID survey backlog.
- Eyeball of the 6 biggest rises (picked for size, so illustrative only):
  - Bayside, Virginia Beach VA: 5 to 40
  - Bay Harbor, Salisbury MD (Marquis): 9 to 41
  - Old Southwest, Roanoke VA: 14 to 46
  - Guggenheimer, Lynchburg VA (Hill Valley): 3 to 28
  - MontCare at Wheaton MD: 7 to 30
  - Fair Oaks, Fairfax VA (Hill Valley): 10 to 30
  - Five of the six are Virginia or Maryland, the backlog states.
- An earlier window test on the deficiency table (S18) is weak. 471 of 944 homes had no citations in the "before" window, because the table keeps only each home's last 3 cycles. I don't use it for the claim.

**Hit means:** new operating companies are taking over for-profit homes that were already cited more than their peers, and the gap widens after the change. It's a "who buys troubled homes, and what happens next" story, with named chains on both sides.

**Miss means:** if the calendar-matched gap is noise, what's left is selection. New operators buy worse homes and don't fix them. That's still descriptive, not causal.

**Boring**
- The incorporation date is a proxy for a change of operator.
  - A new LLC can be the same owner reshuffling.
  - The LLC can form months before the sale closes.
  - The owner table (`LANDING.FED_CMS_SNF_OWNERSHIP`) could separate these. It's not joined here.
- The survey backlog and state survey strictness both move citation counts. The calendar-matched and 2022-on versions only partly control for this.
- The proxy can't see any change after Sept 2024 (trap above).

**Traps**
- INCORPORATION_DATE is blank on 94% of 2025 enrollments and all of 2026. The ownership-change proxy in `trap-nursing-home-chow-flag-constant` goes dark after 2024-09-17.
- The 1800-01-01 sentinel is on 103 rows.
- The glance's NPI top-counts (107) are false.

## 4. CDC leading causes by state: probed

**Checked**
- 10,868 rows = 19 years × 52 places (50 states, DC, US) × 11 causes. Complete, and no null deaths or rates.
- The US row equals the sum of the states exactly, for every cause, in 1999 and 2017.
- **Trap:** the glance's 168M deaths counts each death up to 4 times. The US row repeats the states, and the "All causes" row repeats the ten causes.
- **Peer and time: each state's 1999-to-2017 change in age-adjusted rate, ranked within its cause.**
  - **Flu and pneumonia:** Hawaii 19.6 to 29.6 (+51%), 229 to 637 deaths. It's the only state of 51 that rose. The US went 23.5 to 14.3, and the median state ratio is 0.59. Hawaii's 2017 rate is the highest in the country. The series climbs gradually from 18.0 in 2011, not in a one-year jump.
  - **Alzheimer's:** Mississippi 13.3 to 49.6 (3.7×), vs the US 1.9× and a median state 1.85×. It's the highest in 2017.
  - **Unintentional injuries:** Rhode Island 2.8×, Massachusetts 2.6×, West Virginia 2.4× (100.3, the highest). The US is 1.4×. This is the overdose wave, which is well known.
  - **Suicide:** up in 50 of 51 states. North Dakota 1.8×.
  - **Kidney disease** has the widest state spread (coefficient of variation 0.34). Vermont went 9.2 to 3.3 and Nevada 19.2 to 9.0, which is coding-sized movement.
- **Breaks:** the biggest one-year jumps are small-state flu years, plus California flu and pneumonia going 16.5 to 29.5 in 2000 (1.79×). The US was flat that year.

**Hit means:** the Hawaii pneumonia climb is a real outlier, worth a look with 2018-2023 data.

**Miss means:** Alzheimer's and pneumonia rates track how doctors fill in death certificates. The outliers may be paperwork.

**Boring:** the data stops in 2017. NCHS publishes all of this, and the big movers (overdose, suicide) are known.

**Traps**
- The DEATHS sum repeats each death up to 4 times (US row plus All causes).
- California's 1999 flu and pneumonia value (16.5) jumps to 29.5 in 2000. Don't baseline California on 1999.

## 5. FQHC enrollments: probed

**Checked**

- **The glance's "one NPI on 82 enrollments" is false.** Each of those NPIs appears once (S29). Multi-site NPIs are normal: Sun River Health has 44 sites and Mountain Area Health Education Center 42.
- **Trap: the 104 "for-profit" (P) enrollments** come from 75 organizations. Examples:
  - County of Ventura (county owned)
  - Wood County Auditor
  - University of Wyoming
  - Sault, Siletz, Goshute and Narragansett tribes
  - Choptank, Goshen, Cherokee Health Systems
  - The P flag isn't for-profit.
- INCORPORATION_DATE is blank on 96% of 2025-26 enrollments. The same trap as SNF.
- **Join: HRSA health-center sites.** Matched by site NPI or CCN first, then by ZIP5 plus street number.
  - By ID: 7,864 of 11,063 (71%).
  - By ID or address: about 94%.
  - 2024+ enrollments match by ID only 33% of the time, but by address 95%. HRSA's billing-number fields lag.
- Organizations with no match at all: 76 of 1,559. The top of that list is tribal or urban-Indian: M A C T Health Board, United Indian Health Services, Salish and Kootenai, Sault Tribe. Those programs don't file UDS with HRSA.
  - The non-tribal names (CareSTL Health, Quality Community Health Care) are known health centers. They're likely under other addresses (not checked).
- Enrollments by year, from the date in the enrollment ID: steady at 400-800 a year. The peak is 788 in 2010, and 2023-25 run 716-772.

**Hit means:** nothing story-grade. A Medicare FQHC with no HRSA footprint would be a lead, and none survived the address and tribal checks.

**Miss means:** the table is fine for site counts and joins by CCN.

**Boring:** tribal and urban-Indian FQHCs sit outside HRSA's grantee list by law.

**Traps**
- The P flag marks counties, tribes and nonprofits.
- INCORPORATION_DATE goes dark after Aug 2024.
- The glance's NPI top-counts (82) are false.
- HRSA's Medicare billing numbers lag new sites.
