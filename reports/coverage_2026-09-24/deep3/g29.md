## 1. FEC_COMMITTEES_DIM: "sympathy" super PACs send almost nothing to politics. **live**

The dim is one row per committee: 44,398 committees, name, treasurer, type, connected org.
It is the name-and-treasurer layer. The money comes from joining `POLITICS__FED_FEC_PAC_SUMMARY` on committee ID (99.8% land).

**The screen (fixed before looking at outcomes)**
- Committee-cycles 2018-2024 with $250K+ raised and $250K+ spent, and 80%+ of receipts from individuals.
- No connected org. Leadership PACs (designation D) and the conduits ActBlue and WinRed dropped.
- Super PACs and hybrids (types O, V, W): 740 committees, 1,025 committee-cycles.
- "Sympathy" group = the name holds a word like POLICE, TROOPER, SHERIFF, FIREFIGHTER, FIRST RESPONDER, VETERAN, WOUNDED, DISABLED, CANCER, BREAST, LEUKEMIA, AUTISM, CHILDREN, HEROES, BACK THE BLUE. That's 41 committees.
- "To politics" = contributions to candidates or committees, plus independent ads (IEs), plus transfers to affiliates.
  Each line takes the **highest** of three sources: the PAC summary, itemized Schedule E (S14), itemized committee-to-candidate money (S15). That's the most generous reading for the PACs.

**Checked**

| Group | Committees | Raised | To politics | Committee median |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sympathy-named super PACs | 41 | $181.5M | $22.5M (12.5%) | **13.4%** |
| Every other super PAC/hybrid on the same screen | 699 | | | **61.6%** |
| Type O super PACs only: sympathy vs other | 40 vs 629 | | | **13.8% vs 66.5%** |

- **Same size, per cycle** (median share to politics):
  - $0.25-1M raised: 16.7% vs 37.5%
  - $1-5M: **2.5% vs 56.2%**
  - $5M+: 7.2% vs 77.7%
- **Is it 3-4 committees?** No. On the summary line, the pooled share is 7.8% for all 41 and 7.7% without the top 4 raisers.
  - 20 of 41 sent under 10%, even at the most generous count.
  - Two sent over 50%: Principled Veterans Fund (98.9%) and The American Firefighters Initiative PAC (58%, one cycle). So the name alone doesn't convict.
- **Time. Is it start-up cost?** 27 of them ran 2+ cycles. Median share was 3.1% in their first cycle and 4.8% in their latest. Only 3 got above 30%.
- **Who runs them: the treasurer field from the dim.** 10 treasurers each sign for 2+ of these PACs: 31 PACs, $106.5M raised. 7.6% went to politics on the summary line, 13.5% at most.

- **Still active:** 17 of the 41 are in the 2026 committee file. 7 show itemized 2025-26 gifts. Law Enforcement for a Safer America has 470 itemized donors, 173 of them retired.
- **Donors (S16, itemized gifts only, deduped):**
  - At the median sympathy PAC, only **2.3%** of individual money is itemized. The other ~98% came in gifts too small to itemize. Other super PACs: ~100% itemized.
  - Retired share of itemized donors: 41% pooled (median 43%) vs 24% pooled (median 8%) at other super PACs. Small-dollar peers (median donor total $500 or less): 24% pooled, 17% median.
  - Each gift is keyed on donor, ZIP, date and amount. The bulk file repeats rows.

**Hit means:** a cluster of PACs using cop, soldier and sick-kid names raise mostly tiny donations and spend 85%+ on something other than politics. That's the scam-PAC pattern. The treasurer network says a handful of operators run most of them.

**Miss means:** the money went to legal non-IE political work: issue ads, voter contact, list-building that later pays off. The time test says the payoff never comes for most of them.

**Boring:**
- Scam PACs are a known genre. Some of these names have probably been written about or drawn FEC complaints. **Not checked.** What's new here is the peer screen with numbers, the treasurer map, and the 17 still registered for 2026.
- Where the money went is **not visible**. FEC operating expenditures (Schedule B: vendors, telemarketers) aren't in the warehouse. That's the next join, and the one that makes or breaks it.
- Schedule E shows more IE than the PAC summary for these PACs in 2022 ($21.0M vs $13.4M). 24/48-hour reports probably double-count against the regular report. I used the higher figure anyway, which helps the PACs.
- The name list is my choice. A different list moves the count, not the gap: the 1-5M band gap is 2.5% vs 56%.
- 24% of other super PACs are also under 10%. Low spending on politics isn't unique to these; it's 2x as common among them (48% vs 24%).

**Traps found**
- `CYCLE` isn't "one value": it's 2026 on the 20,007 `bulk_cm26` rows and NULL on the 24,391 `cm_multicycle` rows. It's a source label, not a cycle.
- `IS_AMBIGUOUS` is true on 3,431 rows (7.7%) overall, but on **16 of the 41** sympathy PACs (39%). These committees change names and DBAs. Treat their labels with care.
- `TRES_NM` is blank or NULL on 2,023 rows. The same person is filed two ways ("BASS, ZACHARY" / "ZACHARY BASS"; "PIARO, ROBERT" / "ROBERT PIARO").
- PAC_SUMMARY has no cycle column. I inferred it from `COVERAGE_END_DATE`. 7,897 rows have no coverage date, and 25 committee-cycles have 2 rows.
- The top-line $4.2B "individual contributions" in PAC_SUMMARY is ActBlue/WinRed pass-through. Drop the conduits.

**Next join:** FEC Schedule B operating expenditures (not loaded) for the 41, to name the vendors. Then state charity-regulator actions against the same operators.

## 2. SEC_EDGAR_FINANCIALS: filing index, half duplicated. **probed**

55,635 filings, 8,112 companies, SEC financial-statement data sets 2023q1-2024q4. One row per filing.

**Checked**
- **Duplicate-table check (S12):** all 26,893 rows from 2024q1-q4 are also in `FINANCE__FED_SEC_DERA_SUB_2024Q1..Q4`, same accession and same quarter. Only 2023 is unique to this table.
- `ADSH` is unique: 55,635 distinct. The battery's "ADSH top 201" isn't a repeat count.
- **Peer, late 10-Ks.** Deadline by filer size, plus the 15-day extension, plus 3 days of slack. Non-accelerated filers only:

| Business address | 10-Ks | Past extended deadline |
|---|---|---|
| China, Hong Kong, Taiwan | 158 | 19.6% |
| Other foreign | 484 | 14.3% |
| US | 5,568 | 8.5% |

- **One join: insider sales while a report is overdue (S10, S17).**
  - Insider open-market sales (Form 4, code S) by issuer and day, CIK compared as numbers. 3,942 of 8,112 issuers show sales in the window.
  - In the 30 days before filing, insiders sold at 3.0% of late non-accelerated 10-Ks vs 14.7% of on-time ones. 10-Qs: 4.3% vs 12.6%. Late filers' insiders mostly sit still.
  - Sales inside an overdue window: 27 filings, 19 companies. The $55.7M total is mostly artifacts:
    - Tanger's 10-K carries period 2022-12-31 but a 2024 filing date (period error). That's $14.4M.
    - One Healing Co line at $200 a share next to $1 and $2 lines. Probably $2. That's $10M.
    - Lottery.com's sales are reported twice, by joint filers Woodford Eurasia and ALD Holdings.
  - What's left is 10% holders selling: Horizon Fuel Cell sold 3.77M Hyzon Motors shares at $1.71 on 2022-12-27, while two Hyzon 10-Qs were overdue. DGP Co. sold 3.4M Exicure shares at $1.32 on 2024-02-29.
- **Harm join: PBGC trusteed pensions (S13).** EIN padded to 9 and 000000000 dropped: 8 matches.
  - Recent: Rite Aid (6,106 participants, plan ended 2025-07-31), Yellow Corp (4,383, 2024-05-01), Tupperware (584, 2024-09-30).
  - All are bankruptcies. Already public.

**Hit means:** nothing story-grade. The Hyzon sale is one 10% holder, one day.
**Miss means:** the table is fine as a filing index, but use DERA_SUB for 2024 and this one only for 2023.

**Boring:** late filing by China-based small caps is well known. Blackout policies explain why late filers' insiders don't trade.

**Traps found**
- 2024 half duplicates DERA_SUB_2024Q1-Q4 (26,893 rows).
- `EIN` = `000000000` on 12,468 rows (22%). Every EIN is 9 characters, so a length check won't catch the filler.
- `PERIOD` can be wrong: Tanger's 10-K reads a year early.
- Insider table (for the ledger): code-S sale value for 2024 sums to **$1.69 quadrillion** because of 11 trades priced over $10,000 a share. Under the $10K cap it's $249B. Cap prices before summing.
- Joint Form 4 filers repeat the same trade. Dedupe on issuer + date + shares + price.
- Closed-end funds barely land here: 4 of 973 CIKs. They don't file XBRL 10-Ks, so a miss means nothing.

## 3. OCC_NATIONAL_BANKS: crypto custodians at the end of the charter list. **probed**

723 national banks plus 1 spreadsheet row (`=COUNT(A5:A727)`). One run ID. Loaded around 2026-08-05.
There's no date column. OCC charter numbers are handed out in order, so the number stands in for time. Conversions of old banks get new numbers too.

**Checked**
- **Join, FDIC branch deposits (S08) by CERT:** 664 of 723 land. Eyeballed, the 59 misses are trust companies or new charters with no deposits yet.
- **By charter-number band:**

| Charter no. | Banks | No FDIC deposits | "Digital" or crypto-custody name |
|---|---|---|---|
| under 20000 | 481 | 5 | 0 |
| 20000-23999 | 86 | 16 | 0 |
| 24000-24999 | 79 | 19 | 0 |
| 25000-25199 | 27 | 2 | 0 |
| 25200-25299 | 28 | 10 | 1 (Anchorage Digital, 25243) |
| 25300-25398 | 22 | 8 | 5 (IRACE Digital 25304, Erebor 25357, BitGo 25366, Paxos 25379, Fidelity Digital Assets 25398) |

- Trust-only charters aren't new. 19 of the 79 in the 24000s (Vanguard, Goldman Sachs Trust and others) hold no deposits.
- What's new is **who** gets them: payroll firms (ADP 25173, Dayforce 25237, Paycom 25289), wealth firms (Hightower, Plante Moran, Inspire, TIAA Trust, Alvarez & Marsal), and crypto custodians.
- BitGo, Paxos and Fidelity Digital Assets have **CERT = 0**, meaning no FDIC insurance. Paxos and Fidelity have no RSSD either.
- Concentration: Sioux Falls, SD hosts Anchorage, BitGo, Wells Fargo's main office, Pathward and The Bancorp Bank.

**Hit means:** the OCC's newest charters skew toward non-banks that don't take insured deposits.
**Miss means:** the digital-asset charters were announced publicly at the time, so it's a known story. From memory, check it: Circle and Ripple also got approvals in late 2025. Neither is on this list.

**Boring:** tested. Trust-only charters are decades old, so the new part is only the crypto names. IRACE Digital's business was **not checked**. Erebor being crypto-focused is from memory.

**Traps found**
- Row `=COUNT(A5:A727)` with NAME None.
- `CERT` = 0 is a sentinel for an uninsured bank, not a certificate.
- `RSSD` is an ID. The battery summed it ("sum 795.9M") as if it were money.
- `_LOADED_AT` is epoch micros.

## 4. SEC_CLOSED_END_FUND_INFORMATION: new retail private-markets funds. **probed**

973 funds, one row each, with the latest filing. All file numbers start 811-, so no BDCs (those sit in the BDC report table).

**Checked**
- **Date trap first.** `FILING_DATE` is stored as year 0001-0099. Year 0026 means 2026, 0094 means 1994.
  - The Python driver can't read year 0001 at all. S01 died. Cast to text.
  - Parsed, the snapshot ends 2026-05-29. 698 funds last filed in 2026 (535 of them in May), 41 in 2025.
- **Dormant listings:** 212 of 973 (22%) last filed in 2023 or earlier, back to 1994.
  - 50 last filed an N-2 and 33 an N-2/A: registrations that never went further.
  - 17 last filed N-8F (deregistration) and 12 RW (withdrawal), yet they're still on the list. That's 32 counting N-8F variants.
- **Time, using CIK as a birth date:** SEC numbers are assigned in order. The table calibrates itself: funds whose latest filing is a first N-2 or N-8A have a median CIK of 1.92M for 2022, 1.97M for 2023, 2.02M for 2024 and 2.11M for 2026.

| CIK band (active funds, filed 2025-26) | Funds | Private / credit / alternatives name |
|---|---|---|
| up to 1.5M (pre-2011 or so) | 292 | 37.7% |
| 1.5-1.8M | 143 | 54.5% |
| 1.8-1.9M | 83 | 61.4% |
| 1.9-2.0M (about 2022-23) | 51 | 72-74% |
| 2.0-2.1M (about 2024-25) | 137 | 66-73% |
| over 2.1M (2026) | 33 | 78.8% |

- 221 of 739 active funds (30%) are about 2022 or newer, and 170 (23%) are about 2024 or newer.
- Among the newest: Robinhood Ventures Fund I, RiverNorth Long/Short Prime Unicorn Fund I, Ultra AI Opportunities, Pre-IPO & Growth Fund, Parvin Decentralized Fund.

**Hit means:** the rush of private-markets and pre-IPO funds sold to retail is in the SEC's own list.
**Miss means:** that's a known trend, so this is confirmation, not news.

**Boring:** the name keyword is crude. A skeptic would want assets, and N-PORT isn't here.

**Traps found**
- Year-00YY dates. Driver crash.
- 212 dormant listings, including 32 whose last filing asked to deregister or withdraw.
- `CIK` is 10-digit zero-padded text; `FILING_TYPE` is the latest filing only, not the fund's type.
