**What it is:** 21,930 rows of EPA warning letters and notices, mostly 2000-2026. Every row: `AGENCY` = EPA, `ACTIVITY_TYPE_CODE` = AIF. One action can span many rows: **count `ENF_IDENTIFIER`, not rows.**

### What the glance missed [q11, q28]
The glance's "top facilities" (114, 65, 63 rows) aren't factories. They're **oil-field injection wells**: names like `OS3259 - WELL NO. CI-3 (E)`, towns Hominy, Avant, Skiatook, Pawhuska.
- All 30 top facilities are Region 6 Safe Drinking Water Act notices logged in ICIS.
- That set is **2,284 facilities. 1,718 (75%) sit in Osage County, Okla.** per EPA's facility registry (FRS). Most of the rest: other Oklahoma counties.
- On the Osage mineral estate, EPA runs the injection-well program itself (outside knowledge: 40 CFR part 147). So these are EPA's own notices, not a state's.

## 2. FINANCE__FED_SEC_INSIDER_SUBMISSION: **live**

**What it is:** 1,772,088 filings, one per accession number, July 2016 - March 2025. Every quarter present, no gaps [q06]. Form 4: 1.58M. Form 3: 130K. Form 5: 21K.
**The check the glance can't do:** join each Form 4 to its trades (`INSIDER_NONDERIV_TRANS`, by accession). 1,317,272 of 1,584,625 Form 4s have a non-derivative trade row [q07].

### The rule and the test
- An insider must report a sale within 2 business days (outside knowledge: Section 16(a)).
- Test: Form 4s with an open-market sale (code S), filed **11-365 days** after the first sale. 11 days clears weekends, holidays and the 10b5-1 "deemed execution" grace.
- Sales only: small buys (≤ $10K) may legally wait for Form 5, so buys are left out. 1,586 of 4,856 late buys are that small [q16].

### Concentration with a denominator, peer = other companies [q16, q27]
- 338,899 sale filings. **3,754 late** (1.1%).
- Dedupe: **55** of them mirror an on-time filing by a co-owner (same company, same first sale date, same shares). Dropped. **3,699 remain.**
- 2,740 companies with 30+ sale filings: **median 0%** late. 90th percentile 2.9%. 1,651 at zero. **38 at 10% or more.**

### The dull explanation, and whether it was tested
- **Co-owners file the same trade twice.** Tested and removed: 55 mirrors. **Celsius Holdings** looked like $318M late. It isn't: Dean and Deborah DeSantis filed 9-17 days late, but director William H. Milmoe had filed the same share counts on time (0-4 days) [q25]. 3 real late filings remain there.

- **Typo'd trade dates.** Eyeballed ~90 rows: lags of 11-305 days, prices and dates consistent inside each filing.
- **Late ≠ hidden.** A late Form 4 is a disclosure failure, not proof of insider trading. Companies must list late filers in their proxy (outside knowledge: Item 405). Not checked.

**Hit means:** a named, checkable list of companies whose insiders habitually report sales weeks late, against a 0% median.

## Traps found

**EPA informal actions**
- One action spans many rows: the top well has 114 rows but 20 actions. Count `ENF_IDENTIFIER`.
- About 1,900 rows (9%) have no `ACHIEVED_DATE`: 779 of 1,092 SFDW rows, 523 of 3,832 TSCA rows.
- One FIFRA row dated 1970-06-01.
- `AGENCY` = EPA and `ACTIVITY_TYPE_CODE` = AIF on every row: constants.
- `ENF_IDENTIFIER` starts with the EPA region (`06-...`); the only region field in the table.
- ECHO holds 636 of the 2,284 Region 6 wells and shows $0 penalties on them.

**SEC insider submission**
- Co-owners file the same trade: 55 late filings mirror an on-time one. Celsius's "$318M late" is mostly this.
- Summing `TRANSACTION_VALUE` or shares × price gives nonsense (a 2017 total of $23 quadrillion). Cap each row, or use medians.
- Form 3s have no rows in the transaction table (130,095 filings); the holdings tables aren't loaded.
- One transaction row is shifted: `TRANSACTION_CODE` = 'false', `FORM_TYPE` = 'F'.
- `ISSUER_CIK` is zero-padded text; the ticker file's CIK is a number. Join with `try_to_number`.

**FHLB membership**
- 88 `MEM_DATE`s fall in 2033-2066: a century flip. They're 1933-1966. Mostly old savings associations (50).
- 337 `MEM_DATE`s are 1989-12-31: a stand-in.
- The glance's "top CERT holds 37%" is 2,343 blanks: credit unions, insurers and CDFIs have no FDIC cert.
- 43 members' FDIC records went inactive Feb-Jul 2026 (Comerica, Cadence, FirstBank and others merged). The roster lags FDIC by months.
- CERT join to the FDIC failed-bank list: 7 hits, all false. Citibank 2008 and Bank of America 2009 are bailout assistance entries, and the other fail dates come before membership.
- `STATE` for insurers is the home state of the company: a Cincinnati-district member can be MT.

**FEC candidates**
- 2026 cycle nearly absent (177 rows).
- Election years 2089, 2106, 2820, 2916, 2923, 2924, 2929: typos.
- Blank mailing state makes "lives out of state" checks fire: at least 115 of 2020's 255 House mismatches are a blank `CAND_ST`.
- Compliance-firm PO boxes: one Alexandria, Va. box holds 22 candidates in 11 states. A mailing address isn't a home.
