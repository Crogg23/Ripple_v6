- HPV means "high priority violation." The district flags nearly every violation as one (Chevron: 815 of 1,014). What matters is how many were never closed. Tesla: 318 of 328 still open (97%). The refineries run 49-81% open. Of the 20, only Corteva Pittsburg (30 of 30) has a higher share.

**Hostile checks that ran:**
- **Second ID (a known trap).** The same registry ID also sits on EPA Region 9 air ID `09000CA00100001`. That ID holds an EPA penalty action: **$275,000 on 2022-02-17** (case 09-2022-3503). So the plant-level count since 2019 is **1 formal action, and it was EPA's, not the district's.**
- **Old owner's history.** The plant ID is older than Tesla. The 1995-2004 NOVs and Administrative Orders on it belong to the previous plant owner, not Tesla. I left them out of every Tesla number.
- **Name collision.** "TESLA POWER PROJECT" (`CABAA00006001B3424`) is a power plant, not Tesla Inc. Excluded.
- **Is Tesla the only zero?** No. Since 2019, **Phillips 66 Rodeo** also has 0 formal actions against 213 NOVs. Two plants, 569 NOVs, no district formal action in the federal file.

**The dull explanation:** the district may have resolved these outside ICIS-Air. Press reports in 2024 said it sought an abatement order against Tesla. That is outside knowledge, not in the warehouse, and not verified here. It may also be sitting on a settlement that hasn't been entered yet.
**Partly tested:** the district's records run about 2 months behind. It logged 0 NOVs for June-July 2026 anywhere, against 87 in June-July 2025. But a lag of 2 months can't explain 7 years without a formal action.
**What a hit means:** a factory with district-high violation counts and no local penalty on the federal record. The next step is to pull the district's own NOV and Hearing Board files.
**What a miss would mean:** if the district settled and never reported it to EPA, the story turns into a reporting gap. That still counts, but it's a different story.

### State peer comparison: it measures procedure, not toughness [q04, q08]

- **Dull explanation, and it's likely right:** in some states a notice of violation is the first step of a formal case (Indiana: 9.5% of facilities with any informal action have zero formal). In others it's a stand-alone letter. Don't rank states on this.
- Nationally, **40** facilities have 10+ informal actions since 2010 and no formal action on their own air ID. After checking sibling IDs and EPA civil cases on the same registry ID, **32** still have none. Top of that list: EQ Detroit (US Ecology), MI, 34 NOVs from 2014 to 2025. Each one is the state's own procedure until someone checks it.

### Traps in this table
- `ACHIEVED_DATE` placeholders: 59 at 0001-01-01, 4 at 8888-01-01, 1 at 1900-01-01, 1 at 1950-06-30. Also 174 blank and 2 other future dates (2027, 2104).
- One action can cover many facilities: 175,736 rows, 173,157 actions. Count distinct `ACTIVITY_ID`.
- `ACTIVITY_TYPE_CODE` / `ACTIVITY_TYPE_DESC` hold one value on every row (AIF / "Administrative - Informal").
- **The glance's "top ENF_IDENTIFIER 1.3K rows" is wrong.** `ENF_IDENTIFIER` is unique per action. The largest real group is 133 rows (one EPA letter).
- About 2 months of lag at the end of the series (see Bay Area above).
- California's air numbers are basically the Bay Area's: South Coast is blank and San Joaquin stopped.

## 3. ENVIRONMENT__XC_OWID_CO2: probed

**What it is:** 29,384 country-year rows, 247 entities, 1750-2024. No duplicate entity-years. Year and value are stored as text but every row parses. [q13]

**Checks:**
- **Double counting:** 5,976 rows are regions or groups, not countries. 3,311 have no code (GCP regions, "Europe (excl. EU-27)", ship and plane fuel) and 2,665 carry `OWID_` codes. Kosovo is the one real country under an `OWID_` code.
- **Do the totals add up?** Yes. World 2024 is 38,598.6 Mt. 215 countries (37,398.1) + international shipping (623.4) + aviation (569.0) = 38,590.5. [q16]
- **Rounding steps:** old and small-country values move in steps of 3,664 tonnes (1,000 tonnes of carbon). 3,664 shows up on 273 rows, 7,328 on 110, 10,992 on 153. Year-over-year % change for tiny emitters is noise.
- 1,106 zero rows across 35 entities.
- **Time:** 2024 vs 2019: China +14.7%, India +22.3%, Turkey +26.2%, US -6.3%, Germany -19.2%. Accurate, and already published everywhere. Useful as context, not as a lead.
