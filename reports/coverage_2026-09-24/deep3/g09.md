**Miss means:** if the copies were blocked by valid patents a court upheld, the story is patent policy, not anyone breaking a rule. It's still a clean dollar figure for that policy story.

**Boring:**
- The patent explanation *is* the mechanism. This is a known policy story; the new part is the Medicare dollar pinned to the approval-to-launch gap.
- Part D "total drug cost" is **gross, before rebates**. Don't turn this into a "Medicare could have saved $X" number.
- The prescriber-drug file drops rows under 11 claims. Every figure is a floor.
- The high-share peers are mostly clinic-infused drugs with small Part D spend. The fair peer is insulin glargine, a self-injected pharmacy drug. It sat at 0.3% even with an interchangeable copy. That points the same way.
- Only one year (2022). No DY2023/2024 drug-level Part D file is in the warehouse, so there's no time series.

## Traps for the ledger

- ⚠ **The battery's "top value" counts are fake on unique columns.** `battery.py` uses `APPROX_TOP_K`. On near-unique keys it inflates every counter:
  - UNII "845": really 1.
  - DailyMed SETID "1,166": really 1.
  - Device PRODUCT_CODE "53": really 1.
  - CAS "644": really 1. The true max is 56.
  - Any chunk fact of the form "X top: value N" on an ID column needs an exact recount.
- ⚠ **IRF unit CERTIFICATION_DATE is not an opening date.** It resets. POS `REHAB_UNIT_EFCTV_DT` carries the same reset. WakeMed's 98-bed unit reads 2019-10-01.
  - Freestanding dates are the CCN's original participation date (410 of 410). That doesn't catch an old program moving to a new CCN (S17).
- **POS `REHAB_UNIT_SW` stays 'Y' after the unit closes.** 676 of 678 units with a termination date still read 'Y'. Use `REHAB_UNIT_TRMNTN_DT`.
- **The IRF 'TA' unit CCNs sit in long-term-care hospitals** (like 10TA09 Kindred Tampa). T→0 doesn't give their parent. 50 units miss the POS join; in Florida all 7 misses are TA units.
- The IRF list has one future-dated row: 10T286 Physicians Regional Pine Ridge, 2026-10-01.
- `HEALTH__FED_CMS_PARTD_PRESCRIBERS` has a DATA_YEAR column, but it's **2022 only**.
- Purple Book `APPROVAL_DATE` on supplement rows is the supplement's date. Amjevita rows 004-009 read 2023, but it was approved 2016. Take MIN per BLA.
- DailyMed `SPL_VERSION` is labeler-typed. Max 1,699,296,106. Some are dates, like 20251008. It's not a revision count.
- UNII `EC_NUMBER`: 0 of 168,046 filled.
