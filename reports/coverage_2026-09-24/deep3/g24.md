## 1. NCUA credit unions: a $77M hole in Jackson, Mississippi. **live**

**Checked**
- Profile (S03): 4,250 credit unions, one quarter (2026 Q1).
  - All 4,250 charters land in the NCUA call-report roster (S10). That's where opening year and minority-owned (MDI) status come from.
- **Peer: deposits against assets**, all 4,250:
  - Median: deposits are 0.862x assets.
  - Only 6 credit unions report more deposits than assets.
  - **Jackson Area FCU: 2.08x.** Next is People Trust Community (Little Rock, $1.5M in assets) at 1.69x.
- **Time: the 4-quarter change** in assets (a column in the table):
  - Median: +2.8%. The bottom 0.1% is -33.6%.
  - **Jackson Area: -50.2%, the steepest drop of all 4,250.** Its deposits rose 14.5% over the same year.

| Jackson Area FCU (charter 8445) | Value |
|---|---|
| Opened | 1953. Minority-owned (MDI), low-income designated |
| Members | 15,704 |
| Total assets | $71,355,580 |
| Total deposits | $148,178,414 |
| Net worth ratio | **-107.9%**, so about -$77M. The median credit union is at +12.4% |
| Return on assets | -314% a year |

- **Second load agrees (S17).** The raw call report (FS220) is loaded separately:
  - `ACCT_010` = 71,355,580 (assets)
  - `ACCT_018` = 148,178,414 (deposits)
  - `ACCT_602` = **-91,737,477**. That fits the -314% return (about -$92M for one quarter).
  - `ACCT_940` = +14,713,240. Add the two: 14.7M - 91.7M ≈ -77.0M, the net worth hole.
  - `ACCT_880` = 41,800,000. That's exactly the gap between the two deposit lines, 018 and 013 ($106.4M).
  - What 602, 880 and the two big expense lines (671 = $93.2M, 657 = $63.1M) mean needs NCUA's account dictionary. I didn't look it up.
- The same raw file for Navy Federal (control) matches the list on assets, deposits, loans and members.

**Hit means:** in one quarter, a 73-year-old minority-owned credit union booked a loss bigger than all its assets. The roster flags it as minority-owned but doesn't say which group. A discovered fraud or write-off looks like that. So does a filing error.
**Miss means:** n/a. Every check agreed.
**The dull explanation:** a keying error in the call report. The -314% return and the 50% asset drop come from different fields. They point the same way, so a single typo is less likely. Not ruled out.
**Next:** NCUA conservatorship or liquidation notices. Also the 2025 call reports: the warehouse holds only March 2026, so the prior-quarter balance sheet isn't here.

**Also on this table (probed, not leads)**
- 67 credit unions sit under the 7% well-capitalized line, 31 under 6%, 11 under 4%.
  - Four are negative: Jackson Area -107.9%. People Trust Community (Little Rock, opened 2022) -91.1%. Copper & Glass (Glassport, PA) -62.3%. WeDevelopment (Kansas City, opened 2022) -7.3%.
- **New charters:** 18 credit unions opened in 2020 or later.
  - 72% are minority-owned.
  - 27.8% are under 7% net worth. For credit unions opened before 1960, it's 1.2%.
  - 66.7% lose money, against 14.4%.
  - The dull explanation, startup losses, can't be tested with one quarter.
- **"Low-income" credit unions:** 54.5% of all credit-union assets sit in credit unions with the low-income designation.
  - 13 of the 24 over $10B carry it: America First, Mountain America, Golden 1, Suncoast, ENT, VyStar and others.
  - A known industry fight. Not new.
- Merger events table: 27 rows. Two mergers were for "poor financial condition": Gateway Metro (St. Louis) and North Bay (Santa Rosa).

**Traps**
- Shell charters with $1 and $22 in assets read net worth 100%, return on assets 400%, and asset growth up to 7,168,900%. Filter by size before ranking any ratio.
- The FS220 raw file is `ACCT_xxx` codes. Most need NCUA's lookup.

## 2. FDIC banks: minority-owned banks get consent orders at 3-4x the rate of same-size peers. **live**

**Checked, the trap first**
- The catalog says this is "a partial pull... do not count from it." **It's the full FDIC directory** (S04, S05):
  - 27,836 institutions. 4,254 are active, and 4,245 of those report as of 2026-03-31.
  - Active assets: $26.2T. `ASSET` is in thousands, so the glance's "max 4.0B" is JPMorgan's $4.0T.
  - **3,037 of 3,037** failed banks that have a cert land here.
  - 4,224 of 4,241 (99.6%) enforcement-order certs land here.
- **Join (S18, S19): FDIC enforcement orders to the directory, by CERT.**
  - A "new order" means: cease-and-desist or consent type, not a termination or modification, and the respondent is the bank.
  - That's 986 such orders since 2010.
  - ⚠ The naive filter is wrong: 958 of 5,002 orders since 2010 are titled like "Order Terminating Consent Order."
- **Peer set:** only banks the FDIC supervises (state nonmember and state savings banks). Banks supervised by the OCC or the Fed can't get these orders.
  - The denominator is every such bank open at the start of the window.
- **Two windows, split by size:**

| Size | 2010-19 MDI | 2010-19 others | 2020-26 MDI | 2020-26 others |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| under $100M | 22 of 35 (62.9%) | 137 of 1,158 (11.8%) | 2 of 12 (16.7%) | 24 of 516 (4.7%) |
| $100-300M | 21 of 54 (38.9%) | 221 of 1,662 (13.3%) | 5 of 32 (15.6%) | 16 of 1,032 (1.6%) |
| $300M-1B | 14 of 42 (33.3%) | 161 of 1,357 (11.9%) | 2 of 37 (5.4%) | 12 of 991 (1.2%) |
| $1-10B | 11 of 36 (30.6%) | 87 of 656 (13.3%) | 1 of 29 (3.4%) | 16 of 512 (3.1%) |
| over $10B | 1 of 5 | 6 of 68 | 0 of 4 | 2 of 57 |
| **All** | **69 of 172 (40.1%)** | **612 of 4,901 (12.5%)** | **10 of 114 (8.8%)** | **70 of 3,108 (2.3%)** |

- One-sided Fisher test: p = 6e-19 (2010-19), p = 0.0004 (2020-26).
- **Size-matched** for 2020-26: expected 2.5 minority-owned banks with an order, observed 10.
  - The recent gap sits in banks under $1B. At $1-10B it's even (3.4% vs 3.1%).
- **Split by profit** (active banks only, Q1 2026 return on assets in thirds):
  - Low: 13.0% vs 4.0%
  - Mid: 4.2% vs 1.3%
  - High: 7.4% vs 1.1%
- **Who, 2020-26.** 12 new consent orders at 10 banks. The order links are in `g24/out_S23.csv`:
  - Asian-American owned or boarded: California Business Bank (2020, 2024), International Bank of Chicago (2020, 2024), CBW Bank (2020; also a $20.4M penalty in 2024), Spectra Bank, Monterey County Bank, Loyal Trust Bank, Royal Business Bank
  - Black-owned: Citizens Savings Bank and Trust (Nashville, 2022)
  - Multi-racial board: Piermont Bank (2024), Paramount Bank (2026)
- By group, 2020-26:

| Group | Hits |
|---|---|
| Asian-American | 7 of 52 (13.5%) |
| Black | 1 of 20 |
| Hispanic | 0 of 24 |
| Native American | 0 of 12 |
| Multi-racial | 2 of 6 |

- **Median unit:** 104 of 114 minority-owned banks had no new order. The recent effect is 10 banks.

**Hit means:** since 2010 the FDIC has put minority-owned banks under consent orders at 3-4x the rate of same-size peers it also supervises. The FDIC's own mission includes preserving these banks. Recently the gap is mostly Asian-American banks.
**Miss means:** n/a. The gap held in both windows and in every size band under $1B.
**The dull explanations:**
- Weaker balance sheets. Tested: the gap holds within size bands and within profit thirds.
- Money-laundering scrutiny of cash- and remittance-heavy customers. Not tested. It needs the order texts, and the links are in S23.
- MDI status is today's value. Banks that gained it after the order, or lost it, are misfiled.

**Dead end tried:** deposits per branch. The top tenth under $10B got orders at 3.2%, the rest at 2.5%. Nothing there.

**Traps:** the "sample" note; `ASSET` in thousands; `ESTYMD` goes back to 1782 (13 rows before 1800); termination orders look like consent orders by title.

- **What the filing says (S24):** RA's 2024 filings carry a median of **5 reporting owners each**: the adviser, the funds, and the two managing partners.

## 5. IRS ZIP tax stats: one old year, with state totals mixed in. **probed**

**Checked (S02, S08)**
- ⚠ **Only tax year 2016.** The IRS has published newer years. No time comparison is possible.
- ⚠ **306 rows with ZIP "0" are state totals** (51 states x 6 income brackets).
  - They hold 147.0M returns and $7.10T in wages. The real ZIP rows add to the same again.
  - So the glance's "WAGES sum 14.2B" (the column is in thousands) is double: the true figure is $7.1T.
  - The glance's "max 325.2M" is a California bracket total, not a ZIP.
- 13,942 rows have 4-digit ZIPs, a lost leading zero, in CT, MA, ME, NH, NJ, NY, RI, VT.
- 306 rows are ZIP 99999, the IRS "other ZIPs" bucket.
- **Peer (S09): refund anticipation checks, returns under $50K, ZIPs with 1,000+ such returns:**
  - Nation 17.9%, median ZIP 15.5%
  - Mississippi 30.5%, Louisiana 28.1%, Alabama 26.3%, Georgia 25.9%. Minnesota 11.9%.
  - Top ZIPs:
    - 85550 (San Carlos, AZ): 58.5%. Arizona median: 16.5%.
    - 33311 (Fort Lauderdale): 51.3% of 28,670 returns. Florida median: 17.1%.
  - Tracks the EITC share (r = 0.86). That's the dull explanation: big refunds, bought early.

**Why probed:** a ten-year-old snapshot of a pattern consumer groups have already published. Usable for 2016 ZIP work once the "0" and 99999 rows are dropped and ZIPs are padded.
