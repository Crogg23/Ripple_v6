## 1. USGS earthquakes: a Louisiana gas-field parish that never shook now does. **live**

**What the table is**
- 443,274 events, M2.5 and up, worldwide, 2010-01-01 to 2026-06-13.
- One row per event. No duplicate IDs (S05).
- Near-duplicates across networks: about 114 pairs within 5 seconds and ~20 km (S17). Negligible.
- ⚠ The series stops 2026-06-13 even though records were updated as late as 2026-06-28. So 2026 is Jan 1 - Jun 13. Every 2026 comparison below uses that same window in earlier years.

**The chain: Red River Parish, Louisiana (S14, S15)**
- Box: lat 31.8-32.5, lon -93.55 to -93.25. The PLACE labels name Edgefield, Coushatta, Hall Summit, Ringgold, Pleasant Hill.
- Events of M2.5+ in the box, by year:

| Year | All M2.5+ | M3+ | M4+ | Biggest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2010-2023 | **0** | 0 | 0 | none |
| 2024 | 8 | 1 | 0 | 3.6 |
| 2025 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 2.9 |
| 2026 (to Jun 13) | **18** | **8** | **3** | **4.9** |

- The 29 events fall on 21 different days, so it isn't one burst.
- 2026-03-05: M4.9. USGS's own label is "2026 Red River Parish, Louisiana Earthquake".
- 2026-03-09: M4.1 and M4.4, **52 seconds apart and about 1.7 km apart.** They might be one quake listed twice. Not resolved.
- The M4.9 is the biggest Louisiana-labeled quake in the catalog on land. The only bigger "Louisiana" row is an M4.6 169 km offshore (2018).

**Peer check (S14):**
- Lower-48 half-degree cells east of 105.5°W with at least one M3+ in Jan 1 - Jun 13, 2026: 39. The median has 1.
- The Edgefield cell has 8. That's #2.
- #1 is the Permian's main Delaware Basin cell (36), which had 565 M3+ in 2010-2025.
- Edgefield's whole record before 2026: 1 M3+.

**Next door, same gas field (S15):**
- Caddo Parish (Mooringsport and Blanchard): 10 events from 2021 to Jan 2025, max M3.5.
- Timpson, Texas, 100 km west: 16 events 2012-2020, max M4.8 (2012).

**Dull explanations, tested:**
- *Better detection, not more quakes.* Mostly ruled out.
  - In 2012-13 the catalog logged M2.5-2.8 events at Timpson, so the region already had M2.5 coverage.
  - This box logged zero for 14 years.
  - An M4.9 is never missed.
- *Natural quakes.* **Not ruled out.** Louisiana has had rare natural quakes (outside knowledge). The catalog can't tell natural from induced.
- *One swarm.* Ruled out: 21 separate days over 29 months.

**Traps inside the lead:**
- 22 of the 29 events sit at exactly 5.0 km, a fixed depth. So nobody knows how deep they are.
- All 29 come from the `us` network and are "reviewed".

**Context, not proof (S16):**
- FracFocus lists 1,430 frack disclosures in the box.
  - Top operators: Aethon 227, Chesapeake 179, Vine 126, BHP 110, Encana 90, Southwestern 84.
- ⚠ The mart has **no job dates**, so fracking can't be lined up against 2024-26. Nothing here ties any operator to any quake.

**Hit means:** a parish with no recorded quakes for 14 years now has a growing sequence, capped by an M4.9. It sits in the Haynesville gas field (the name is outside knowledge; FracFocus shows 1,430 frack disclosures there).
**Miss means:** if the state's injection-well records show no change in disposal nearby, the likely answer is natural. It's still news, but a smaller story.
**Next join:** Louisiana SONRIS Class II disposal-well volumes by month, within 15 km (not in the warehouse). Or FracFocus job dates, once the staging cast is fixed (g38 parked this).

### Table traps (for the ledger)
- ⚠ **PLACE names the nearest town's state, not where the quake is.** 1,649 of the 3,675 Texas-located Permian events are labeled "New Mexico" (e.g. "55 km S of Whites City, New Mexico"). A state count built on PLACE is wrong. Use lat/lon.
- PLACE writes the same state two ways: `CA` 14,217 vs `California` 4,620 in the lower 48. Also `MX`/`Mexico`, `NV`/`Nevada`, plus `western Texas` and "... Earthquake" name tags.
- **29.0% of rows sit on four fixed depths:** 10 km (96,443), 35 km (21,402), 5 km (6,636), 0 km (4,165). The glance's "31,990 rows over 10x median depth" are deep quakes (median 17 km, max 697 km), not bad rows. Where they sit wasn't checked.
- MAGTYPE has case twins: `md` 37,998 vs `Md` 9,014, `ml`/`Ml`, `mb`/`Mb`, `mb_lg`/`mblg`. TexNet quakes show up as `ml` and, in 2023 only, `ml(texnet)` (147).
- The Permian switches networks in 2021: `us` located 280 of 281 events in 2020; `tx` (TexNet) located 547 of 608 in 2021. Magnitude scales change with it, so trust M4+ over M3+ for 2020-vs-2021 comparisons.
- The June-July 2018 spike (7,623 rows in July) is Hawaii: 10,692 events vs 50 in the same months of 2017 (S18).
- 3,950 rows are not earthquakes: mining explosions 2,753, explosions 747, ice quakes, quarry blasts, 4 nuclear explosions. TYPE has case twins too (`Landslide`/`landslide`, `Ice Quake`/`ice quake`). Filter on TYPE.

## 2. NTSB injury counts: who got hurt on each aircraft. **probed**

**What the table is (S07-S09)**
- 179,179 rows: one per aircraft × kind of person × injury level.
  - Kinds of person: Flig = flight crew, Cabi = cabin crew, Pass = passengers, LapC = lap children.
- One load run. Every natural key is unique. No duplicate loads.
- 29,516 events, every one in the events table. 1,452 events in the events table have no injury rows.
- Deaths reconcile exactly: FATL summed per event equals the events table's INJ_TOT_F on all 29,516 events.

**⚠ The trap that matters: cabin crew vanish from mid-2020 to late 2023 (S11, S12)**

| US airline flights (Part 121) | Flight crew per aircraft | Cabin crew per aircraft |
|---|---|---|
| 2008-2019 | 2.1-2.6 (one year 4.0) | 2.5-4.6 |
| 2020 | 4.1 | 1.4 |
| 2021 | 5.5 | **0.0** |
| 2022 | 5.7 | 0.4 |
| 2023 | 5.0 | 1.1 |
| 2024-2026 | 2.1-2.3 | 3.1-4.4 |

- By quarter: from 2020 Q3 to 2023 Q3 nearly every airliner record puts all crew under "Flig". The split comes back in 2024 Q1.
- Foreign airlines (Part 129) show the same thing: 0 cabin crew in 2021, 2022 and 2023.
- **Any "pilot injuries" count for 2020-23 is really pilots plus flight attendants.**

**The flight-attendant numbers (US Part 121, 2008 to Jul 2026)**
- 708 aircraft in 667 events. 77,131 people aboard.
- Clean years only (2020-23 left out):
  - Cabin crew: 1,889 of 63,437 aboard (**3.0%**)
  - Seriously injured: **175 cabin crew, 57 passengers, 4 pilots.** Cabin crew are **74%**.
  - Serious-injury rate: cabin crew 9.3%, passengers 0.1%.
- 202 of the 218 aircraft with a seriously hurt crew member have no damage recorded. That's the turbulence pattern: someone standing gets thrown.
- **Time:** aircraft with at least one crew member seriously hurt (crew = Flig+Cabi, so the trap cancels out):
  - 2008-19: mean 11.2 a year, median 11.5
  - 2023: 16. 2024: 15. 2025: 15. Mean 15.3.
  - Poisson against the old mean: p = 0.023. Crew seriously hurt: 11.5/yr → 19, 19, 17 (p = 0.0008).
- **Who carries it:** Southwest, United, Delta and American hold 11-12 a year in 2023-25, against 6.5 a year in 2008-19. Everyone else: 3-5 a year in 2023-25, against 4.7 a year in 2008-19.

**Dull explanations:**
- *More flights.* **Not tested.** The warehouse has no departure counts.
- *Mergers* fold other airlines into the big four. That explains their share, not the total.
- *Known story.* NTSB published a turbulence study saying flight attendants take most serious injuries (outside knowledge, not rechecked). This replicates it and adds 2023-25.
- *Selection.* An airline "accident" usually exists because someone was seriously hurt, so rates per person aboard are inflated for everyone. The share still holds.

**Other checks**
- **Balloons:** 200 US balloon accidents, all Part 91, with 964 passengers aboard. 150 passengers seriously injured, 27 killed. 114 of the 200 had a passenger seriously hurt.
  - Balloons are 102 of the 125 serious passenger injuries (82%) on Part 91 sightseeing rides.
  - The top named operator, Rainbow Ryders (6 accidents, 5 with serious injuries), is a large ride operator (outside knowledge), so fleet size likely explains it. 81 of the 200 have no operator name.
- **Lap children:** 29 US aircraft with a lap child aboard. 3 lap children killed, 1 seriously hurt.
  - One of the 3 deaths (Wallis, TX, 2012) is coded on an IS-28B2 two-seat glider with two passengers also dead. That looks miscoded.

**Table traps (for the ledger)**
- **TOTL is not a total.** It's blank on 22,587 of 48,981 aircraft-person groups (46%). When filled, it matches the sum of the other levels only 13,212 times and disagrees 13,182 times.
  - The glance's "sum 260.6K" includes 12,801 TOTL people, and 187,224 of it is uninjured passengers.
  - All 13% blank INJ_PERSON_COUNT rows are TOTL rows.
- **LCHG_DATE is a migration stamp.** 96,424 rows (54%) carry 2020-09-25, the earliest date in the column. "Trend by LCHG_DATE" measures nothing. Use EV_DATE from the events table.
- LCHG_USERID is the word `None` on 108,376 rows (60%).
- Aircraft table: PHASE_FLT_SPEC is blank on all 1,157 extract rows, so phase of flight isn't available. DAMAGE is blank on 350 of 708 airline rows.
