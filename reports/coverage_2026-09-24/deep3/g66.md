## Traps for the ledger (plain list)

4. **CFTC futures:** reports were twice a month until 1992 and weekly after. Don't compare yearly row counts across 1992.
5. **Google:** "EIN only" does not mean "not FEC-registered". AMERICANS FOR PROSPERITY ACTION INC, a super PAC with an FEC ID (outside knowledge), gave Google only an EIN. 3,418 accounts give a state registration instead, and some "FEC IDs" are really candidate IDs (H…, S…) or typos (`COO`, lower-case `c`).

7. **Google:** all 81 official House office accounts share the House's EIN 53-6002523. An EIN join merges them into one organization.
8. **Google:** one group can have two advertiser IDs, e.g. One Nation (EIN) and ONE NATION (FEC C90016262). Sum by group before ranking.
9. **FEC_PAC_SUMMARY** leaves out candidate committees. Only 399 of 1,059 Google FEC IDs land there, but 1,053 land in COMMITTEES_DIM.
