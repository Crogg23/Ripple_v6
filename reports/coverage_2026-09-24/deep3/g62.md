## 2. ENERGY__FED_EIA860_2_PLANT: **probed**

**What it is:** 16,132 rows = 16,132 plant codes, one file (`2___Plant_Y2024.xlsx`). 28 rows have no latitude.

### The ash-pond fields [q03, q07]
One Y/N per plant for "ash impoundment", one for "lined", one status code.
- 316 plants say Y. Unlined + OP: **119**. Lined + OP: **101**. Unlined + out of service: 70.

### Named, with the generators joined [q11]
Plant → 2024 generators (PLANT_CODE) → eGRID 2022 (ORIS code). 103 ash-pond plants still run coal and call the pond OP.
- Unlined: **51 plants, 60.2 GW of coal.** 34 of them have **no retirement date on any coal unit**: 37.3 GW, and 126 TWh of coal power in 2022.
  - Biggest (data match): Bowen (Georgia Power, 3,499 MW), Gibson (Duke Energy Indiana, 3,340), John E Amos (Appalachian Power, 2,933), James H Miller Jr (Alabama Power, 2,822), Scherer (Georgia Power, 2,673), Roxboro (Duke Energy Progress, 2,558).
  - 7 of the 34 carry "Duke Energy" in the operator name.
- **Peer test:** among lined-pond coal plants, **43 of 52** have no retirement date. Among unlined, **34 of 51**. Unlined plants are *more* likely to have a date. No "unlined plants dodge retirement" signal.

### Dull explanation, tested [q15]: does "OP" mean "taking ash now"?
**No.** 68 of the 119 unlined "operating" ponds sit at plants with **no coal unit running** in 2024:
- 23 have no generator at all. The plant is gone, and the pond still reads OP. Examples: Widows Creek, Kanawha River, Pirkey, Rush Island, Harllee Branch.
- 15 are paper mills (black liquor). The pond holds mill boiler ash, not coal ash.
- 18 now run mainly on gas. 4 of those still burned coal in 2022 (eGRID), so they're dual-fuel or recently converted.
- Walter C Beckjord (OH) is now a 4 MW battery, and its unlined pond still reads OP.

So "OP" reads as "not closed yet", not "receiving ash". Nobody can write "still dumping" from this field.

### Enforcement join [q16]
Plant code → CAMD program ID in the combined-emissions table → FRS registry ID → ECHO. Land: 29 of 34 in the unlined no-date group, 48 of 52 lined. The state agrees on every hit.

| Coal plants, 2024 | Plants in ECHO | Median quarters in noncompliance (of 12) | 4+ quarters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlined OP, no coal date | 29 | 3 | 48% |
| Unlined OP, some dated | 17 | 4 | 59% |
| Lined OP | 48 | 2 | 40% |
| Pond not OP | 19 | 5 | 63% |
| No pond reported | 65 | 1 | 31% |

**Miss.** No gradient that singles out unlined ponds. ECHO counts every program (mostly air), not ash. Plants with ponds are bigger, older coal plants.

### FERC flags vs the plant's own generators [q17]
- "Small power producer" = Y on 4,732 plants. **29 run more than 80 MW** (3,922 MW). Some have exempt-wholesale-generator dockets (EG15-49-000) in the small-producer docket box. Sun Streams 3 (AZ, 430 MW, 2024) is the biggest. The pre-1995 waste-coal and geothermal ones may be legit under the 1990 exemption *(outside)*.
- "Cogeneration" = Y on 447 plants. **9 have no heat-and-power unit** and sit in a non-CHP sector (404 MW). Biggest: Bucksport Generation (ME, 187 MW). Its paper mill closed in 2014 *(outside)*.
- 97 FERC docket numbers sit on 2+ plants. "TBD" is the docket on 7.

**Verdict: probed.** Three soft fields are stacked (pond status, one lined flag per plant, retirement date), and each has a dull reading. The 34-plant list is a **lead to check** against the utilities' federal coal-ash compliance postings *(outside, not in the warehouse)*. It is not a finding.
**Next join:** EPA coal-ash (CCR) rule compliance data, per pond, if it can be landed.
