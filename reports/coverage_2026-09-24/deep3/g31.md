## 1. What the table is, and the traps [q01, q02, q24]

One row per filing. 6,231 to 7,675 rows a quarter.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| ADSH (filing number) | Unique in every quarter. Max 1 row per ADSH. |
| Glance's "ADSH top 57 / 50 / 48 / 46 / 52" | **Not repeats.** Every ADSH appears once. Ignore those glance numbers. |
| Same ADSH in two quarter tables | 0. No duplicate loads. |
| FILED dates | Parse on 100% of rows. Every date falls inside its own quarter. |
| PERIOD dates | 0-3 bad per quarter. |
| EIN | Always 9 characters. `000000000` filler on 1,363-1,851 rows a quarter (22-26%). Known trap, reconfirmed. |

**Trap: the "later amended" flag (PREVRPT) goes dead in 2025** [q24]
It only marks amendments that landed before the SEC cut that quarter's file.

| Quarter | Originals the index shows were later amended | Flagged PREVRPT=1 |
|---|---|---|
| 2024Q2 | 181 | 119 |
| 2024Q3 | 100 | 37 |
| 2024Q4 | 65 | 9 |
| 2025Q1 | 99 | **0** |
| 2025Q2 | 80 | **1** |

Fix: find amendments by matching CIK + PERIOD + FORM to a later `10-K/A` or `10-Q/A`. Never trust the flag.

**Trap: the April 1, 2024 bulge is on-time filing** [q02]
- **310** 10-Ks were filed on 2024-04-01 alone.
- The small-company deadline (90 days after Dec 31) fell on Saturday, March 30, 2024, so it rolled to Monday, April 1.
- In 2025 it fell on Monday, March 31, inside Q1.
- So 2024Q2 holds 1,033 10-Ks and 2025Q2 only 624. That's the calendar, not a change in behavior.

## 2. Time: how late, same quarters across years [q02]

"Late" = filed after the SEC deadline for that form and filer size, plus the one allowed extension, plus 3 weekend days.
- 10-K: 60 / 75 / 90 days (large / accelerated / small filer), +18.
- 10-Q: 40 / 40 / 45 days, +8.

| Quarter | 10-Ks late | 10-Qs late | 10-Qs 90+ days past |

- 10-Qs filed 90+ days past deadline rose a bit (62 to 74, 60 to 71). That's too small to lead.
- Extension users: 355 10-Ks filed April 2-16, 2024, against 295 filed April 1-15, 2025.

## 10. Traps for the ledger

1. **PREVRPT is dead from 2025Q1:** 0 flagged against 99 amended. Even in 2024Q2-Q4 it caught only 14-66% of amendments (9 of 65 up to 119 of 181). Match on CIK + PERIOD + `/A` instead.
2. **The glance battery's "ADSH top 46-57" numbers are not repeats.** ADSH is unique in every quarter.
3. **The Q2 10-K count swings with the calendar.** 310 on-time 10-Ks landed on 2024-04-01 because the deadline rolled off a weekend. Compare Q1+Q2 together, or parse deadlines.
4. **Form AP blank CIK on SEC registrants:** 222 of 32,608 company audit rows since 2023 (0.7%). 41 match an SEC filer by name + fiscal year end. Michael Gillespie & Associates filed the most (30 rows). A CIK-only join calls these companies unaudited.
5. **Form AP typo CIK:** Bimergen under 10667674, not 1066764.
6. **Form AP under an old CIK after a holding-company reorganization:** 1895 Bancorp of Wisconsin.
7. **Form AP firm renames change FIRM_ID:** BDO USA LLP to BDO USA, P.C.; Mazars USA to Forvis Mazars; Marcum to CBIZ. Merge them before counting "auditor switches."
8. **Form AP includes 401(k) and other plan audits under the sponsor's CIK.** Filter `AUDIT_REPORT_TYPE`, or a second "audit" appears (The Bancorp's plan, dated June).
9. **EIN `000000000` filler on 22-26% of rows every quarter** (reconfirmed).
