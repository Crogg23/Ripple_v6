## 1. FED_VOTEVIEW_MEMBERS: **live**

The members table gives each person's name, party and state for each Congress.
The work is a join to `POLITICS__VOTEVIEW_VOTES` (one row per member per roll call) and `POLITICS__VOTEVIEW_ROLLCALLS` (the dates).
The join key is ICPSR, stored as text in the members table and as a number in the votes table. 1 unmatched row per chamber per Congress.

**Checked**
- The votes table covers only the 118th and 119th Congresses. House: 1,235 roll calls (118th), then 587 through 2026-06-25. Senate: 691, then 851.
- "Missed" = cast code 9 (not voting). "Present" votes don't count as missed.
- Each member is counted only over roll calls held while they served. Members who leave simply stop having rows: Sherrill's end at roll 303, Swalwell's at 468, Grijalva's at 70.
- Delegates (AS, GU, PR, MP, VI, DC) are left out of the streak ranking. They only vote in Committee of the Whole (538 of 1,235 roll calls), so they topped the raw list for no reason.

**Peer: 119th House, 445 voting members**
- Median missed rate 1.87%. p90 7.0%.
- Median longest missed streak **3**. p90 **14**.

**The lead: Kean (K000398)**
- 144 missed of 587 (24.5%).
- **140 in a row**, from 2026-03-17 to 2026-06-25. The streak runs right up to the last roll call in the data.
- Last vote cast: 2026-03-05.
- By month: 0-2 missed each month through Feb 2026. Then March 21/30, April 47/47, May 36/36, June 36/36.
- He still has a row on every roll call, so the data says he's still a member who isn't voting, not a vacancy.
- Benchmark from the 118th: Kay Granger's end-of-term run was **127** in a row (2024-07-24 to 2024-12-20). Kean's streak is already longer.

**Other checked absences (119th House)**
- Wesley Hunt (R-TX): 155/587 = 26.4%. Peak was Jan 2026 at 43/47. Then Feb 14/30, Mar 6/30, Apr 3/47, May 1/36, Jun 0/36.
- Mikie Sherrill: 171/303 = 56.4% before her rows end on 2025-11-20.
- Eric Swalwell: 164/468 = 35.0%, including 57/57 in Dec 2025. His rows end 2026-03-27.
- Josh Gottheimer: 71 of 184 missed from Jan to Jun 2025, then 0-3 a month.

**Dull explanation, tested: running for another office**
- 18 House members match a 2026 Senate or President FEC filing on surname + state.
  - Their median missed rate is 2.64%, against 1.87% for the other 422. Pooled: 4.81% vs 3.12%.
- Matching on FEC ID through `MEMBER_CROSSWALK.FEC_IDS` found **0**, because the crosswalk doesn't carry the new Senate IDs.
- The surname match is loose. Shontel Brown (OH) matched a different Brown's Senate filing.
- Governor races aren't in FEC data at all, so those campaigns can't be seen here.
- Kean matched no 2026 Senate or President filing. A campaign doesn't explain his run. Illness or leave might; the warehouse can't test that.

**Hit means:** a sitting member has cast no vote for three months while the House kept voting (the House-wide missed rate stayed at 3.1-3.7% a month).
**Miss would mean:** the streak ends in the source, or the House Clerk's roll calls show him voting.
**Next:** check the House Clerk roll calls for 2026-03-17 onward, then call his office. (Outside fact, not in the warehouse: Granger's absence became a national story in Dec 2024.)

**Traps**
- ⚠ `PARTY_CODE` is text: '100.0'/'200.0' in the 115th-117th, '100'/'200' everywhere else. Filtering `= '100'` silently drops three Congresses.
- A "President" chamber row appears in every Congress (121 before the 113th). BIOGUIDE is blank on 61 of them.
- Party switchers get a second ICPSR. Manchin's 328 (independent) row starts at roll 543. One person, two rows.
- `NOMINATE_NUMBER_OF_VOTES` counts only scaled votes: a 119th House median of 490, against 587 roll calls held.
- The battery's "CONGRESS sum 3.6M" adds up Congress numbers. CONGRESS is a label, not a metric.

## 2. FED_GOVINFO_BILLSTATUS: **live**

107,150 bills from the 113th through 119th Congresses, introduced 2013-01-03 to 2026-06-26.
Final public-law counts: 296, 329, 442, 344, 362, 274. The 119th has 99 so far.
Rows match bill keys one to one, except the 117th: 10 duplicate keys and 11 blank sponsors.

**Time check: the same point in every Congress**
The 119th's data ends on day 539 of that Congress, so every Congress is cut at its own day 539.

| Congress | Law-eligible bills introduced | Public laws | Per 1,000 bills | Laws that aren't CRA / naming / spending | Joint resolutions enacted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 113 | 7,718 | 121 | 15.7 | 93 | 6 |
| 114 | 8,797 | 183 | 20.8 | 134 | 3 |
| 115 | 9,557 | 192 | 20.1 | 145 | 24 |
| 116 | 11,625 | 143 | 12.3 | 106 | 7 |
| 117 | 12,859 | 158 | 12.3 | 121 | 6 |
| 118 | 13,732 | 65 | 4.7 | 43 | 5 |
| 119 | 14,814 | 99 | 6.7 | **67** | 25 |

- The 119th's 99 laws include 21 titled "congressional disapproval": Congressional Review Act (CRA) votes striking agency rules. It also has 1 naming law and 10 spending or extension laws.
- The 119th's 25 enacted joint resolutions match the 115th's 24 (2017). Same shape: a new trifecta repealing the last administration's rules.
- Bill introductions nearly doubled at the same point: 7,718 became 14,814. Laws didn't follow.

**Post offices, checked and dropped**
- The 119th has enacted 1 naming law, against 5-28 at the same point before.
- But 104 post-office naming bills were introduced (61-127 is normal), and 22 are sitting at "reached floor" in `BILLS`.
- Naming laws pass in end-of-Congress batches: the 118th went from 7 at day 539 to 68 final. Not a story yet.

**Sponsors with zero laws (113th-118th, peer = same chamber and party)**
- Rand Paul: 329 law-eligible bills, 0 public laws. Senate Republican median: 128.5 bills, 3 laws.
  - 40 of his titles are "disapproval" resolutions (built to fail). That still leaves 289.
  - 47 Senate Republicans filed 100+ bills. Two had zero laws: Paul and Marshall.
- Michael Bennet: 256 bills, 0 laws. Senate Democrat median: 160 bills, 3 laws.
- Companion-bill test, same Congress, exact title: of the 19 sponsors with 100+ bills and 0 laws, 8 have at least one bill whose twin became law under someone else's name. Paul has none.

**Dull explanation:** fewer, bigger laws. The NDAA and omnibus bills carry dozens of other bills' text. It **can't be tested here**: there's no page count and no "text included in" field.
**Hit means:** the 119th is on pace for the second-thinnest record in this window, behind only the 118th.
**Miss would mean:** a big lame-duck batch, which is how naming bills and packages usually land.
**Next join:** `FED_GOVINFO_BILL_COSPONSORS` to test whether bipartisan cosponsorship still predicts enactment in the 118th-119th.

**Traps**
- ⚠ `LATEST_ACTION_DATE` isn't always the enactment date. In the 115th, only 326 of 442 laws have "Became Public Law" as their latest action. Treat by-date counts for the 115th as approximate.
- ⚠ Title keywords undercount CRA votes. Some start "Disapproving the rule…": the 115th shows 9 titled disapprovals but 24 enacted joint resolutions. The "not CRA / naming / spending" column is a keyword cut, not a real classifier.
- `N_ACTIONS` and `N_COSPONSORS` are stored as text. All of them parse.
- Blank `BILL_TYPE` values turn up in the 113th, 115th and 117th.
- The battery's "CONGRESS sum 12.5M / top sponsor holds 1%" is bill counts times ~117. It means nothing.

## 3. BILLS: probed

**Checked**
- An exact copy of BILLSTATUS for the 118th-119th: 36,465 rows, every one matched on Congress + type + number.
- 0 disagreements on law number, latest action date or action count. Both tables end 2026-06-26.
- It adds derived flags. `CONGRESS_PARTIAL` is true on all 17,150 rows of the 119th.

**⚠ Trap: `ADVANCED_PAST_COMMITTEE`**
- 118th House, law-eligible bills: 4,314 are flagged advanced but sit at stage "committee_action".
- For **3,465 of them (80%)**, the latest action is "Referred to the Subcommittee on…". They never left committee.
- House flag rate: 44% (D) and 49% (R). Share that actually reached the floor: 3.5% (D) and 9.4% (R).
- Use `LATEST_STAGE in ('reached_floor','passed_both_to_president','became_law')` instead.

**Peer check with the real stage (118th)**
- 118 of 219 House Democrats (minority party) got no bill to the floor. Only 27 of 228 Republicans didn't.
- Senate (Democratic majority): Shaheen 73 bills, none to the floor, against a Democratic median of 61.5 bills and 2 floor bills. Cotton 68, Luján 66.
- Dull explanation: minority status, plus provisions riding in packages. Not story-grade.

## 4. SENATE_TRADES: probed

8,350 rows, 57 senators, 2012-06-14 to 2020-12-02.
Already mined: F-009 (banking), probes 78 (committee overlap) and 91 (trade then bill).

**Paper filers (peer = every senator with a row dated 2015 or later, 52 of them)**
- 463 rows are "PDF Disclosed Filing" placeholders. Each is one row per scanned report, with no ticker, no transaction type and no amount.
- 46 of the 52 filed no paper report after 2014. Six did.
- Four filed **only** paper, 2012-2020: Burr 96 of 96 reports, Blumenthal 50/50, Boozman 43/43, Feinstein 29/29. That's 186 paper reports since 2015.
- 2012-2013 are mostly placeholders: 218 of 244 rows.

**COVID window check (24 Jan - 20 Feb, electronic rows only)**
- 2020: 72 sales by 5 senators. The same weeks in 2015-2019: 149, 55, 44, 51, 44. By count, there was no Senate-wide selling spike.
- Loeffler: 36 sales, at least $1.38M. She was new in Jan 2020, so there's no baseline to compare.
- Perdue: 27 sales, against his own same-weeks average of 22.8. Normal for him.
- Inhofe: 6 sales, against an average of 1.2, at least $230K.
- ⚠ Burr's reports from 2020-02-26 and 2020-02-27 are two blank paper rows here. Feinstein's 2020-02-25 report is one. (Outside fact, not in the warehouse: Burr's February 2020 sales drew the insider-trading inquiry.) This table can't see the best-known case.

**Hit means:** a senator's trades are only readable by opening scans.
**Miss means little:** for four senators, a "no trade" result here is blindness, not innocence.

**Traps**
- ⚠ Placeholder rows: 463. Their TRANSACTION_DATE looks like a report date, not a trade date. Row counts per senator undercount the paper filers.
- 354 groups of identical rows sit inside a single electronic report (752 rows), and 504 of those rows are Perdue's. They may be real separate lots; the table can't say.
- 74 rows show the same trade under two report links (amendments). Dedupe before counting.
- `MATCH_METHOD` has one value on every row: 'surname+senate+term-span'.
- `ASSET_DESCRIPTION` carries HTML leftovers (`AT&amp;T`).

## 5. MEMBER_SPINE: skip (lookup)

12,794 rows, one per member key. It has the same row count as `MEMBER_CROSSWALK`. It's a dimension table.

**⚠ Trap for anyone using its ideology columns**
- `HAS_VOTEVIEW_MATCH` is false for 496 rows. Spine ICPSR is null on all 496.
- **217 of them are current members** (198 House, 19 Senate), and all 217 **are** in `FED_VOTEVIEW_MEMBERS` by bioguide. So 217 of the 537 current rows (40%) show `IDEOLOGY_LABEL = 'unknown'` and a blank DW-NOMINATE score for no real reason.
- The same goes for 61 historical House members and 8 historical senators.
- `IDEOLOGY_LABEL` is just the sign of DIM1. "Centrist" means exactly 0 (5 rows). A score of -0.001 counts as "left/liberal".
- 13 stand-in keys (`gt:…`) have no bioguide.
