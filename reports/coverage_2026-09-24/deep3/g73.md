## 1. XWALK_HOSPITAL_CCN_EIN: 40% of the "hospitals" are hospital units. **probed**

What it is: a name match between Medicare hospital numbers (CCN) and IRS tax IDs (EIN), built 2026-09-11 by `scripts/xwalk_hospital_ccn_ein.py`.
Four rules, tried in order: name+ZIP, doing-business-as+ZIP, name+state, street+ZIP.

**Checked**
- Profile by rule (S07): 4,003 rows, 4,003 distinct CCNs, one build time.
  - name+ZIP 2,332 · dba+ZIP 33 · name+state 1,280 · street+ZIP 358.
  - Every EIN is 9 digits. No width trap here.
- **Grain (S12, S13, S14).** 1,607 CCNs are not six plain digits.
  - 1,566 are unit codes: a letter in slot 3. S is a psych unit, T a rehab unit, U and Z swing beds, M and R units in critical-access hospitals.
  - 41 carry suffixes or extra digits: `330195004`, `07002201`, `310032A`.
  - **1,554 of the 1,607 sit in the table next to their own parent hospital's row, with the same EIN** (S14). 2 have a different EIN; 51 have no parent row.
  - The 2,396 plain CCNs map to 2,046 EINs.
  - Example: Long Island Jewish Medical Center shows up as 5 CCNs on one EIN.
- **EIN fan-out (S08).** 2,075 EINs. 1,292 of them hold 2+ CCNs, and those cover 3,220 of 4,003 rows (80%).
  - Top: Kaiser Foundation Hospitals 35 · IHC Health Services 29 · NYC Health + Hospitals 26 · Sutter Bay Hospitals 18.
  - All of those came in through name+state only (tier 3).
- **Street+ZIP matches (S12).** 101 of 358 (28%) have an IRS name that looks nothing like the hospital (Jaro-Winkler under 70).
  - For name matches (tiers 1 and 3), 0 do. That's by design.
  - It's a mix, so every one needs eyes:
  - **Tenants.** A hospital-in-a-hospital gets the host's EIN, because they share a street address. SELECT SPECIALTY HOSPITAL - FLINT (a for-profit long-term care hospital) ➔ MCLAREN FLINT. LHCG CXVIII LLC (CCN 042008, long-term care) ➔ MERCY HEALTH FORT SMITH COMMUNITIES. 10 of the 101 are long-term care hospitals.
  - **Side groups at the same address.** MAINEHEALTH (200021) ➔ MID COAST GERIATRIC SERVICES CORP.
  - **Old owner or old name, probably.** STATE UNIVERSITY OF IOWA (160029) ➔ "MHIC". Not checked.
  - **Real parents.** WEST PENN ALLEGHENY HEALTH SYSTEM ➔ HIGHMARK HEALTH.
- **For-profits (S09, S13).** 110 rows are flagged for-profit (P) but matched to a tax-exempt EIN.
  - 37 of them are units whose parent hospital is flagged nonprofit in the same Medicare file.
  - One eyeballed row: CCN 050179, legal name DOCTORS MEDICAL CENTER OF MODESTO INC, ZIP 95382 ➔ EMANUEL MEDICAL CENTER **MEDICAL STAFF ASSOCIATION**. That's a doctors' group at the hospital, not the hospital.
- **The for-profit flag flips inside one hospital (S13).** Unit and parent disagree in 119 of 3,003 pairs (4.0%). It goes both ways.
  - 34T047 P vs 340047 N, NORTH CAROLINA BAPTIST HOSPITAL.
  - 19S011 N vs 190011 P, BRFHH MONROE LLC.
- **Coverage against its own source (S10).** 9,175 CCNs in Medicare hospital enrollments.
  - Nonprofit short-term hospitals: 1,491 of 2,405 matched (62.0%).
  - For-profit short-term: 28 of 717 (3.9%). Expected, since the IRS file lists tax-exempt groups only.
  - Psych hospitals, nonprofit: 63 of 254 (24.8%).
- **Peer, by state** (nonprofit short-term, states with 10+ hospitals): median 60.9%.
  - Lowest: Alabama 14 of 47 (29.8%) · New Jersey 17 of 54 (31.5%) · Oklahoma 18 of 49 (36.7%).
  - Highest: Connecticut 25 of 28 (89.3%) · New York 111 of 136 (81.6%).

**Hit means:** tiers 1-3 on a plain six-digit CCN give you a 990 filer. Often it's the system, not the building.
**Miss means:** nothing about the hospital. 38% of nonprofit short-term hospitals miss. In Alabama and New Jersey it's 2 in 3.
**Dull explanation:** the Medicare file lists each unit as its own CCN, and the build matched every row. Tested (S13, S14).
Why Alabama, New Jersey and Oklahoma miss more is **not tested**. Guess: public hospital authorities that aren't in the IRS exempt list, and systems that file under a different name.

**How to use it:** drop unit and suffixed CCNs, skip tier 4 or eyeball it, and dedupe to EIN before summing any 990 money.

## 2. DIM_ZIP_POINT: every ZIP dot is a county dot. **skip**

**Checked (S06, S11, S15)**
- 33,486 of 33,486 points equal DIM_COUNTY's population center for the ZIP's county.
  - 3,066 distinct dots. Median 7 ZIPs per dot. **304 ZIPs sit on Los Angeles County's one dot.**
  - `POINT_METHOD` says "county centroid". It's the population center, not the middle of the map shape.
- **Missing: 305 ZIPs** that the crosswalk has.
  - Connecticut 288, which is all of it: 288 CT ZIPs in the crosswalk, 0 CT rows here (S15). Guam 7, Virgin Islands 6, Northern Marianas 3, American Samoa 1.
  - Cause: CT's old county codes in the crosswalk don't exist in DIM_COUNTY (S04). A join dropped them.
- **Primary county = the most populous county the ZIP touches.** 10,174 of 10,174 multi-county ZIPs.
  - The catalog says "the county the ZIP area mostly falls in." That's wrong.
  - Example: 93243 is Lebec, a Kern County town. It's filed under Los Angeles County (10.0M people vs Kern's 909K).
  - 384 multi-county ZIPs touch counties 20x apart in size. That's where a small-town ZIP most likely lands in the big county.
  - Neither table holds how a ZIP splits by area or people, so the real share can't be checked in the warehouse.
- `COUNTY_SPAN` agrees with the crosswalk on every ZIP (0 disagree).

**What it means:** fine for county-level maps. Not a ZIP map. Any ZIP layer built on it has no Connecticut.

## Traps for the ledger

1. **Glance "top" counts come from an estimate and can be up to 170x too high.** 245 vs 31, 347 vs 2. Recount before quoting.
2. **DIM_ZIP_POINT is a county dot.** No Connecticut. Multi-county ZIPs go to the biggest county, not the one they mostly sit in.
3. **Connecticut splits the geography tables.** Crosswalk: old counties. DIM_COUNTY: planning regions. ZIP ➔ county ➔ people drops the whole state.
4. **Hospital crosswalk grain.** 1,607 of 4,003 CCNs are units or suffixed codes. 1,554 share an EIN with their parent's row in the table. Sums double count.
5. **Hospital crosswalk street+ZIP rule.** 101 of 358 (28%) name mismatch. Tenant hospitals and side groups at the same address get matched.
6. **Medicare's for-profit flag flips between a hospital and its own units** in 4.0% of pairs. Filter on the parent's flag.

parked: every group's `found` line quotes the same `APPROX_TOP_K` counts. A recount pass on the ones used in leads is cheap: one query per table.
