## 1. REF__DIM_GEOGRAPHY: **dead** (a lookup that looks up wrong)

**What it is:** one row per FIPS code seen in EPA's facility registry (FRS), with a facility count.
It's built by `ref__dim_geography.sql`: group FRS rows by padded FIPS, then `any_value()` for name, state and EPA region.

**The mechanism, in plain words**
- `any_value()` grabs whatever row comes first. It picks each column on its own.
- So one row can get its county name from one stray facility, its state from another, and its state name from a third.
- San Diego (06073) has 65,509 FRS rows. 65,465 say CA. 17 say MN, 16 say IL.
  - The row it got: **COUNTY_NAME = WILL, STATE_ABBR = MN, STATE_NAME = ILLINOIS**. Its 61,899 facilities and its centroid are right.

**Checked**
- **Shapes of FIPS_CODE** (S05):

| Shape | Rows | Facilities | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 digits, e.g. `48201` | 3,422 | 3,089,019 (84.6%) | Harris TX |
| 2 letters + 3 digits, e.g. `TX201` | 3,239 | 433,588 (11.9%) | also Harris TX |
| `00` + 3 digits, e.g. `00201` | 325 | 126,670 (3.5%) | county number with no state |
| junk | 2 | 3 | `23OO7` (letter O's), `0NONE` |

- **Against the Census county file** (S09, `REFERENCE__CENSUS_CB_COUNTY`, newest vintage, 3,235 counties):
  - 3,180 counties show up **twice**, once as digits and once as letters. 256 of those show up a **third** time as `00xxx`.
  - 50 show up once. 5 are missing: Kusilvak AK and four tiny territory units.
  - 289 rows map to no current county. One is `12025`, the old Dade County code, with 1,028 facilities.
- **Labels against the FRS rows they came from** (S13):
  - FRS rows under one numeric FIPS agree on the state **99.93%** of the time.
  - Still, **99 of 3,422** numeric rows carry a state that isn't the majority state of their own rows.
  - Those 99 hold **535,112 facilities, 17.3%** of the numeric rows' total. Bigger counties have more stray rows, so they get hit more.
- **Top 25 counties by facility count** (S08): **9 of 25** carry a wrong state, and 4 of those a wrong county name too.

| FIPS | Real county | Label in the table |
|---|---|---|
| 06073 | San Diego CA | WILL / MN / ILLINOIS |
| 48201 | Harris TX | TANGIPAHOA / LA / TEXAS |
| 06085 | Santa Clara CA | SANTA CLARA COUNTY / OH, EPA region 05 |
| 04013 | Maricopa AZ | MARICOPA COUNTY / OH, state name blank, region 05 |
| 34029 | Ocean NJ | ORANGE / NY |
| 34025 | Monmouth NJ | SULLIVAN / NY |
| 34013, 34027, 34023 | Essex, Morris, Middlesex NJ | state OH, MN, NY |
| 12057 | Hillsborough FL (not top 25) | YORK / NY / PENNSYLVANIA |

- **What a name join does** (S10): "HARRIS" + TX lands on
  - `48375`, which is Potter County (Amarillo). Its label reads HARRIS COUNTY TX; its centroid sits at 35.20, -101.77.
  - `TX047`, which is Brooks County. Its label also reads HARRIS COUNTY.
  - `00201`, a no-state blend.
  - The real Harris row (48201) says TANGIPAHOA, so the join misses it.
- **What a FIPS join does:** Harris's facilities are split. 31,266 sit on `48201`, and 4,107 more on `TX201`, which is labeled state OK, name OKLAHOMA.
  - A join on 48201 alone misses at least 11.6% of Harris.
- **The `00xxx` rows blend states.** `00033` is labeled PRINCE GEORGE'S MD, but its centroid is 45.30, -118.76, in eastern Oregon. `00073` is labeled LEON FL, with a centroid on the Idaho-Montana line.
  - The 2026-07-28 padding fix made these. It turned a bare 3-digit county number into `00` + 3 digits.
- **Centroids on numeric rows are mostly fine** (S07): the median is 5.8 km from the Census polygon's center. 3,037 of 3,230 are within 25 km.
  - 13 are 100 km or more off. Aleutians West is 2,550 km off, because averaging longitudes across the date line breaks.

- **The dbt unique test on FIPS_CODE passes**, and it can't catch this. `48201`, `TX201` and `00201` are three different strings for one county.

**Hit means:** anything that reads a county name, state or EPA region off this table can be wrong, most often on the biggest counties.
**Miss means:** nothing downstream in live dbt reads it today (checked with grep: only its own model and a test list). The ledger auto-skips pairing it.
**Dull explanation, tested:** FRS has a few stray rows (0.07%), and `any_value()` doesn't vote. S13 shows the majority label is right 99.93% of the time. So `mode()`, or plain Census names, would fix it.
**Use instead:** `REFERENCE__CENSUS_CB_COUNTY` for name and state to FIPS. Get facility counts straight from FRS after mapping letter codes to digits.

## 2-5. The ITIS species list: four clean lookup tables, **skip**

**What it is:** ITIS, the federal master list of scientific names for plants, animals and microbes. TSN is the name's ID number.

**Checked** (S01-S03)

| Table | Rows | Distinct TSN | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TAXONOMIC_UNITS | 993,346 | 993,346 | 589,280 valid, 89,083 accepted, 221,458 invalid, 93,525 not accepted |
| LONGNAMES | 993,346 | 993,346 | Same TSN and same name as TAXONOMIC_UNITS on all 993,346. 982,920 distinct names, so 10,426 IDs share a name with another |
| HIERARCHY | 678,363 | 678,363 | Exactly the valid + accepted names (589,280 + 89,083). 7 roots, max depth 21, 574,465 dead-end branches |
| REFERENCE_LINKS | 1,970,107 | 993,339 | The built key is unique. 7 names have no link |

- **Orphans:** 0 TSNs in any child table are missing from TAXONOMIC_UNITS.
- **One load each:** a single `_SOURCE_RUN_ID` per table.

**Time check** (S04): new names per year, January to July only, so 2026 compares fairly. 2026 data ends 2026-07-28.

| Year | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| New names, Jan-Jul | 6,579 | 4,192 | 11,808 | 13,196 | 10,886 | 15,186 | 22,551 | **6,016** |
| Records updated, Jan-Jul | 10,817 | 15,519 | 25,471 | 15,797 | 15,461 | 23,066 | 40,888 | 24,632 |

- 2026 is down 73% from 2025 on new names. But 2019 and 2020 were as low, and updates are running at the 2024 pace.
- ITIS adds names in big batches, one group of species at a time (2012 put in 58,117 by July). No break worth a story.

**One join** (S11): FDA's substance registry (`HEALTH__FED_FDA_UNII_GSRS_SUBSTANCES`) is the only other table with a TSN.
- 168,046 FDA rows. 18,713 carry a TSN, 8,793 distinct.
- **All 8,793 land in ITIS (100%).** TSN is a real key.
- 337 (3.8%) point to names ITIS has since retired. Many are fish: `Psetta maxima` (turbot), `Pangasius hypophthalmus` (swai).
- Dull reason: scientists renamed the species and FDA's record kept the old ID. That's drift, not wrongdoing.

**Traps**
- TSN is an ID, not an amount. The glance summed it (1.3T) and took its median. Both numbers mean nothing. Same for PARENT_TSN.
- REFERENCE_LINKS: VERNACULAR_NAME is the text `None` on all 1,970,107 rows. CHANGE_TRACK_ID is 0 on all but 1.
- TAXONOMIC_UNITS: PARENT_TSN = 0 ("no parent") on 234,695 rows.
- LONGNAMES duplicates what's in TAXONOMIC_UNITS. Pick one.
