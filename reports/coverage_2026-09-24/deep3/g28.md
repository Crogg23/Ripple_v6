## 1. SEC 13F filing index: years of missing holdings reports, filed in one day. **live**

**The mechanic.** A money manager with $100M+ in US stocks must file a 13F within 45 days of each quarter-end.
Some managers skip it for years. Then they file every missing quarter on one day.
I call that a **burst**: 4+ original 13F reports from one manager on one filing day, each filed more than a year after its quarter ended.

**Checked**
- Profile (S04, pulled whole): 336,124 filings, 16,310 CIKs. No duplicate accession numbers.
  - Filing dates run 2013-05-20 to 2026-05-29. They're text, parsed as DD-MON-YYYY.
  - Original 13F-HR holdings reports go in a median 41 days after quarter-end. 6.0% go in after day 47.
- ⚠ **7 filing quarters are not loaded:** 2021 Q3-Q4, 2022 Q4 and all of 2023. deep2/deep-6 found this in the holdings table. The index has the same hole.
- **Controlling for the hole.** A late filing only counts if its on-time filing window *is* loaded, so an on-time original would show if one existed.
  - Checked all 4,118 burst filings: **0** have an earlier original for the same manager and quarter.
- **Time comparison, bursts with 4+ countable filings:**

| Filing era | Loaded quarters | Bursts | Per quarter | Filings | Managers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2013-2022 | 36 | 121 | **3.4** | 1,007 | 104 |
| 2024-May 2026 | 10 | 157 | **15.7** | 1,689 | 131 |

- By year: 2019 25, 2020 17, 2022 19, then **2024 66, 2025 55, 2026 36 (five months).**
- The jump shows up for US and foreign managers alike.
  - US: 2.9 bursts a quarter → 13.2
  - Foreign: 1.3 → 10.2
  - (Those two splits count all bursts, before the loaded-window filter.)
- **Join, holdings (S17):** summed each burst filing's holdings from FINANCE__FED_SEC_13F_HOLDINGS. The 2024+ filings are in dollars, not the old thousands.
  - 107 of the 131 managers filed holdings reports. Took each one's latest filed quarter.
  - Total **$207.5B**, median **$283M**, 25 over $1B.
- **Join, names (S16):** manager names come from the 13F cover-page table (FINANCE__FED_SEC_13F_FILERS), joined on accession number. 387 of 387 bursts landed.
- **Second check:** in 75 of the 183 managers with 2024+ bursts, the burst is the first filing that CIK has anywhere in the table.

## 2. SEC insider derivative trades: companies that report option awards months late, again and again. **live**

**The mechanic.** When a company gives an officer or director stock options, the insider must file a Form 4 within 2 business days.
This table has the trade date. The insider submission table has the filing date. The gap between them is the lateness.

**Checked**
- Profile (S10): 1,049,121 lines on 649,583 filings.
  - ⚠ The glance was wrong: `DERIV_TRANS_SK` is unique on every row, not repeated 2.1K times.
  - 7,553 lines repeat another line's content. They're small, left in.
  - Trade dates run from year 0023 to 2047. 5 are before 2000 and 25 are after today. Bounded every window.
  - Filings cover 2016-07-01 to 2025-03-31.
- Joined to FINANCE__FED_SEC_INSIDER_SUBMISSION on ACCESSION_NUMBER (S11). 1,030,368 of the 1,031,903 Form 4 lines land inside the 2014-2025 date bound. Owner comes from FINANCE__FED_SEC_INSIDER_REPORTINGOWNER.
- **First pass by line (S11) was wrong.** Line counts inflate the late share, for three reasons.
  - Security National Financial re-reports every outstanding option after each annual stock dividend, with the original award date. 2,446 award lines, median "lag" 1,387 days.
  - Marlin Business Services: 115 lines, 16 real awards, 0 late.
  - FlexShopper: 524 lines, 135 real awards.
- **Fix (S14, S20):**
  - Count each award once: company + owner + award date + expiration + security. The lag is its *first* filing.
  - Option awards only: code A with an exercise price above 0.
  - Awards dated 2017-01 to 2024-09 only.
  - Drop awards dated before the company's first insider filing, which are pre-IPO awards. That's 1,135 dropped.
  - Late = first filed 30+ days after the award. That's far past the 2-day rule, so it undercounts.
- **Result:** 150,950 option awards. **4,628 (3.1%)** first reported 30+ days late.
  - Only 40 of those 4,628 are marked "E" by the filer. E means the filer called it an early Form 5 item.
- **Peer:** 2,251 companies with 20+ awards.
  - Median company: **0%** late. The 90th percentile is 9.1%.
  - 32 companies are late on half or more (E-marked awards left out).
- **Time, out of sample:** took the companies that were 50%+ late in 2017-20 (10+ awards in both halves), then checked them in 2021-24.

| Group (2017-20) | Companies | 2021-24 awards | Late 30+ days | Rate | Median company |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Late on half or more | 23 | 637 | 129 | **20.3%** | 5.7% |
| Everyone else | 1,248 | 46,757 | 711 | **1.5%** | 0% |

- ⚠ **Six companies carry it: 99 of the 129.** Most of the 23 cleaned up. Only 4 stayed 50%+ late.
- By award year, the late rate falls from 3.0% (2017) to 1.8% (2024). 2024 is slightly short, because filings end 2025-03-31.

**The repeat-late companies** (data matches; lag = first filing minus award date):

| Company | Awards | 30+ days late | Worst lag | What the rows show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AgEagle Aerial Systems (UAVS) | 117 | 85 | 529 days | Batches: 22 awards first filed 2019-05-21, 23 on 2022-06-13. Directors' 2020-12-31 awards were filed 529 days later |
| Medicine Man Technologies / Schwazze (SHWZ) | 45 | 34 | 512 days | 16 awards first filed 2021-04-30. The CFO's and general counsel's 550,000-share options from 2019-12-05 were among them |
| theMaven (MVEN) | 59 | 37 (46 with E) | 1,173 days | Directors' 2017-18 options first filed Dec 2020-Feb 2021. From memory, not checked here: the company was behind on its other SEC filings then too |
| EVO Transportation & Energy (EVOA) | 33 | 29 | 1,597 days | 7 of 10 still late in 2021-24 |
| ESSA Pharma (EPIX) | 37 | 24 | 125 days | 2022-06-29 awards to directors and officers first filed 2022-11-01 |
| Scientific Industries (SCND) | 39 | 27 | 266 days | Still late on 11 of 18 in 2021-24 |
| Security National Financial (SNFCA) | 98 | 62 | 1,235 days | ⚠ Mostly the stock-dividend re-report artifact. Needs the filings read by hand |

- **Celsius Holdings dropped out.** It had 25 of 35 awards 30+ days late, but 22 of the 25 are E-marked. One unmarked row stands out: 300,000 options for CEO John Fieldly at $42.64, dated 2021-01-01, first filed 2024-01-05. That looks like a performance award reported when it vested. Not checked.
- **Hit means:** a company whose insiders keep reporting option awards months late. Late award reports are also where backdating can hide, because the price date is picked after the fact.
- **Miss means:** if the 2017-20 late group had fallen back to the 1.5% baseline, it was a one-off cleanup. Most of them did. Six didn't.
- **Boring explanation, partly tested:**
  - Re-reports and pre-IPO awards: ruled out by the dedupe.
  - "It was a Form 5 item": ruled out. Only 40 late awards claim it.
  - Performance awards reported when they vest: not tested. That could explain part of any company's list.
  - Tiny companies with thin legal staff: likely for most of the six. That's still a violation, and the SEC has fined late Section 16 filers.
  - This can't test backdating. There's no stock price table in the warehouse.
- **Next join:** the non-derivative insider trades table on the same companies and owners, to see if their stock sales are late too. Then EDGAR for SEC Section 16(a) orders.

## New data traps (for the trap file)

1. **The glance battery miscounts values on some tables.** Real figures:
   - FEC bulk's top committee name: 4 rows (glance said 154)
   - SEC derivative `DERIV_TRANS_SK`: unique (glance said 2.1K)
   - NCUA `RSSD`: unique (glance said 32)
2. **FINANCE__FED_FEC_BULK = POLITICS__FEC_COMMITTEE, cycle 2024.** Same 20,938 IDs. Blank treasurers are empty strings here.
3. **SEC derivative lines are not awards.** Stock-dividend re-reports (Security National Financial) and restated lines repeat one award many times with the original date. Dedupe on company + owner + award date + expiration + security before measuring lateness.
4. **SEC `TRANS_TIMELINESS` is never 'L' on an original Form 4.** 0 of 42,599 original Form 4 lines filed 6+ days late admit it, and 0 of 13,415 on amended Form 4s. 1,122 of the 1,232 'L' rows sit on Form 5 filings. Compute lateness from dates.
5. **The SEC derivative table's filing window is 2016-07-01 to 2025-03-31.** Trades from 2014-15 are all "late" by construction, and 2025 trades are cut off.
6. **The 13F index has a 1987-03-31 period on one row, and is missing 7 filing quarters** (2021 Q3-Q4, 2022 Q4, all of 2023, confirming deep-6). For any "late" 13F test, keep only quarters whose on-time window is loaded.
7. **Committee spending is not candidate support.** Only 2.1% of old campaign committees' 2024 spending shows up in the committee-to-candidate file.
8. **MSRB registrants is the dealer list only.** No standalone municipal advisors.
