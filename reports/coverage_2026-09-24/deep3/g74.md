## 1. UK Companies House: the day ID checks went live. **live**

**What the table is (S02)**
- 5,734,780 companies. One load, 2026-07-27. Formations run to 2026-06-30.
- ⚠ **Live companies only.** DISSOLUTION_DATE is empty on all 5.7M rows. Dead companies are gone, not flagged.
- So counts by formation month shrink with age. A fair count needs the dead ones back.

**The fix: company numbers (S22, S23, S24b)**
- England & Wales numbers are issued in order. Across 363 days, no day's number range overlaps the next day's.
- So a day's number range counts every company formed that day, dead or alive.
- The numbers missing from the live file were looked up in the PSC (owner) file, which keeps dissolved companies.
  - Six weeks to mid-November: 2,757 of the 2,891 missing numbers turn up there (95%). They're dissolved companies.
- "Real companies" below = live file + PSC-only.

**The break**

| Window | 2025-26: real companies a week | 2024-25: numbers a week |
|---|---|---|
| 6 weeks before mid-November | 15,790 | 14,006 |
| 5 weeks from mid-November | **11,158 (-29%)** | 12,809 (-9%; -6% without the near-empty 6-7 Dec 2024) |
| 4 weeks of January | 15,985 | 14,925 |

- Tue 18 Nov 2025: 847 companies. Tue 11 Nov: 3,156. Tue 4 Nov: 2,928.
- Same Tuesday in 2024 (19 Nov): 2,242 numbers, against 2,607 the Tuesday before. No cliff.
- Mon 17 Nov 2025: 4,371 numbers, about 360 above the Monday before. A small last-day rush.
- Against 2024's pattern, about **16,000-18,500 fewer companies** in the five weeks.
- January 2026 ran 7% above January 2025. The dip didn't last.
- Too big for a few agents to carry: the 15 biggest formation postcodes hold 14% of October's live formations (10,337 of 74,055). The drop is 29% of everything. The rest fell too: 63,718 live in October, 50,368 in November.

**⚠ The holes (S24b)**
- 18 Nov to 22 Dec 2025: **10,338 of 64,456 numbers (16.0%) match no company** in the live file or the PSC file.
- The six weeks before: 136 of 94,877 (0.1%). 23 Dec to 1 Feb: 481 of 77,785 (0.6%).
- They grow every day: 15 on 18 Nov, 400-635 a day by mid-December.
- They run at weekends too: 247 a day at weekends, 315 on weekdays. On December Saturdays more than half the numbers are holes.
- They stop dead on Tue 23 Dec.
- None got filled later. A late-finished company would show an old number with a new date. Zero overlaps.

**Hit means:** the checks stopped or stalled about 10,000 incorporations after a number was handed out. That's one question to Companies House: rejected, abandoned, or annulled?
**Miss means:** a numbering or publishing quirk in the free data. Still a finding: neither file counts formations right for those five weeks.
**Boring:** the rule working as designed. Friction on day one, normal by January. Companies House may have published this already. Press not checked. The go-live date is from Companies House's announcements, not re-checked here.

**Second lead: one Manchester address grew 2.65x after the checks (S17, S18, S20, S21)**
- Peer table: the 52 postcodes with 400+ new live companies across two windows.
  - Windows: 18 Nov 2024-30 Jun 2025 against 18 Nov 2025-30 Jun 2026.
  - Median growth 1.10x. Median share with a UK-resident controller: 68%.
- **M40 8WN, Initial Business Centre, Manchester: 943 → 2,498 (2.65x). 5.9% have a UK-resident controller.**
  - January 2026: 1,001 new companies there. That's 1.4% of all live UK formations that month.
- Inside it: three PSCs sharing one surname, Eltantawy. They're Egyptian or Turkish nationals living in Turkey, born 1992, 1996 and 2001.
  - They sit on 860 companies formed there in Dec 2025-Jan 2026. 716 of those came in January.
  - Nationally, those three names rank 2nd, 4th and 6th for companies formed since the checks began.
  - 1st: Ashraf Mohammed Abdou Awad Ibrahim, an Egyptian national living in the UAE. 400 companies, top postcode N1 7AA.
  - The one born in 2001 is probably sweep 2's "Egyptian national born 2001" (F-028, 631 companies). Same nationality and birth year. That write-up gives no name, so this isn't confirmed.
- **Dull explanation tested: a shelf-company seller.** Take the 884 companies formed there from late 2021 to early 2025 with that name as PSC. **743 (84%) have since passed to another controller.** That's the pattern of selling ready-made companies. It's legal.
- So it isn't a fraud story. The story: after the checks, which require verified directors and PSCs, three sellers sharing one surname stockpiled 860 companies at one address in December and January.
- Other peers, not drilled:
  - WV6 0SR, Wolverhampton: 133 → 642 (4.8x), 15% UK-resident.
  - N1 7AA, London: 298 → 536, 9.5% UK-resident.
  - E6 2JA, East Ham: 1,440 → 2,910, 22% UK-resident.

**Portfolio grade:** B+. The national break is clean, and the number-sequence trick beats the live-only trap. The holes are a sharp question for Companies House. The Manchester stockpile needs a phone call before it's a story.

## 2. Ireland's register: the 1990s shell-address boom and its end. **live (history)**

| Year | New companies | At mass addresses | Share |
|---|---|---|---|

| 1998 | 21,155 | 2,628 | 12.4% |

| 2001 | 14,375 | 173 | 1.2% |

- The biggest: 41 Central Chambers, Dame Court, Dublin 2.

  - 8,590 dissolved, 223 live.

- Not one agent: the top 4 hold 54% of the mass-address 1990s total. The median mass address took 951.

**Hit means:** the register records Ireland's 1990s trade in off-the-shelf companies sold abroad, and its end in 1999. That fits the 1999 laws that required an EEA-resident director and changed company tax residence. The law link isn't verified here.

**Boring:** formation agents and accountants, legal at the time. The history is known in outline. The per-address numbers may not be.
**Today:** no boom. 3.0% of 2025 formations went to mass addresses. The busiest new address is Venture Hub, 136 Capel Street, Dublin 1: 396 companies since April 2025.

**Portfolio grade:** C+ as news, B as a chart: "the year Ireland shut the shell shop."
