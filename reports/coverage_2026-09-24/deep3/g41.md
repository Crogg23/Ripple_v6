## 1. REFERENCE__FED_USGS_GNIS_ALL_NAMES: **live**

**What it is:** every name the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) has on file for a place: creeks, hills, lakes, towns.
- 1,249,624 rows. **981,698 official names**, one per feature. **267,926 variant names** (older or other names) on 165,024 features. [q05]
- One file, one load run, loaded 2026-08-08 (from `INGESTED_AT`, microseconds since 1970).
- **The file is current.** Mount McKinley is the official name again, with Denali as a 2015 variant. Gulf of America is official. Mount Blue Sky is official (record made 2023-09-25). [q09]
- No state, county or feature type column. More on that under "next join".

### What the glance missed
The glance ranked "who racks up the most CITATION". `CITATION` is one line of source text, not a count of anything. That angle is dead.
The glance's triage line, "slur place names still on federal records", was the real lead. So I tested it.

- **Spanish split:** 50 of the 488 "Negro" names are Spanish *negro* = black: Cerro Negro, Ojo Negro, Arroyo Negro, Toro Negro, the surname Negrón. I removed them. That leaves 438.
- One borderline case, "Negro Arroyo", sits in the Spanish pile. So 438 is the low figure.

### The chain: the 1963 swap is still on the map [q13, local]
- **225** features have the n-word as a variant name. None has it as the official name.
- **105** of those 225 carry **"Negro"** in their official name today. The other 120 got some other name.
- Background (outside knowledge, not checked here): in 1963 Interior ordered the n-word replaced with "Negro" on federal maps. That matches this pattern.
- **105 is a floor.** 309 of the 438 English-use "Negro" names have no variant on file at all, so we can't tell which ones were the n-word first.
- Five rows eyeballed:
  - `737583` official "Negro Wool Hollow (not official)" ← variant "N----- Wool Hollow", 1944 Missouri place-names book. Yes, the official name field says "(not official)".
  - `958584` Negro Marsh ← variant 1950 map. `1124635` Negro Rock ← 1981. `2681563` Negro Creek ← 1950. `1570145` Negro Lakes ← 1981.

### Time: new "Negro" records after 2000 [q13, local]
The English-use "Negro" names aren't all old. **19 official records were created after 2000.** The newest ones:
- `2804828` **Negrohead Mountain**: record created 2019-10-21, from a Board on Geographic Names revision dated 2019. Its variant is "N-----head Mountain", from a 1954 USGS map. So a federal record made in 2019 uses the 1963 substitute.
- `2813622` Negro Mountain: created 2020-09-19, from an 1861 Coast Survey map of Loudoun County, Va.
- `2813581` Estate Negro Bay: created 2020-09-14, a Census Bureau name.
- 336 of the 438 cite the 1976-1981 first digital compilation. 372 of 438 cite USGS itself.

### Peer: one term got a federal order, the other didn't [q13, local]
Renamings dated by the new official name's citation year.

| Citation year | "Squaw" features renamed | "Negro"/n-word features renamed |
|---|---|---|
| 2000 | 191 | 39 |
| 2010-2019 | 93 | 19 |
| 2020 | 1 | 9 |
| 2021 | 9 | **22** |
| 2022 | **625** | 6 |
| 2023 | 6 | 4 |
| **2020-2023 total** | **641** | **41** |

- The 625 are all Board on Geographic Names citations: 609 on **2022-09-08**, 9 on 2022-11-10, 7 on 2022-12-08.
- The 22 in 2021 are mostly one batch on **2021-06-10**. That day, 17 features got names like Henry Flipper Hill, Bill Pickett Hill and Buffalo Soldier Creek.
- So: "Squaw" got a national order. 1,004 of the 1,008 features with a "Squaw" name on file now have another official name, and 17 official "Squaw" names are left.
- "Negro" never got an order. Since 2010 it has never cleared more than 22 features in a year, and 438 are left.
- 1981 and 2000 are bulk citation dates (the first compilation and its first revision pass), not dated rename actions. So the fair comparison is 2020-2023: 641 vs 41.

### The 17 "Squaw" names left, taken apart [q09, q13]
- **9 are "(historical)"**: features that no longer exist, like Squaw Beach (historical).
- **4 are Census Bureau civil names**, which local governments set: City of Squaw Lake, Township of Squaw Grove, Township of Squaw Creek, Squaw Valley Census Designated Place.
  - Township of Squaw Creek (`468747`): **record created 2023-09-29**, after the order.
- **4 are physical features:** Squaw Lick (`1074443`), Squaw Lake (`658441`), Squaw Lake (`856982`), Squaw With a Basket (`857000`). Two of them (Squaw Lick, Squaw Lake `856982`) have no citation at all.

### The dull explanation, and whether it was tested
- **"'Negro' isn't a banned term, so of course it's still there."** True. That's the story, not a rebuttal. Outside knowledge, not checked here: the 2021 Interior order named only "Squaw", and other renamings need a local proposal to the Board. The table shows what that produced: 41 renamings in four years. *Tested with the time table above.*
- **"Most of these are Spanish."** Tested. 50 of 488 are, and they're removed.
- **"They're dead features."** Tested. 12 of 438 are "(historical)". 426 are current.
- **"The file is stale."** Tested. It carries the 2023 and 2025 renamings.
- Not tested: whether anyone has *proposed* renaming these. The Board's proposal docket isn't in the warehouse.

### What a hit means / what a miss means
- **Hit:** the feature's *official* federal name today, as of the 2026-08-08 load, contains the term.
- **Miss on the official name:** renamed, or never had it.
- **Miss on the variant:** no older name is on file. It does *not* mean the feature never carried the n-word. That's why 105 is a floor.

### Traps found
- **`DATE_CREATED` on variant rows is a copy of `PUBLICATION_DATE`.** It's equal on all 45,859 variant rows dated 1990 or later [q07], and on all 1,760 dated variant rows in the q13 extract, 1,382 of them pre-1990. Overall, both columns have the same min, max and null count (127,219) [q05]. It isn't a creation date.
- **Year-only dates are stored as Dec 31.** 803,140 of 989,023 non-null `PUBLICATION_DATE` values end in 12-31. The minimum is 1700-12-31. [q05]
- **The newest renamings have blank citations.** Mount McKinley (`1414314`) and Gulf of America (`558730`) are official with no source, date or citation. 133,382 official rows have no publication date.
- **An official name that says "(not official)"**: `737583`.
- **"Squaw" as a substring catches "Squawk" and "Squawkeag".** 3 false hits before the fix.
- **Landing has 1,249,630 rows, the mart 1,249,624.** 6 rows dropped between them. One feature has no official row. One official row has a blank name: distinct (feature, name) pairs come to 1 less than the row count.
- **`CITATION` isn't a harm column.** It's text.

## 2. ENVIRONMENT__XC_OWID_FOSSIL_SHARE: **probed**

**What it is:** fossil fuels' percent of each country's primary energy, 1965-2024, from Our World in Data (sourced from the Energy Institute).
- 6,379 rows, 112 entities: **79 countries** with ISO codes, 12 Our World in Data aggregates, 21 Energy Institute regions with a blank code. [q03]
- No duplicate (entity, year). `FOSSIL_FUELS` is stored as text.
- Only 79 countries: the Energy Institute lists big energy users one by one. Most of Africa shows up only inside regions.

### Peer: 10-year change, 2014→2024 [q03, local]
- World: 86.0% → 81.3% (**-4.7 points**). OECD: -5.2. Non-OECD: -5.2.
- Median of the 79 countries: **-4.4**. **6 of 79 went up.**

| Went up | 2014 | 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iceland | 17.0 | 19.5 | +2.5 |
| Philippines | 85.2 | 87.2 | +2.0 |
| **Canada** | 66.1 | 67.4 | **+1.3** |
| Taiwan | 90.2 | 91.4 | +1.2 |
| Iraq | 98.5 | 99.4 | +1.0 |
| Vietnam | 77.8 | 78.5 | +0.7 |

- **G7 peers, same years:** US -4.4, Germany -4.5, France -3.8, Italy -2.4, Japan -10.4, UK -10.8. **Canada +1.3, the only riser.**
- Canada's whole rise is 2023-2024: 65.0 (2022) → 66.5 → 67.4.
- Biggest drops: Finland -20.6 (to 36.8%), Denmark -16.4, Lithuania -15.5, Netherlands -14.4.

### Time: breaks
Year-over-year jumps over 6 points are real-world events, not data breaks:
- Lithuania **+30.1** in 2010: its nuclear plant closed at the end of 2009 (outside knowledge).
- Japan +6.7 in 2012: after Fukushima (outside knowledge).

### The dull explanation
- Canada: drought cut hydro output in 2023-24 (outside knowledge). **Not tested.** This table only has the fossil share, not hydro.
- Whatever the cause, this is a public chart on Our World in Data. It's context, not a scoop.

### Traps found
- **Regions are double-listed.** "Africa" = "Africa (EI)" exactly, all 60 years. "USSR" = "CIS (EI)" exactly, 1965-1984. Summing entities double-counts.
- **"100" is real, early-year and fossil-only.** 365 rows across 12 countries, the last in 2012 (Kuwait, Oman). The glance's counts ("100" on 373 rows, 81.25719 on 45) don't reproduce: 81.25719 is World 2024 and appears once.
- USSR stops in 1984.
