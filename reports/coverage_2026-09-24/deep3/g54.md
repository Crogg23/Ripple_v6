## 1. ECONOMICS__FED_IRS_SOI_CHARITIES: **live**

**What it really is:** the IRS exempt-org master file's **international region file**, not an "SOI" sample.

### The chain
1. Split the national IRS list into foreign (the 1,359 in this file) and US.
2. Compare 501(c)(4) "social welfare" groups against (c)(3) private foundations and public charities, both sides [c1, b4].
3. Time: IRS ruling year, groups with $100M+ in assets [b4].
4. Money join: SEC 13F holdings by name + city [c2]. 990 e-file index by EIN [b5].

### 990 filings [b5]

### The dull explanation
- **It's legal, and maybe routine.** A foreign group can't give US donors a deduction anyway, so (c)(4) costs it nothing.
- Outside knowledge, **not verified**: a foreign (c)(3) private foundation pays a 4% tax on US investment income (IRC 4948) and follows private-foundation rules. A (c)(4) does neither. That would explain the choice.
- For Canada, the US-Canada tax treaty may already exempt the Mastercard Foundation's dividends. If so, (c)(4) status changes little for it. Not tested. It needs a tax lawyer.
- Intergovernmental bodies (Arab Fund) and sports federations (FIFA 1994, IOC 1992, both ahead of US-hosted events) may have their own reasons.

**Hit means:** foreign endowments with tens of billions in US stock sit on the IRS list as US "social welfare" groups. They got 3 of every 4 new big-(c)(4) dollars since 2019. If the status shields US investment income, that's a tax story with named entities and public 990s to read.
**Miss means:** even if it's tax-neutral, this is a trap for every "dark money" or (c)(4) story. **Add up (c)(4) assets from the IRS list and 40% are foreign endowments**, not US political nonprofits.
**Next join:** the 990 XML for these EINs (US-source income, Schedule R); SEC 13D/G filings; the 1,359 foreign rows against FARA registrants.

- For foreign rows, **CITY holds the country** ("CANADA" 455, "UNITED KINGDOM" 114, "AFGHANISTAN" 75 for military chapters). ZIP is 00000-0000 on all 1,359.
- 32 rows have CITY "PUERTO RICO" and no state: PR groups filed in the international file.
- **INCOME_AMT is gross, not net.** Novo Holdings shows $297.0B income on $36.2B assets and $3.5B revenue. The glance's $417.1B income total is meaningless.
- Subsection 04 isn't the IRS's default code for foreign groups: 136 foreign (c)(3) private foundations sit beside the 35 (c)(4)s.

### Peer and time: new charities [b7]

## 4. JUSTICE__XC_OWID_NUCLEAR_WARHEADS: **probed**

**What it is:** Our World in Data, from the Federation of American Scientists. 11 entities × 82 years (1945-2026) = 902 rows. All values parse as numbers [a1].

### Time
- China: 260 (2016) → 350 (2020) → 500 (2024) → **620 (2026)**, +138% in ten years. India 120 → 190. North Korea 5 → 60.
- The world total fell 15,228 → 12,187 in the same years. Russia 4,400 and the US 3,700 hold 83% of the country stockpiles in 2026.
- **Why probed:** FAS, SIPRI and the Pentagon publish these numbers every year. Nothing new in them.

### Trap: "World" is a different measure
- World ≠ sum of countries in **all 82 years**.
- 2025: countries sum to 9,614, World says 12,241. 2026: 9,745 vs 12,187.
- 9,614 matches FAS's 2025 *military stockpile* exactly, and 12,241 its *total inventory*, which counts retired warheads awaiting dismantlement (outside knowledge; the sum match is checked).
- Never divide a country by the World row as "share of the world."
- South Africa is zero from 1990 on (nonzero 1982-1989, peak 6). It's still a row every year.

## 5. ECONOMICS__INTL_IT_ISTAT: **dead**

**What it is:** 213,284 values from 4 Istat datasets. **Farm only** [a5, d1]:
- `101_1015` crops by region: 186,191 rows, 51,353 series (hectares, quintals, production).
- `101_1033` livestock slaughter: 13,357 annual + 10,460 monthly rows.
- `101_1030` protected-origin food producers: 3,276 rows, 2020-2022 only.
- The catalog summary ("economy, jobs, trade, population") is wrong.

### Why dead
- COUNTRY = 'IT' on every row. No key joins to any US table.
- Codes like `ITC49`, `TP_HECT_EXT` need Istat's code lists, which aren't loaded.
- Nothing to rank for a US story.

### Traps
- **Units are mixed** (hectares, quintals, heads, kg, percent), and UNIT_MEASURE, UNIT_MULT and UNIT_MULTIPLIER_VALUE are empty on all rows. So the glance's "sum 152.7B" and "top 1% hold 87%" mean nothing.
- IS_NORMAL_VALUE is false on every row.
- IS_MISSING_VALUE is true on 244 rows, all in `101_1030`. That dataset also has 244 null values and 244 rows with OBS_STATUS c or u.
- `_SOURCE_RUN_ID` and `ISTAT_OBS_ID` are distinct on every row (the per-row uuid trap).
- 1,119 negative values are year-over-year % changes (SOWCROP, *_YCHA), which is fine. But `OBS_VALUE_ABSOLUTE` drops their sign.
- The monthly slaughter series stops at January 2023. No duplicate series-dates.
