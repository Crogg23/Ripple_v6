1984-04-09 | unknown | 1.0 | scud-b | tonghae | unknown | unknown | unknown | 90 | success | First known test by North Korea of a missile with a range greater than or equal to 300 km and a payload of 500 kg or greater. The 1984 tests probably took place over the course of several months rather than on the same days as they are entered here. What we know though is that North Korea carried out at least 6 tests. We also know that the tests were carried out at Tonghae. We also know that 3 of the tests succeeded and that at least 3 failed. We do not know exactly which ones were failures and which ones were successes. We also do not know exactly when these tests took place except that it was between April and September of 1984. Readers should interpret the information about these first tests as our best estimates as to what occurred in a secret program within one of the most secret regimes in the world.
