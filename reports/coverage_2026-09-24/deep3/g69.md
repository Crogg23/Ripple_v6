## 1. IMMIGRATION__FED_EOIR_CASE_DATA: **live**

### What it really is [q02, q06, q13]
- 12,631,225 rows. Each row is one tab-separated line of EOIR's case file, `A_TblCase`: 39 fields crammed into the one column called `CASE_TYPE`.
- 12,631,125 rows split cleanly into 39 fields. 100 rows have a stray tab in the city name, which shifts every later field. Filter on `regexp_count(CASE_TYPE,'\t') = 38`.
- Every row is distinct and the case ID (field 1, IDNCASE) is unique.
  - **Trap:** the glance said the top values repeat "20.9K" times. That was a rounding error in the approximate top-values function (`APPROX_TOP_K`). No row repeats.
- The data runs to **2026-05-31**: the latest detention date, lawyer-notice date and address change are all that day.
- A parsed twin exists: `IMMIGRATION__FED_EOIR_CASES`, built 2026-09-07. It **dropped `E_28_DATE`**, the date a lawyer filed a notice to represent the person. That field is only in this raw table (and in staging).

Fields used here (field number in the tab line):

| # | Field | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | IDNCASE | case number. It counts up over time, which gives each case a rough opening date |
| 4 | ZIP | address on file. For a detained person, it's usually the jail's ZIP |
| 7 | NAT | nationality, EOIR's two-letter codes (HO = Honduras, NU = Nicaragua, ES = El Salvador) |
| 9 | CUSTODY | D detained, R released, N never detained |
| 11 | E_28_DATE | date the lawyer's notice was filed |
| 14 | court | court code (MIA, NYC, ...) |
| 15 | LATEST_HEARING | most recent or next hearing, runs to 2031 |
| 24 | DATE_OF_ENTRY | date the person entered the US |
| 32 | DATE_DETAINED | detention date |

### How "old case" is measured [q08]
- The case number counts up. For each quarter, I took the median case number of people detained within 30 days of crossing. Those are brand-new cases, so the median shows the number being handed out that quarter.
- The median rises every quarter, 2012 to 2026, with no step backwards. The calendar holds.
- **Old case** = the person's case number is below the median handed out **8 quarters (2 years) before** the quarter they were detained.

### The dull explanations, tested

| Dull explanation | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Border crossings collapsed, so the detained are older cases by default | Compare counts and rates, not shares | Counts are up 12x and the rate per 1,000 is up 8.6x. Shares alone would hide that |
| EOIR changed how it records detention dates in 2025 | Independent source: ICE detention stays (`FED_ICE_DETENTION_STINTS`), no row join, month totals only [q11] | Stays where the person entered the US 2+ years before book-in: **Jan 2024 1,438 → Jan 2026 11,617 (8.1x)**. Total detained, Jan+Feb: EOIR 1.69x, ICE 1.56x. The two sources move together |
| A one-time data dump stamped the dates | Spread by day [q10b] | Old-case detentions land on 28-31 days of every month. The busiest day in Jan 2026 holds 258 of 4,840 (5%) |
| These are old deportation orders being carried out, not open cases | Hearings after detention [q09] | **Can't be fully tested.** Decisions aren't in this file. 10,083 (54%) have a hearing dated on or after the detention day (held or scheduled), and 5,325 still have one set after May 2026. The other 8,484 have no later hearing; some may be old removal orders |

### Where they're held [q15, q16]
- Join: ZIP on file → ICE's detention-facility code list (`FED_ICE_DETENTION_FACILITY_CODES`), 5-digit ZIP.
- **Land rate: 10,266 of 11,121 (92.3%)** people in the 2026 group (cases opened ~2022-2024, still marked D) sit at a ZIP that holds an ICE facility.
- Top: Stewart Detention Center, GA 31815 (492); Diamondback Corr Facility, OK 73772 (424); North Lake Correctional, MI 49304 (351); Moshannon Valley, PA 16866 (349); Folkston IPC, GA 31537 (338).
- Eyeball, 6 rows [q13]: a case opened in the New York (Broadway) court in 2023, with a lawyer since October 2023, detained 2026-01-03, now at an Encinal TX address. The address-change date falls within 2 days of the detention date on 5 of 6 rows.
- Lawyer notice before detention, by facility: 19% (Baker, FL) to 39% (Montgomery, TX). Median of the top 15 is 25%. Not a story on its own.

### Second line, same table [q07]
People detained Jan-May whose entry date was 10+ years before detention: **2019: 3,075 · 2024: 1,396 · 2026: 5,753.**
Entry date is filled on only 39% of 2026 detention cases (50% in 2024), so this count is a floor.

### Traps (for the ledger, not the story)
- **The catalog summary is wrong.** Court, custody, hearing, entry and detention dates are all there. Only judge and decision are missing.
- **`E_28_DATE` (lawyer notice) is only in this raw table.** The parsed `FED_EOIR_CASES` dropped it.
- **CUSTODY 'D' sticks.** 6,370 of 11,333 people detained in Jan 2018 still read D in 2026 [q07]. D means "last known detained," not "held now."
- **LATEST_HEARING gets overwritten.** Among old cases, a hearing on the same day as the detention shows up at most 10 times a month [q10b], so arrests at the courthouse can't be seen here.
- Address ZIP holds `00000` and `99999` sentinels: 1.7% and 0.5% of a 2% sample [q06].
- `C_RELEASE_DATE` runs to 2100-12-31, a sentinel.
- `UP_BOND_DATE`, `UP_BOND_RSN`, `DETENTION_DATE` and `DETENTION_FACILITY_TYPE` are empty on all 253,029 rows of the 2% sample [q06].

## 2. IMMIGRATION__XC_OWID_REFUGEES: probed

[q14 whole table, analyzed in `g69/owid.py`]
- 7,442 country-year rows, 1951-2024. 211 countries with ISO codes, plus 4 without: Unknown Origin, Tibetan, Serbia and Kosovo, Stateless.
- The counts are stored as text, but all 7,442 parse as numbers. There's no duplicate country-year.
- **No World row.** Add it up yourself: **30.69M in 2024** (20.12M in 2019).
- **Concentration:** Syria 19.2%, Afghanistan 18.6%, Ukraine 16.5%, South Sudan 7.4%, Sudan 6.8%. The top 5 hold **68.6%**.
- **Time:** the biggest rise from 2019 to 2024 is Ukraine (+5.06M), then Afghanistan (+3.04M) and Sudan (+1.36M).
- **Peer, within OWID region** (countries with 10K+ refugees in 2019, ratio of 2024 to 2019):
  - North America (median 2.68x): Haiti 5.55x, Honduras 3.83x.
  - Asia (median 1.23x): Azerbaijan 13.2x, India 3.3x.
  - Africa (median 1.09x): Burkina Faso 12.8x.
- **Trap: Vietnam 316,728 (2020) → 14,630 (2021), down 95% in one year.** No return event of that size shows in the table, so it looks like a relabel. Check UNHCR's notes before using Vietnam's series.
- **Trap: Azerbaijan swings:** 11,626 (2019) → 101,468 (2020) → 39,478 (2021) → 158,098 (2023). Don't trend it.
- **Trap (outside knowledge, not verified in the warehouse):** UNHCR counts most displaced Venezuelans in a separate category, so this table's Venezuela figure (370,169) is far below the true displacement.
- No join key to EOIR: EOIR uses its own two-letter country codes, OWID uses ISO3. Mapping them would take a hand-built crosswalk.
- Usable as world context. Nothing story-grade on its own.
