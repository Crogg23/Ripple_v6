- The table mixes 3 kinds of rows:
  - 187 entities with an ISO code (8,999 rows)
  - 15 with `OWID_` codes (570 rows): World, continents, USSR, Czechoslovakia, Kosovo and others

- **Peer, inside Africa (52 countries), 2012-14 average → 2018-20 average:** the median country moved 0.
  - The whole rise sits in 7 countries: DR Congo +729 a year, Mali +554, Burkina Faso +533 (from 0), Ethiopia +462, Mozambique +373, Niger +257, Chad +180.
  - The 8th, Burundi, is +25.
  - Worldwide only Afghanistan rose more (+5,169).
- **Dull explanation:** this is the Global Terrorism Index's own headline. It's public and old (the table ends in 2021).

**Traps (for the ledger)**
- **No 1993.** The table has 51 years for 1970-2021, and 1993 is the missing one. The source database (GTD) lost that year; that's from memory, check it. A line chart will bridge it and a sum will skip it.
- **2021 looks like a series break, not a decline.**
  - World: 22,832 → 11,528 (-50%).
  - Of the 23 countries with 50+ deaths in 2020, 22 fell. The median change was -48%.
  - In every other year since 2000, at most 71% of countries fell.
  - Without Afghanistan (the Taliban took power in Aug 2021, from memory) the world still fell 44%: 12,766 → 7,208.
  - Don't report a 2021 drop from this table without the source's notes.
- **Zero-filled dead countries.** Rhodesia has 0-death rows every year 1981-2021. South Yemen and New Hebrides are 0 in all 51 rows. Those zeros are filler, not counts. 6,901 of the 10,481 rows are 0.
- **Real places with a blank code:** Republic of the Congo (182 deaths), Macedonia (49), West Bank and Gaza Strip (1,680). An ISO-code filter drops them.
- **Overlaps:** Germany and West Germany both have 1990. Serbia and Montenegro (2003-21) overlaps Serbia (2006-21). The countries add up to more than World in 44 of 51 years, by up to 243 (1996).

- **Dull explanation:** Ecuador's and Haiti's surges and El Salvador's drop were front-page news. There's nothing new here.

**The join: terrorism deaths × homicide coverage (by ISO code and year, 2015-20)**
- 12 of the 20 countries with the most terrorism deaths have **no homicide rate for any year** 2015-20:
  - Iraq, Syria, Yemen, Somalia, DR Congo, Mali, Libya, Ethiopia, Niger, Burkina Faso, South Sudan, Central African Republic.
- By contrast, 73% of countries with zero terrorism deaths do have homicide data.
- **Meaning:** any "most violent countries" ranking from this table leaves out the war zones. The absence tracks conflict, not safety.

**Traps (for the ledger)**
- The data thins at the end: 137 countries in 2020, 112 in 2022, 90 in 2023, 16 in 2024. A "latest year" ranking compares different years and a shrinking set of countries.
- Places inside places: United Kingdom sits next to England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Iraq sits next to "Iraq (Central Iraq)" and "Iraq (Kurdistan Region)". 21 UN regions carry a blank code.
- The battery's "6.7583313 ×36" is fake. The value appears once (Zimbabwe 2022). That's the APPROX_TOP_K count problem g09/g22 found.
- The zeros are real: 143 rows in tiny places (Liechtenstein, San Marino, Saint Helena, Vatican).
