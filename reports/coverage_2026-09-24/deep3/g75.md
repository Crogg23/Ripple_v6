- 24,368 carry a US country code. 16,631 of those are Appleby (Paradise Papers).
- The address rows link to people and companies through the relationships table (S05):
  - 483,764 `registered_address` edges from officers
  - 336,951 from entities
  - 10,496 from intermediaries

**The join**
- S10: every person or company tied to a US address, plus US-coded officers with no address. 47,362 rows.
- After dropping company-like names: 27,761 person nodes, 25,592 distinct first+last names.
- Matched on first + last name against:
  - the OIG exclusion list (LEIE, S11)
  - SAM exclusions (S12)

- **With a second field agreeing** (street, ZIP or city from the ICIJ address), 4 people are left. FHFA: 0. FDIC: 0.

## 2. HUD subsidized projects: empty public housing HUD doesn't excuse. **live**

**What the table is**
- One quarter only: every row says 31DEC2025. So there's no time series inside it.
- 35,601 rows. Programs: Section 8 17,075, public housing 12,094, 202/PRAC 2,783, 811/PRAC 2,933, 811 PRA 679, S236/BMIR 37.

**Traps (checked, S07 locally)**
- **Every public housing project is listed twice.** 6,047 projects show up once with a blank sub-program and once as "PH, MTW" or "PH, non-MTW".
  - The numbers are identical on both rows. That doubles public housing in any sum.
  - The battery's 3.2M units counted 848,338 public housing units twice.
  - Deduped, the table has 29,554 rows.
- ZIP lost leading zeros on 4,880 rows (4,045 four-digit, 835 three-digit), mostly MA, NJ, PR, CT, RI. The same trap as ATF.
- 811 PRA: all 679 rows are suppressed codes, and units read 0.
- MONTHS_WAITING exists only for public housing. Section 8 and PRAC are 100% negative codes.
- MONTHS_WAITING averages only the year's move-ins. At Stanley Holmes Village it reads 266 months with a 1% move-in rate, so it rests on 1-2 households.
- 98 Section 8/PRAC rows show PCT_OCCUPIED over 100. The max is 1,334.
- For public housing, SPENDING_PER_MONTH is authority-wide: every Alaska AK001 project reads 1,089.
- FEDHSE is empty. GSL and SUMLEVEL are constants.

**Joins (checked)**
- Section 8/PRAC projects to Section 8 contracts on the first token of CODE, the property ID: **23,384 of 23,507 land (99.5%)**.
- Public housing to the housing-authority file (S08) on the first 5 characters of CODE: all 2,590 authorities land.

**The finding**
- Nationally: 65,488 of 830,848 public housing units are empty (7.9%). The median authority is at 3.9%.
  - NYCHA alone holds 12,093 of the empty units (18.5%).
- The housing-authority file splits empty units into "regular" vacancies and units HUD has excused (modernization, demolition and so on).
- **Peer group:** 436 non-MTW authorities with 300+ units, ranked against others of the same HUD size code.
  - Median regular vacancy: 3.2%.
  - 12 authorities run at 5x or more of their size median.
  - 6 of those also show a move-in-weighted wait 1.5x or more above their peers, on at least 10 move-ins.

**Hit means:** hundreds of units that HUD counts as ready to rent sit empty while applicants wait years.

**Boring, and tested:**

- Known context, from memory (check it): Atlantic City's authority has had state and HUD scrutiny in recent years. The ranking against peers is the new part.
