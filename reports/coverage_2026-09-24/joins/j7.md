# One company, two states: Grain Processing Corp in Iowa and Indiana (plus Nebraska's 15 open cases)

2026-09-24 · agent j7 · Python door · **23 statements** (one of them a compile error that was re-run; all listed in `j7.sql`, tagged [S1]-[S22]) · raw results in `j7/*.json`
Every company and plant named here is **a data match, not verified against primary records.**

---

**The one-line story:** Grain Processing Corp's corn mill in Muscatine, Iowa failed smokestack tests on 49 days from 2015 to 2025, more than any other big corn mill in the US. Iowa logged 36 violations there, called none of them "high priority," and filed no formal action. The same company's Indiana plant (same parent in 3 federal files) failed on 20 days, drew 21 high-priority flags and 7 state orders, and paid $687K. All 8 Iowa corn mills together drew 0 formal actions in those 11 years. Indiana's 6 drew 25. Meanwhile USDA sent the Muscatine plant $5.09M in COVID biofuel relief in 2022-23.

## Chain

### 1. Is the Indiana plant really the same company? Yes, three ways
- **Checked:** parent fields in three unrelated federal files [S4][S9].
  - GHGRP: "KENT CORP (100%)" on both plants, 2015-2023.
  - TRI: parent D&B number 005269923 on both.
  - OSHA ITA: EIN 421321075 on both (and on GPC's small Grinnell and Oskaloosa, IA sites).
- **Hit means:** the sister-plant contrast is one company under two regulators. The skeptic's open question ("confirm the Indiana plant is the same company") is closed on the data side.
- **Miss would have meant:** two unrelated firms with the same name. Ruled out.

### 2. Stack-test fails and enforcement, side by side [S5]
- **Checked:** every ICIS-Air stack test, violation, informal and formal action for both plants, by year. Fails are counted as **distinct fail days**, not rows (one row is one unit × pollutant).
- 2015-2025:

| | Muscatine IA | Washington IN |
|---|---|---|
| Test days | 147 | 116 |
| Fail days | **49** (33% of test days) | 20 (17%) |
| Violations logged | 36 | 26 |
| Flagged high priority (HPV) | **0** | 21 |
| Informal actions (letters, NOVs) | 40 | 10 |
| Formal actions | **0** | **7** |
| Penalties | $0 | **$687,030** |

- Iowa's last formal air action at Muscatine: 2007 (civil judicial). Three total, 2005-2007, $10,000.
- Indiana added an 8th order on 2026-06-02 for $309,998.
- Iowa flagged Muscatine's first HPV since 2010 with a 2026 day-zero date. One row; not followed further.
- **Hit means:** the gap is not in how often the plant fails. It is in the label. Iowa logs each failure as an ordinary violation, not "high priority." EPA's HPV policy is what pushes a state toward a formal order. Indiana labels most failures HPV and files orders.
- **Miss would have meant:** Iowa filed orders under another program. Checked below. It didn't.

### 3. ECHO says Muscatine had a formal action in 2013. Whose? [S6][S7][S8]
- **Checked:** ECHO `DATE_LAST_FORMAL_ACTION` = 2013-04-10, $129,000. I traced it through NPDES formal actions and EPA's own case file.
- It is **EPA, under the Clean Water Act**: two compliance orders on 2011-07-29 and a $129,000 Class II penalty order on 2013-04-10 (EPA cases 07-2011-0215, 07-2011-0314, 07-2013-0083).
- RCRA: only EPA warning letters (1999, 2007).
- **Hit means:** Iowa's last formal action at this plant in **any** EPA-tracked program is the 2007 air case. The only agency to file an order since then is EPA.
- Water side since 2015: 6 quarters in significant noncompliance (2015-Q1 to 2016-Q2), 48 effluent-limit exceedances, 19 of them in 2023. One warning letter, no state order [S22].

- **Hit means:** the story is bigger than one plant. Iowa labels almost nothing HPV (2 of 67 violations; Indiana 42 of 71) and files no orders against corn mills.
- **Miss would have meant:** Muscatine as a one-off. It isn't.

### 5. What Muscatine puts in the air (TRI, NEI, GHGRP) [S3][S17][S19]
- **Trap found:** Muscatine's NEI (EIS) data sits on a **second FRS registry ID, 110072214456**, not the ICIS-Air one (110017404548). A registry join from ICIS-Air finds no NEI rows at all [S18].

- **SO2 at Muscatine fell 98.7%** between NEI 2014 (26.1M lb) and NEI 2017 (345K lb). HCl fell from 692,840 lb to 594. Lead fell from 6,010 lb to 3. Coal burning looks like it stopped between 2014 and 2017. The warehouse does not say why.
- GHGRP 2023: 470,875 t CO2 at Muscatine, 235,001 t at Washington (2x).
- TRI air: 159K-190K lb a year in 2016-2022, mostly acetaldehyde (87K-115K lb). Then **60,411 lb in 2023**. Formaldehyde, ammonia, acrolein and H2S all read 0 in 2023-24.
- In TRI 2023 Muscatine ranks 12th of 33 wet corn mills on air pounds. At its 2016-22 level it would rank about 6th.
- **Hit means:** the plant that fails most is also the bigger emitter: 2x the CO2 of its sister and 5-6x the VOC and NOx.
- **Weak spot:** the 2023 TRI drop may be a reporting change. Four chemicals going to exactly 0 in one year looks like a form change, not a new control.

### 8. The Nebraska 15 [S1][S14][S20]
- **Checked:** today's 15 Nebraska operating majors with "Unaddressed-State" HPV status.
- 23 open HPVs. Oldest day zero 2020-10-29 (Lincoln Premium Poultry, ADM Columbus). ADM Columbus alone has 6.
- Open HPV pollutants: VOC, PM10, NOx, SO2, styrene (A-1 Fiberglass) and hexachlorobenzene (Ash Grove Cement).
- **0 formal actions since 2015 at all 15, from any agency, in ICIS-Air.** 60 informal actions. 31 stack-test fail days.
- Other programs: the only post-2015 formal actions at these 15 are EPA's (an NPDES order at Ash Grove in 2018; EPA case 07-2017-0403 at AGP Hastings). The ECHO 2015 date at ADM Columbus was not traced to a case.
- Emissions trend (TRI air, the 15 combined): 789,873 lb in 2015, then 1,152,541 lb in 2024 (+46%). The biggest drivers:
  - AGP Soy Hastings hexane: 364K lb in 2015, peak 871K in 2022.

- **Peer check kills the trend angle:** Nebraska's unaddressed ethanol plants rose 38% in TRI air 2015-2024. Iowa's 30 ethanol majors rose 43%. Other states' 67 rose 7%. **Nebraska's rise is not different from Iowa's.** Don't pitch "emissions rose while nobody enforced."

## Weak points a hostile editor would hit

- **"Iowa just doesn't key its orders into ICIS."** Possible. Iowa DNR may resolve cases by consent order or AG referral and never enter them. The warehouse can't tell "didn't act" from "didn't report." But Iowa does enter HPVs and formal actions at other plant types (skeptic-g5: 34% of Iowa repeat failers got one).
- **"Muscatine is a bigger plant, so it tests and fails more."** Partly. It has 2x the CO2 and more test days (147 vs 116). But its fail share is still 33% against 17%, and 49 fail days is 2.5x the next mill.
- **"HPV is a judgment call."** True. That judgment is the story: the same failures get the label in Indiana and not in Iowa.
- **Pollutant is unknown after 2016.** Muscatine's fail rows carry a blank pollutant code from 2017 on. You can't say which pollutant failed.
- **The TRI 2023 drop** may be a form change, not cleaner air.
- **The COVID grants are formula money.** Paid by lost gallons, not a reward for anything. Say "both at once," never "because."
- **The ECHO % minority figure** is EPA's radius estimate, not census tracts I checked.
- **OSHA:** the warehouse has inspections but no citations or penalties, so there's no safety story here. Muscatine has had 4 inspections since 2021 (none between 2013 and 2021).
- **Name joins** (OSHA, USAspending) all have a second field agreeing: city + street for OSHA, UEI + 1600 Oregon St for USAspending. The "Kent Corp" contract hits are other companies.

## Data traps found (for the trap file, not saved by me)

- **GPC Muscatine's NEI data lives on a second FRS registry ID** (110072214456, "formerly Kent Feeds"), not the ICIS-Air registry 110017404548. A registry-ID join from ICIS-Air to EIS returns nothing. Search FRS by address before calling NEI "missing."
- **ECHO `DATE_LAST_FORMAL_ACTION` doesn't say which agency or program.** Muscatine's 2013 date is an EPA water penalty, not a state air action. Trace it through NPDES/FE&C before quoting it as state enforcement.
- **"Kent Corporation" is at least 6 unrelated companies** in USAspending (OR, OH, IL, KS, AL, IA). Use UEI TAR2QK955KK4 for GPC.
- **NPDES QNCR `HLRNC`** mixes letter codes (S, E, N, V, R, U, W, D, X, blank). I didn't decode them beyond S = significant noncompliance. Don't count "noncompliant quarters" from it without the code list.
