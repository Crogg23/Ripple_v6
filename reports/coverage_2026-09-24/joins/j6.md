# Las Vegas Sands in Texas

j6, join pass, 2026-09-24. Python door, tag `joins-2026-09-24`. **25 statements**, all in `j6.sql`. [14] failed on a column name and was rerun as [15]. Both count.
Every person, company and committee named here is a data match. None is checked against primary records.

**The one-line story:** Las Vegas Sands spent $20.58M on Texas "media" through one lobbyist, Andy Abboud, May 2021 to June 2026. That's 71% of all Texas lobby media dollars in the same months, and about 3.7x the $5.5M it paid federal lobby firms over the same years. $15.19M of it (74%) landed in the January-May months of legislative-session years. The biggest burst: $9.37M spent January-April 2025.

**Correction to earlier passes:** the $9.37M is money **spent** January-April 2025. The reports carrying it were **filed** February-May. Each report covers the month before it's filed. Chart by `PERIOD_START_DT`, not by report name.

## Weak points a hostile editor would hit

- **"Media" is not defined in the table.** TEC's instructions decide whether it's ad buys, production, or pass-through to an agency. Earlier passes checked the category name, not the TEC rule.
- **Abboud is a Sands employee (senior VP per general knowledge, not checked).** So "lobbyist media spend" may just be Sands' own ad campaign filed under his name. It's still disclosed as lobby expenditure, but the framing "a lobbyist spent" could mislead.
- **Sands' Texas spending is widely covered** (Texas Tribune and others, 2023 and 2025). The five-year TEC total and the 71% share may or may not be new. Not checked.
- **The 3.7x federal comparison** puts ad spend next to lobby fees. They're different kinds of money. Fair as scale, not as like-for-like.
- **The FEC links are thin.** $2M into a $36.95M super PAC is 5.4%. The RAGA → Paxton and RSLC → Phelan links are second hops through pooled funds at about 1%. Don't draw an arrow.
- **The real money is invisible.** Texas Sands PAC reportedly gave millions to Texas legislators (press, not warehouse). No Texas campaign-finance table exists. That's where a hostile editor says "you didn't look where the money actually is."
- **Subject and docket joins are 0 for 62.** We can't tie any single month's spend to a bill from warehouse data.
- **MAGA Inc.'s two $5M 2026 lines** may be one buy. Excluded from any sum.

## New traps

- `POLITICS__FED_SENATE_LDA_LOBBYIST_POSITIONS` covers filing year **2011 only** (140,463 rows, one load). A person-level LDA miss there means nothing for any other year.
- `FINANCE__FED_FEC_INDEPENDENT_EXPENDITURES.EXP_DATE` is `DD-MON-YY` text. min() and max() sort alphabetically; parse before comparing.
- TEC subject and docket lines sit on about 5% and 0.2% of cover reports. A 0-line join there is the normal case, not a finding.
- TEC report names (LOBBYACTFEB) mark the filing month. Money was spent in the prior month (`PERIOD_START_DT`).
