# Two suppliers own Medicare's alginate-dressing code (F-032)

All names below are data matches. None has been checked against primary records.
Every query is in `j2.sql`. The raw results are in `j2/results.jsonl`, and the runner is `j2/q.py`.
22 SELECT statements were sent and 2 failed to compile. There were also 20 session settings, two per connection.

---

## The one-line story

**Two mail-order suppliers, one in a Delray Beach office suite and one at a Queens basement address, took $593.6M of the $649.2M Medicare paid for large alginate wound dressings (HCPCS A6197) in 2024. That's 91.4%. They billed 8-11x a normal supplier per patient. Of all 55,598 suppliers in the file, they are the only 2 billing the same five-product menu. Medicare later banned the Florida one (June 2026). Both are gone from Medicare's enrollment file. The New York one is on no exclusion list.**

## The chain

### 0. The base number (DME by Supplier, HCPCS A6197)
- **Checked:** money = services × average Medicare payment, summed per supplier. The file publishes no total, only an average.
- **What the file shows:**
  - A6197 overall: 174 suppliers, **$649,233,082 paid** and $828.7M allowed.
  - Sunshine Senior Solutions: $402,206,846. Almaz Med Supply: $191,425,335. Together **$593.6M, 91.4%**.
  - All four alginate codes (A6196-A6199): $703.6M, of which the two hold 84.4%. The other three codes they bill nothing on.
- **Peer group** is the other A6197 suppliers with a published patient count, 122 of them.

| | Paid per patient | Dressings per patient |
|---|---|---|
| Sunshine | $8,323 | 494 |
| Almaz | $6,322 | 359 |
| Peer median | $758 | 44 |
| Median of the next-10 biggest peers | $1,021 | 58 |

- Against the peer median that's **11.0x and 8.3x**. Against big peers it's 8.2x and 6.2x.
- 494 dressings is 1.35 a day, every day of the year, for every patient.
- **What year is the data:** the file has no year column. Carbon-dated with NPPES: the newest supplier NPI start date is 2024-10-30, and zero NPIs from 2025 appear. **So it's DY2024.** This confirms F-032's "2024".
- **The same playbook (a join inside the file):** only **2 of 55,598 suppliers** bill all five of these codes: catheters A4353, alginate A6197, glucose-monitor supplies A4239, and knee and wrist braces L1852 and L3916. Those 2 are Sunshine and Almaz. Next closest is Continuum LLC, PA, with 4 of 5 codes spread across 104 codes, $4.3M.
- **Their share of other codes:** 47.1% of catheters (A4353, $543M of $1.15B), 33% of glucose-monitor receivers (E2103), 32-34% of knee and wrist braces, and 96% of L0486 back braces.

### 1. NPPES, on NPI
- **Checked:** both NPIs, then any NPI that shares their authorized-official surname, their street, or their phone.
- **What matched:**
  - Sunshine: NPI 1811518392, first issued **2020-05-06**. Address 100 E Linton Blvd Ste 404B, Delray Beach FL. Official **Dana J. Pessoa, Owner**. Taxonomy 332B00000X (a DME supplier).
  - Almaz: NPI 1487343505, first issued **2023-05-04**. Address **4407 69th St Bsmt**, Woodside NY. Official **Zurab Tsotskhalashvili, President**.
  - So Almaz went from no NPI to $473M of Medicare payments inside its first full calendar year.
- **The sibling search, 66 rows:**
  - 100 E Linton Blvd is a multi-tenant office building: 56 other NPIs practice there (therapists, home-care agencies). **No other NPI uses suite 404B.**
  - Nothing else uses the Woodside basement address.
  - Neither official is on any other NPI.
  - The only other Pessoa rows are doctors in AL, CA and GA, plus one FL behavior technician. Namesakes.
- **A hit would mean** a network of shell NPIs. **The miss means** there are no sibling NPIs under the same names, address or phones. A network, if one exists, would sit under other names.

### 2. OIG LEIE (the ban list), on NPI
- **Sunshine:** listed on NPI, **excluded 2026-06-20**, type 1128Aa. Name, suite and city match on both sides.
- **The batch around it:** 24 DME businesses were excluded 1128Aa between June 19 and June 28, 2026, in FL, TX, KY, NY, PA, GA, AL, CA, MS, CT and IL. 8 of them appear in the supplier file and took $1.425B.
  - Sunshine is the only one of the 8 that billed alginate.
  - The other 7 are almost all catheters.
- **Almaz:** not on LEIE by NPI, by name, or by the official's surname.
- **Timing:** the billing is 2024 and the ban is 2026. **The money was paid before the ban.** Nothing here says they billed after it.
- **What a miss means for Almaz:** either there's no action, or the action isn't on LEIE yet. The newest LEIE row is from August 2026.

### 3. PECOS enrollment, on NPI (the sharpest join)
- **Checked:** both NPIs, and anything sharing their PECOS PAC ID. The PECOS file landed **2026-08-08**; its stamp is epoch-micros, decoded.
- **Result:** 0 rows for either NPI.
- **Is that just coverage?** No. The same test on everyone:
  - The rest of the file: **91.2% present** (50,644 of 55,548).
  - Top 50 by money: only 37 present. **13 missing, 26%.**
  - 7 of the 8 banned suppliers are among those 13.
  - Also missing: Almaz, **ND Medical Solutions** (PA; NPI first issued 2024-05-07; $88.0M, all catheters; not on LEIE), the second NPI of **Express Healthcare** (AL; $75.2M; **same authorized official, Kendall Miller, as the banned Express Healthcare NPI**; not on LEIE), and **Hawkeye Medical** (MD, the #3 alginate supplier at $34.1M, 18,425 patients; not on LEIE).
- **Alginate top 15:** the top 3 are absent (Sunshine, Almaz, Hawkeye). 10 of the next 12 are present. The two absent are Horizon Wound Care (TX, NPI 2023) and Asure Wound Solutions (FL).
- **A hit would mean:** a current enrollment record. **The miss means** they're no longer in Medicare's public enrollment file. The cause could be revocation, deactivation or voluntary exit, and **the file doesn't say which.** For Almaz this is the only federal sign in the warehouse that anything happened.

### 4. SAM, PPP, courts, ICIJ, DOJ: the misses
- **SAM:** 0 on NPI. The name "ALMAZ" hits 34 Russian OFAC entities (Almaz-Antey and others). Wrong country, wrong business. Rejected.
- **PPP, $150K and up:** 0 for either company. Almaz had no NPI until 2023, after PPP ended. Sunshine got its NPI in May 2020, but **the under-$150K PPP file isn't in the warehouse**, so a small loan can't be ruled out.
- **CourtListener:** 0 dockets name either company. "United States v. Pessoa" dockets exist, including S.D. Fla. 2016 and M.D. Fla. 2023, but only the surname matches. **Not claimed.**
- **FJC criminal:** can't be tested. The defendant name column is empty on every row.
- **DOJ press listing:** 0. It covers Dec 2025 to Jun 2026 and holds no health-fraud links at all, so it can't see the June 2025 or June 2026 takedown releases.

### 5. Doctors and Open Payments: blocked
- The DME-by-referrer file has referrer NPIs but no supplier identity. The supplier file has suppliers but no referrer. **They share no key.**
- So "which doctors ordered for them, and did manufacturers pay those doctors" can't be answered from public CMS files. It needs claims data.
- Not attempted rather than faked.

## Weak points a hostile editor would hit

1. **Money is computed, not published.** It's services × average payment. The average is rounded per row, so the totals are close but not exact. Allowed ($828.7M) and paid ($649.2M) differ, and every figure here is paid.
2. **"Normal supplier" is a small pool.** 122 peers with a published patient count. Many peers are tiny: the median one bills $34,845 on A6197. Against the 10 biggest peers the gap shrinks to 6-8x.
3. **"Patients" are per supplier per code.** One person can be counted by two suppliers. 494 dressings per patient assumes a patient stays with one supplier all year.
4. **What 1128Aa means is unverified.** The Sept 5 deep dive called it a conviction. My reading: it's the civil-money-penalty authority (section 1128A), which would make it administrative, not criminal. **Check OIG's code list before printing either.**
5. **Missing from PECOS is not proof of revocation.** It could be a voluntary exit or a data lag.
6. **The ban came after the billing.** No data here shows billing after 2026-06-20.
7. **Alginate dressings are legitimate products.** Mail-order wound-care suppliers exist. The case rests on volume per patient and the matching product menu, not on the product.
8. **Almaz is on no enforcement list in the warehouse.** Everything said about it is billing pattern plus disappearing from enrollment. **Naming it needs an outside source.**
9. **The data year is inferred** (carbon-dated from NPPES), not stated by the file.

## New traps found

- **`JUSTICE__FED_FJC_IDB_CRIMINAL.DEFENDANT_NAME` is blank on all 6,299,908 rows.** A name search returns 0 and looks like a clean miss. It isn't.
- **Missing from PECOS is a signal among big billers.** The top 50 DME billers are 26% absent against 8.8% for the rest, so treat absence as data, not a coverage gap. The PECOS landing `INGESTED_AT` is epoch micros (2026-08-08).
- **"ALMAZ" in SAM is 34 Russian OFAC entities.** Any name match on it is a namesake.
