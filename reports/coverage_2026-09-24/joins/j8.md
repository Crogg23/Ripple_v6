# Police, fire, veteran and cancer 527s: one vendor network, one donor list, two filing systems

Agent j8, 2026-09-24. Python door, read-only, tag `joins-2026-09-24`. **24 statements** (2 failed on a regex typo, counted). SQL: `j8.sql`. Raw output: `j8/q*.tsv`.
Every person, group and company named is a **data match, not verified against primary records.**

## The one-line story

**19 police, fire, veteran and cancer-named 527 groups spent $198.6M from 2014 to mid-2026 (IRS). About three-quarters went to fundraising, and $0.36M went to anything called a contribution. 17 of the 19 pay the same pool of 26 telemarketing and caging vendors, and those vendors get 91-100% of their 527 money from these groups. Their donors are also the donor list of 13 look-alike FEC PACs that raised $111.4M in 2018-2024 and sent 7% of it to politics. Eight robocall lawsuits name groups on both sides.**

One lump is named "WITHHELD", the other "FEMSF/APOA/NPSF/AVHF ALL DISBURSEMENTS FROM 7/1-12/31/2025", same amount. Payee names differ, so the dedup key missed them. ## Weak points a hostile editor would hit

- **"Fundraising" is a keyword match on purpose text.** Raw text agrees ("CAGING AND ESCROW", "TELE-FUNDRAISING"). But many rows say "PROGRAM-POLITICAL TELEMARKETING", which the groups call program work. The groups would say the calls are voter education. That's legal. Telemarketing-heavy 527s are not illegal on their face.
- **The total moves with the dedup rule:** $222.6M raw, $201.8M after dropping copies, $198.6M after the H2-2025 double lump. Say which one.
- **FEC donor match is zip + surname + first initial**, not a donor ID; FEC has none. Common names in one zip can merge two people. The 8-row sample was clean, and the 1-3% mainstream baseline controls for chance. Still, it's a probabilistic join.
- **The FEC side has no vendor data.** There's no FEC operating-expenditure (disbursement) table in the warehouse, so **I could not show the FEC twins pay the same vendors.** The FEC link is donors + treasurers + one shared address. PAC summary covers 2018-2024 only.
- **"To politics" on the FEC side** = contributions to other committees + independent expenditures from the PAC summary. IEs can themselves be paid to the same telemarketers (calls "for" a candidate). So 7% is a ceiling on real candidate support, not a floor.
- **Scam-PAC coverage exists** (CNN 2020, Daily Beast, Jacobin, The Lever, per deep-3). Several FEC twins may already be named there. What's new here is the IRS 527 side, the vendor exclusivity, and the donor-list overlap that ties the two filing systems together.
- **Residential Programs "exclusivity" covers 527s only.** It may have big charity or FEC clients this warehouse can't see.
- **None of the 19 EINs is in the exempt-org master file.** That's expected for 527s, not a finding.
- **Court outcomes are FJC codes read from memory of the codebook** (12 = voluntary dismissal, 2 = want of prosecution). I didn't open the codebook.
- **Donor names are private people.** They were used for matching only and must not be published.
