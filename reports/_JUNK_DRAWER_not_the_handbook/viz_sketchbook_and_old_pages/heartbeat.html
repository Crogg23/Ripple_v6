Each tile is one source table's row count per month (square-root scale, each tile normalized to its own busiest month, shown as the amber dot). Flat-lines, cliffs, and lone spikes are collection artifacts as often as world events — this wall is a data x-ray first, a story second. Click a tile for the full-size trace.
