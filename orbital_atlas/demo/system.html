Design system

One look in, one whole page out.

A look is a theme file for the map. This page shows what the system solves from it: six colour roles, three voices, and every component, all checked for contrast. Switch the look and everything follows.
Look
The rules
  1. Seven type sizes and seven gaps. Nothing else, and nothing under 11 pixels.
  2. Three voices: display for the finding, text for sentences, data for numbers and labels. All come from the look.
  3. Six colour roles, solved from the look and proven readable. Quiet text is allowed. Faint text is not.
  4. Chrome is neutral ink. Colour on the page always means data.
  5. Figures inside sentences are set heavier, so the eye can hop from number to number.
  6. Nothing moves.
