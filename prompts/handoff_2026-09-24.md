## The actor cross-reference

Everything is in `reports/actor_pilot_2026-09-24/`. Read its `README.md`.

- **The 25-company pilot:** `pilot.py` and `id_pass.py`. IDs reach only the money, labor, environment and science domains. Health, courts and politics connect by name only.
- **Seeds:** `seed_500.py` builds `seeds.csv`, 595 companies drawn from the top-80 lists: contracts, doctor payments, lobbying, PACs, TRI, CFPB complaints, OSHA injuries, H-1B visas. Its cache is `lists.json`.
- **Scanner:** `scan_500.py`. Distinct names are split into words and joined on the rarest anchor word, then the full pattern is confirmed.
  - Rule 1: a one-word company must be the whole party name plus legal endings, or sit beside "V" in a case name
  - Rule 2: a surname, per NPPES with 25 or more people, gets the full legal name
  - Rule 3: plain words like SOUTHERN, from a hand list, get the full legal name
- **Outputs:**
  - `hits_500.csv` is the strict run, graded 87.5% clean
  - `hits_500_clean.csv` has 16 junk seeds cut, 555 companies, expected 93-95% clean
- **Crossings:**
  - 76 companies sit in doctor payments, courts and politics
  - 278 sit in contracts, EPA, OSHA, courts and politics
  - Presence mostly measures size
- **Harm ranking:**
  - `rank_harm.py` pulls FY2023-24 contract dollars from R2
  - `rank_osha.py` measures injury rate against the same 6-digit NAICS, falling back to 4-digit, with the company excluded from its own benchmark. Output: `injury_rank.csv`.
  - Skeptic results: SpaceX 2.8x, confirmed. Ford 2.0x, confirmed. General Dynamics 1.2-1.4x and HII about 1.1x, both narrowed.
